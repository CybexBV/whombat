What changes, and why

  1. One page, no tabsServers, connections and what you pinned are on one screen. Today each sits behind its own tab, so you never see the whole estate at once.
  2. Servers line upOne row a server with the same columns, so a busy processor or a full disk stands out against the others. Six servers fit where two cards fit today.
  3. The day, not only the momentThe processor shows its last 24 hours as a small line (the readings are already there). A spike at 03:00 is visible without opening the server.
  4. What runs whereEach dot is a machine or app: filled runs, hollow is stopped, amber is unwell. A server that runs on another sits under it.
  5. Healthy is quietOnly what isn’t fine gets a pill, a colour and a reason in words. What needs you sorts to the top of its group; the broken thing still leads the page.
  6. Connections say somethingA small tile with one line of what it says now and when it was last used, instead of a large card that is mostly empty.
