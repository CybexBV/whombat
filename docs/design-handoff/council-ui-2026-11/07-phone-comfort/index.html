Room to read. Room to tap.

Names keep their width. The next line carries the status. A visible button opens the web’s message actions.

Sources, interactions & gaps ↗

Current cap, calmer rows

The largest iOS and Android settings are drawn at the effective 1.6× cap. Council and delegation names wrap; provider, status and time follow underneath. Approval answers already stack above 1.3× and keep their order.

One reachable action button

On the phone-width web, each message has one persistent 44×44 button. Enter or Space opens its sheet. Focus starts on the first action; Escape or Close returns it to the button.

A bigger cap has a cost

The variant draws iOS at 2.4× and Android at its 2.0× system maximum. At 2.4×, Feedback joins Settled in the header menu; Android at 2.0× still keeps Feedback visible. A wider side rail preserves full labels; it leaves far less room for the page. Desk-preview tiles keep their drawing size.
