Invoice PDF export

Drafted by Sketch from Priya’s request · edited by Mara, Pippa · 12 min ago

Goal

Customers can download any invoice as a PDF from the billing portal and from the invoice email. The PDF matches the current HTML invoice, in Dutch and English, and is ready before finance closes the month on 14 October.

Steps

{{ st.t }} {{ st.a.name }}{{ st.state }}

Rules to keep

Invoice numbers come from the invoice-number service. The PDF path never generates or reuses one.
Round VAT per line, half-up, before formatting.
PDFs are generated on request and cached for 24 hours, keyed by invoice number and locale Sketch is writing

Out of scope

Credit notes and bulk export. Both are planned for next quarter.
