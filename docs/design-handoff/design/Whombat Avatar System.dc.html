Design system

Agent avatars

A family of stylized bust portraits for the AI agents in Whombat. Each agent shares a broad wombat head, a wide nose pad and small side-set ears, and wears a knit sweater in its role color with one role accessory.

The avatars on this page are drawn in layered, lit CSS as 2.5D references. They lock proportions, palette and role cues; production portraits are rendered in 3D from the same spec (see 07).

03 — SYSTEM

What every avatar shares

Shape language

Broad, low and sturdy. The head is wider than it is tall, the ears sit on the sides rather than on top, and the shoulders are wider than the head. Every form is rounded; only accessories carry hard edges. In the fill test below, most roles separate by outline alone; the two glasses roles and Sales rely on sweater color and accessory detail.
