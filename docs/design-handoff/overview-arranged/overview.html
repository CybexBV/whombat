Overview

What changes, and why

  1. A summary firstA greeting, one sentence from Dusty and four figures. When something is broken it leads; when all is fine the page says so in green, with what was checked. While arranging, each figure has a gear: pick any figure from any connection.
  2. TodayOne line through the day across every server and connection: backups, updates, payments, meetings, the failure. Planned things show hollow after “Now”.
  3. Connections show their numbersEach tile leads with one figure and a small chart, with a touch of the service’s own colour.
  4. Arrange, then a gear on every tileThe gear beside the section links starts arranging. Only then does every tile, figure and pinned chart get its gear: what it shows, the time span of its chart, its size (small, wide, large), its place, or Remove. Drag a tile onto another to move it. Removed tiles wait under “Add to Overview”. Done (or Esc) ends it; outside arranging a tile is just a link to its page.
  5. One Overview per WhombatEveryone sees the same page. People who can add connections can change it; the others only look. Removing a tile never hides a problem: it still shows at the top.
  6. It looks liveThe dot pulses and the internet speed and power update in place, wherever they are shown. Nothing moves for people who asked for less motion.
  7. Charts with bodyShaded areas, a dot at the latest reading, faint grid lines. Pinned charts have the same gear: half or full width, their place, Remove.
  8. Servers stay as acceptedThe 12 November list is unchanged apart from the shaded processor line. Dark mode gets raised cards and soft light at the top of each.

Try it: press Arrange, then a gear: change what Home Assistant shows, make Plex large, drag Mail to the front, remove Hetzner and add it back.
