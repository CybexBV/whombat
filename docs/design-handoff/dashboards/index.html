1 · HeaderName, one status, a few chips, the facts line, and at most two actions (Ask Whombat plus one kind action: Console, Restart, Open in Hetzner).pve-02 · Proxmox VE 9.0 · Ryzen 7 5700G · 64 GB · up 12 d · 10.100.0.12
2 · Tabs + time rangeSummary first, then the kind’s own tabs, then Activity and Settings. One range picker (1 h to 90 d) drives every chart on the page.
3 · BannerOnly when something is wrong right now. Shows the worst alert, with one link.
4 · Stat tiles (3–6)The number, what it is out of, and a 24 h sparkline or a meter. Amber or red only near a limit.CPU · Memory · Disk · Temperature · Guests | Version · Integrations · Database · Log errors
5 · Chart cards (1–3 per row, 1–2 rows)Current value, average and peak, the chart, and 2–4 small facts under it.
Data from the agentServers and guests: CPU, memory, load, network, disk I/O, temperatures, space per volume.
Data from the connectionHetzner metrics API, UniFi device stats, Home Assistant log and database, Whombat’s own 5-minute samples.
6 · Main sections (stacked)Kind sections the template draws the same way: table, bar list, list, facts.Guests · Storage · Array disks · Integrations not loading · Servers · Access points
7 · Side column (stacked)“Worth a look” shows the top five and folds the rest. Small cards follow: backups, cluster, updates.No empty space: each column stacks its own cards
