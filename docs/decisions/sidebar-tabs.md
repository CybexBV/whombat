# Servers and Connections as tabs, Overview, Settings → Connections (SBT)

John, 2026-11-02 (main checkout), with a screenshot of the web sidebar's SERVERS and SERVICES: seeing the servers by
default matters, seeing Services doesn't, but it should be easy to reach, so a tabbed list with Servers on by default;
rename Services to Connections; and a Connections tab back in Settings. He asked for a mockup and my own view. My view
(mockup B): tabs yes, but the sidebar's “Connections” row (the page with Servers · Services · Pinned) would then share
its word with a tab that means only what isn't a server, so that page becomes **Overview**; a problem must not hide
behind a tab; two connections with the same name need telling apart; and Settings → Connections is about sign-ins, with
what needs you first. His answer: “Implement your suggestion fully.”

Changes `connections-page.md` CN-01 and CN-02, `sidebar.md` SB-10 and SB-11, and `settings-groups.md` STG-01, STG-03,
STG-05 and STG-06. Code: `apps/web/src/app/shell/` (`assistantSidebarModel.ts` `sidebarLists`, `AssistantSidebar.tsx`,
`useSidebarList.ts`), `packages/client/src/chat/rooms.ts` (`CHAT_WORDS.dashboard`), `packages/client/src/home/tabs.ts`,
`packages/client/src/home/things.ts` (`nameAndNote`), `packages/client/src/connections/settingsList.ts`,
`packages/client/src/settingsGroups.ts`, web `features/settings/` and `features/connectors/ConnectionsOverview.tsx`,
phone `apps/mobile/src/data/shell.ts`, `data/connections.ts`, `settings/list.ts`, `screens/ConnectionsScreen.tsx`. No
contract, schema or migration changes (agent-facing descriptions say “Overview page”).

| SBT-03 | **Superseded by servers-connections-places.md SCP-01, SCP-02** (Servers and Connections are places in the menu, each a list beside a page). **The web sidebar shows SERVERS and CONNECTIONS as two tabs, Servers first.** The browser keeps the open one (`whombat.sidebar.list`). With only one list (no hosts service and no server, or nothing connected) its plain heading shows, no tabs. Folding (SB-11) is gone. | Servers matter by default; connections are one click away without leaving the page. |
| SBT-04 | **Superseded by servers-connections-places.md SCP-04** (the menu item carries the worst mark of what it holds). **A problem never hides behind a tab.** The tab that isn't open has a dot in the worst tone of what it holds, and its rows that aren't fine (needs a look, a problem, offline) and the one open now show under the open list, below a dashed line (“Also in Connections”). A device being learned isn't carried. | SB-11's rule, kept for tabs. |
