# Settings in four groups (STG)

The “effortless” work of 2026-10-30 for John: make Whombat easy and effortless for people who aren't developers, with
the assistant doing the work. The review (`flows.md` Journey 6 — 6.1, 6.2 — and 3.6, 5.2, 5.3; `visual.md` #6, #7, #10,
#15, #16, #19, #20, #21, #25, #28, #31, #32, #37, #38; `copy.md` §5) counted about 65 labelled sections in eight Settings
tabs on the web and about 40 on the phone, Spend three times, the AI sign-in twice, Notes beside Memory, two pages called
Connections, five vocabularies for what the assistant may do (and one switch whose label contradicted its choice), a
Vault that asks for cryptography by hand and developer pages a home user meets through search. Settings becomes four
groups — **You · {assistant} · This Whombat · Advanced** — with expert things in Advanced, folded; every setting stays
reachable and every old address lands where its content went.

Code: the shared model `packages/client/src/settingsGroups.ts` (groups, parts, Advanced's rows, where an old part went)
and `packages/client/src/agentPermissions.ts` (the three words); web `apps/web/src/features/settings/` (`SettingsScreen`,
`YouTab`, `AdvancedTab`, `ThisWhombatTab`, `ConnectionsPages`, `DangerFold`, `useDeveloperTools`),
`apps/web/src/features/agent/settings/` (`AgentTab`, `PermissionsTab`), `apps/web/src/app/routing/search.ts`
(`settingsRedirect`); phone `apps/mobile/src/settings/` (`groups.ts`, `list.ts`, `AdvancedScreens.tsx`),
`apps/mobile/src/screens/agent/` (`MayPart`, `AiPart`, `MemoryPart`), `apps/mobile/src/data/agentMay.ts`; the Vault in
`apps/web/src/features/vault/` and core `packages/core/src/vault/copy.ts`. No contract, schema or migration changes.
