Claude subscription execution below is superseded by [Claude subscription DirectSDK](claude-directsdk.md)
(CDS-01…CDS-03, John’s instruction on 2026-10-24). The agent's computer and what several people shared are superseded
by [one person per Whombat](one-person.md) (OP-01…OP-19, 2026-10-25). Other Hermes decisions remain in force. Each row

John, 2026-10-23 (main checkout): stop building our own engine and focus on the harness; Hermes Agent
(NousResearch/hermes-agent, MIT) becomes the engine; keep the appliance and dev/app.whombat.com; add an open-source
community appliance reached over Tailscale next to the commercial one. Whombat is not released and nobody uses it, so
nothing is kept for compatibility. Research with sources: `~/.cache/wb-tmp/research/2026-10-23-engine/engines.md`
(Hermes `afe3cbd8`, OpenClaw `b814a1ef`, both 2026-10-23).

His answers to the first list: keep the Claude Code and Codex CLIs and today's sign-in in onboarding, and let Hermes use
them; one agent per appliance (no multiple assistants, no hiring; one-agent.md); subscriptions are mandatory, and running
the CLIs on a person's own sign-in is permitted (provider-subscriptions.md SUB-01, settled).

Order of work: one-agent.md first (removes multi-agent), then a trial on the DEV box (HE-28), then the engine, computers
and gateway, then memory, skills, browser and Vault, then the product shape and the community edition.
