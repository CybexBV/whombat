# The status line and Done today in Chat

The “effortless” work of 2026-10-30 for John: the first screen answers “Is everything OK, and do you need me?”. The
review (flows.md 1.5, 2.8, 5.4 and its first direction rule; visual.md #2, #15, #23) found the status sentence said
“Everything is running. 6 things need you” right above “The disk array on blackbox has failed”, that it lived only on
Connections while the app opens on Chat, that nothing in the apps showed what the assistant did by itself, and that
Files could only be reached from a message. Code: `packages/client/src/home/greeting.ts` (`homeStatus`),
`packages/client/src/home/statusLine.ts`, `apps/server/src/home/done.ts` (`doneToday`), web
`apps/web/src/features/chats/ChatStatus.tsx`, phone `apps/mobile/src/screens/ChatStatusCard.tsx` and
`apps/mobile/src/data/chatStatus.ts`.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| STL-01 | **The status sentence is true to the worst state**, in this order: a serious problem named — the first Needs you item of severity `bad` that isn't an approval or a question and whose research didn't end `fixed`, in the assistant's own title once it found out (`found`, `fixing`), with “I’m looking into it.” / “I’m fixing it.” and “N other things need you.”; else what is offline (“pve-01 is offline.”, “2 things are offline.”) or broken (“Something’s wrong with blackbox.”); else “**N things need you**, and I’m adding …”; else, with open problems nobody has to decide, “Nothing needs you. I’m keeping an eye on N things.”; else “All good.” with what it fixed (“I fixed 1 thing overnight.” when every fix of the day was before 07:00, else “today”) and what it is doing, or “Nothing needs you.”. A warm machine or a heads-up never makes it say something is wrong, and nothing open ever reads “All good”. Replaces AP-1265's “Everything is running.” first clause. | The one reassuring sentence was wrong exactly when it mattered: it only knew “offline”. The person reads one line; it must lead with what is worst. |
| STL-02 | **The sentence has a tone** (`ok`, `warn`, `bad`) for its dot and for its strong part's colour: the named problem in the problem colour, “N things need you” in amber as before. Connections' title (web and phone) uses the same tone. | Red text for a failed array, amber for a decision: the colour says how urgent before the words do. |
| STL-03 | **One sentence, shared**: `homeStatus` (web, phone, Connections and Chat); `homeStatusLine` stays as its parts. | Two screens saying different things about the same state is the review's first complaint. |
| STL-04 | *Superseded by STL-10.* **Chat's head carries the sentence on one line under it**, with its dot and “See them” (“See it” for one) to the one Needs you list when the tone isn't `ok`; all good, no link. Only Chat, not Feedback. Web: under the head, aligned with it, a hairline under it. Phone: a 44dp button under the header to the Needs you tab. | The app opens on Chat (CHT-22); the answer belongs on the first screen, and a line costs Chat almost nothing. |
| STL-05 | **Where it leads is one constant, `STATUS_LINKS`** (`packages/client/src/home/statusLine.ts`): `/needs-you` on the web, the `NeedsYou` tab on the phone, `/done`, `/files`. The phone opens `NeedsYou` once the bar has that tab and Connections (`INBOX_TAB`) until then (`needsYouTab`). | The needs-you stream builds the list at the same time; the lead repoints one place if its address differs. |
| STL-06 | **`home.overview` carries `doneToday`** (optional, contracts additive): the viewer's `done_tasks` since the start of their day in the burrow's time zone, newest first, at most 20, each `{id, text, at, href?, fixed?, undo?}`. `href` is the thread in their Chat or Feedback (or the task chat); `fixed` when a look's research in the same thread ended `fixed` (the fix reports there, LR-S16); `undo` the change made in that thread that can still be undone (available, not asked for already) when there is exactly one. With several, no Undo on the row. | The assistant's words are what it did (done-today.md); its changes are how to take it back. A task that made several changes can't have one Undo without guessing which. Read with the overview, so `report_done`'s `home.changed {working}` already refreshes it. |
| STL-07 | *Superseded by STL-10.* **Done today in Chat**: under the sentence, the newest three (`DONE_TODAY_SHOWN`), each the assistant's words opening its thread, the time, Undo when the row has one; “See everything” to Everything I did (`/done`). Nothing done: no Done today at all. Web: the label and See everything in a narrow column beside the rows (“Done today · 4” when more), so it costs three short lines. Phone: folded under “Done today · 4” (`MoreFold`), opening to the rows and See everything. | The review: what the assistant does by itself must be visible, or it can't be trusted to do more. Three rows keep Chat's messages near the top; on a phone even that is too much until asked for. |
| STL-08 | **Files in Chat's head while there is a file to see** (`useShowsFiles`; web beside Settled, to `/files`; phone in the header, to Files). | Files had no way in but a message chip or a typed address (flows.md 5.4); the head is where Chat's other places already are. |
| STL-09 | **The fixture world's states**: `VITE_FIXTURE_STATUS=good` (or `whombat:fixture-status`) makes everything fine and nothing open; `VITE_FIXTURE_DONE=0` (or `whombat:fixture-done`) leaves Done today out; by default blackbox's array has failed and Neo finished four things today, one a fix in the night and one with Undo (`apps/web/src/app/statusFixtures.ts`). The phone's demo world has two. | Every state of the sentence and of Done today can be looked at. |
| STL-10 | **No status line in Chat** (John, 2026-11-01): Chat's head has no status sentence, no “See them” and no Done today, on web and phone; what needs the person is the Needs you page (the phone's Needs you tab) alone. The sentence stays Connections' title; `home.overview`'s `doneToday` stays for what it says was fixed. `chatStatus` and `statusLine.ts` are gone; Files' word is `CHAT_WORDS.files`. Replaces STL-04 and STL-07. | John: “we only use the page Needs you”. A second, expandable summary above the messages repeated the page and pushed Chat down. |
