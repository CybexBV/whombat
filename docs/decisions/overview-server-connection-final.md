# Overview, a server's page and a connection's page: the final design (OSC)

From the accepted final design (`docs/design-handoff/council-ui-2026-11/16-final/overview/`, `server/`, `connection/`
and their NOTES.md; John accepted it, 2026-11-06). The same on the web (desktop and phone width) and the phone app
(iOS, Android). Code: `packages/client` (`hosts/tiles.ts`, `hosts/dashboard.ts`, `home/greeting.ts`
`overviewHeadline`, `home/time.ts` `readingTime`/`readState`, `home/tabs.ts` `OVERVIEW_WORDS`, `home/widgets.ts`),
`apps/web/src/features/home/` (`HomeScreen`, `ReadState`, `WidgetCard`),
`apps/web/src/features/things/` (`ServerPage`, `dashboard/Panels`, `dashboard/NoticeStrip`, `dashboard/slots.ts`,
`page/ThingPageScreen`), `apps/mobile/src/screens/` (`HomeScreen`, `home/`, `ServerScreen`, `ThingPageScreen`,
`thing/Panels`) and `apps/mobile/src/data/` (`homeServers.ts`, `thingPage.ts`). No contract, server or migration
change.

| Id | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OSC-01 | **Overview's first line is the broken thing** when something serious is open (`overviewHeadline`): a card with its title as a heading in `--bad` (a link to its Inbox row), its consequence under it, and one **Look into it** (the Inbox's own handle action). Under it **“N other things need you ›”** opens the Inbox. While the assistant is already looking into it or fixing it, its words replace the button. With nothing serious open, the status sentence stays as before. Supersedes ui-refresh-status.md UIR-14 (the red sentence and its “Ask {assistant} to handle it”). | The person sees what is broken and what to do about it before anything else; one button, not a sentence and a second action. |
| OSC-02 | **A server's tiles say words with their figures**, the same on its Overview card and its page (`serverTiles`, the server Summary's figures): Processor (Quiet, Busy, Very busy, Flat out) · Memory (Plenty free, Getting full, Nearly full, Full) · Storage (Plenty free, Filling up, Nearly full, Full) with “10 % in use · 24 h avg 7 %”, “42 GB free · 76 of 118 GB in use (64 %)”, “1.6 of 3.6 TB · 44 % in use” under them. **Temperature is always its figure** (“71 °C”, its limit or its sensor under it), never “Normal” or “Warm”. A server that **isn't working** shows its bare figures (“4 %”, “31 %”), never a reassuring word beside “Disk array failed”. The Summary's “Space” is “Storage”, as on the card. Supersedes connections-page.md AP-2568 in part (labels and numbers) and dashboards.md's temperature word. | One set of words everywhere; a word that contradicts the pill, or calls a failed array's space “plenty free”, misleads. |
| OSC-03 | **A tile is amber at most.** Red is only for what isn't working: the pill (“Disk array failed”, via `LevelPill`) and the broken thing's heading. A full disk, a hot sensor or broken parity colour their tile amber. | John: red only for Not working. |
| OSC-04 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-52** (Overview draws 20 blocks on the phone too; the phone's server page keeps 10). **Today's block bars stay**: 20 segments on the web, 10 on the phone, under all four tiles on the card and, on the server's page, under the four figures instead of the sparkline (`Blocks`; the history is one tap away behind the figure and **See reading history**, which opens the first figure's charts). The bars are decorative copies of the numbers, hidden from screen readers. | John's wish; the page and its card look the same. |
| OSC-05 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-08, OVP-20** (on Overview a reading says its time only when it is older than five minutes; a fresh one's is the row's tooltip; the server's page keeps it). **Each reading says its own time**: “Read 12 min ago” under a server's name on its card (`lastSeen`) and on its page (`serverReportAt`: the page's moment less the agent's last report), or **“Reading time unknown”** without one. Never the fleet's or the fetch's time. The exact moment (“Read at 13:53 on 23 Sept”) is the tooltip and what a screen reader hears. | A reading's age is its own report's; a shared or fetch time would make an old reading look fresh. |
| OSC-06 | **A server's page says the same as its card**: the back link says **Overview**, the pill is the card's condition word (“Disk array failed”, “Running warm”; a count “2 to look at” only when the server gave no word), and the pill and the reading time lead the fact line under the name. The verdict sentence, the logo, every tab (Machines, Storage, Sensors, Backups, System; Unraid's and the others' own) and Technical details stay. A connection's page's back link says Overview too. | One word for one thing across Overview, the Inbox and the page. |
| OSC-07 | **A pinned widget's last point says its own time**: “5 min ago · 14:00” (`lastPointLabel`), never “Now”; an hour label within a sixth of the span of it is left out so they don't run into each other. Supersedes one-whombat-shell.md AP-1268 in part (its “Now”). | A point read five minutes ago isn't now, and after a failed refresh it gets older. |
| OSC-08 | **Read states are kept apart** (`readState`): loading draws page-shaped placeholders (cards in the page's grid, “Loading Overview…”); a first read that fails says **“Couldn’t read Overview.” / “There’s no saved copy to show on this device yet.”** and shows no figures; a failed refresh keeps every value on screen under **“Couldn’t refresh Overview.” / “Showing saved readings. Each server keeps its own reading time.”** (a pinned widget or a server's page: **“Couldn’t refresh; showing readings from 14:00 on 23 Sept.”**; a connection's page: its saved page, whose controls may have changed). Each note has **Try again**, which only reads again, and what the app said under More detail. A refresh that is going says nothing alarming. | “Refreshing” must never read as a failure, and a failure must never throw away what was saved. |
| OSC-09 | **No Ask button on a read failure's note** (the mockups show “Ask {assistant}”): Overview has no Ask pane to put the question in, and a failed read isn't something the assistant can fix from here. Try again and More detail are enough. | Avoids a button that would only start an empty chat. |
| OSC-10 | **A connection's Worth a look puts the current problems first**, with their buttons (five, the rest behind “Show N more”); **every heads-up folds behind its own “Show N heads-ups”** and isn't counted with the problems; quiet lines last. Controls follow Worth a look; their confirmations (Unlock the front door?) are unchanged. On the phone the same fold applies to every Worth a look card. Supersedes ui-refresh-chat.md UIR-70 in part (heads-ups under SOON still in sight). | Routine news (an update, a forecast) doesn't bury what needs the person, and doesn't inflate the count. |
| OSC-11 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-02, OVP-04, OVP-05, OVP-07, OVP-11** (on the web a server is a row of one list, a card only on a narrow page; the processor shows its last 24 hours, not its name; what runs is dots with its count; the pill shows only when it isn't fine; no “Open server readings ›”; the OS and uptime line stays). **A server's card keeps what it showed**: the OS and uptime line, the processor line and “13 of 21 machines running” stay beside the design's new lines; the web adds “Open server readings ›” at the foot (the card is the link; the words only say so). The name is in the page's face (sans 600 19 px), not mono. On the phone each server is a card of its own, its pill beside the name (under it when the name is long), tiles two by two. | Nothing the app has today disappears; the mockup's look wins where both fit. |
| OSC-12 | **Copy** is `OVERVIEW_WORDS` in `@whombat/client` (`home/tabs.ts`), the phone's own few words in `HOME_SERVERS_COPY`; the assistant's name always from the name helper. | One copy file for these screens; no typed names. |
