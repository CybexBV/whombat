# Overview on one page (OVP)

John asked for a redesign of Overview (2026-11-12): the page should give an overview of the servers and connections,
useful and beautiful. He accepted the mockup `docs/design-handoff/overview-one-page/overview.html` (switch State,
Theme and Width at its top; the mock wins on look, copy and layout) and said “build this”. The same on the web
(desktop and phone width) and in the phone app (iOS, Android). No contract, server or migration change: every figure
is already in `HostServer` and `ThingSummary`.

Code: `packages/client` (`hosts/overview.ts` the server rows, their order, the Servers line and the 24 h line;
`home/overview.ts` the sections, jump links and connection tiles; `home/tabs.ts` `OVERVIEW_WORDS`; `hosts/tiles.ts`
`serverFigures`), `apps/web/src/features/home/`, `apps/mobile/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` and `home/`.

| Id | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OVP-01 | **Superseded in part by overview-arranged.md OVA-07** (the order: the summary card, Today, Connections, Servers, Pinned). **One page, no tabs.** Under the broken thing (overview-server-connection-final.md OSC-01, unchanged) come **Servers**, **Connections** and **Pinned**, each a section with its heading, its count and one summary line. The tabs' places are **jump links** at the top right (on the phone under the title): the name, the count and the dot when something in it isn't fine; a tap scrolls to the section. An old `?tab=` address scrolls to that section. A section with nothing in it isn't drawn (Pinned only when something is pinned). Supersedes connections-page.md CN-11 and sidebar-tabs.md's tab row on Overview. | Behind tabs the person never saw everything at once; the count and the dot were the tab row's useful part. |
| OVP-02 | **Servers are one list with the same columns**: Server · Processor · 24 h · Memory · Storage · Temperature · Running. One row a server; the row opens the server, a figure opens its charts (as its tile did). Supersedes OSC-11's card on the web and connections-page.md CN-12. | Figures under each other compare at a glance, and six servers fit where two cards did. |
| OVP-03 | **The figure first, its word beside it** (“10 % Quiet · avg 7 %”, “64 % Getting full” with “76 of 118 GB” under it), the block bar under memory, storage and temperature. OSC-02's words, OSC-03's amber-at-most and the bare figures of a server that isn't working stay. A server without a sensor says “–” and “No sensor”. | The columns need the number in one place; the words still say what it means. |
| OVP-04 | **The processor shows its last 24 hours** as a small line with a wash under it (`HostServer.cpu24h`), in place of a block bar. Fewer than two readings: no line. Decorative: the figure and “avg” say it in words. | The readings were already sent; a spike in the night shows without opening the server. |
| OVP-05 | **Running is dots**: one for each machine or app, filled when it runs, hollow when stopped, amber when unwell (`HostGuestCount.running`, `total`, `unhealthy`), unwell first. Beside the count “19 of 22” stands **machines** or **apps** (a server that only runs Docker), or “2 unwell” when some are. At most 60 dots, then “+N”. Nothing on it: “Nothing running”. Decorative: the count is in words. | How much runs and how much is switched off is seen at once. The fleet sends counts, not names, so a dot is a number, not a machine. |
| OVP-06 | **A server that runs on another sits under it** (`HostServer.parent`), set in with an elbow line, its line saying “on pve-02”. Top-level servers sort worst first, then by name; a child follows its parent, worst first among its siblings. A child whose parent isn't in the list is a top-level row. | What runs where without a diagram. |
| OVP-07 | **Healthy is quiet.** A server that runs fine has no pill (a screen reader hears “running fine”); only what isn't fine has its pill (`LevelPill`, OSC-06's word). The server that isn't working has a tinted row with a `--bad` edge. The server this Whombat runs on has the tag **This Whombat**. | Six “Running” pills are noise; the eye should land on the one that isn't. |
| OVP-08 | **A reading says its time only when it is old**: older than five minutes it says “Read 12 min ago” under the name; without a time, “Reading time unknown”. A fresh reading's time is the row's tooltip and in its accessible name. Supersedes OSC-05 in part (the time on every card); a failed refresh's note (OSC-08) is unchanged. | “Read 2 min ago” six times says nothing; a stale reading is the one worth saying. |
| OVP-09 | **Superseded in part by overview-arranged.md OVA-05, OVA-06** (a tile's look, its sizes and the person's order; its line stays until its view is read). **A connection is a small tile**: its logo, its name, one line, its mark at the top right (the status level's shape; connected is the plain dot), and a foot. The **line** is why it isn't fine (the reason, amber), or that it is being learned, or what it says now (its first two figures in words, “38 devices · 1 new”), else what it is, else “Connected”. The **foot** is what it is on the left when the line didn't say it, and when it was last used on the right. Nothing is made up: a connection without figures has a short tile. Worst first, as before. | A large card that says only “Connected” wastes the page; one line is what a person reads. |
| OVP-10 | **Add sits in its section's heading**: “Add a server” beside Servers (with “Everything running”), “Add a connection” beside Connections. The button at the top right goes. The empty page (nothing connected) is unchanged. | The action is next to what it adds to. |
| OVP-11 | **Narrow pages**: when the page's own column is under 1060 px the servers are cards, two across, the figures two by two with their labels on each; under 620 px one across, connections one under the other. The phone app is that last form: sections in place of its switch, each server a card (as it was) with the 24 h line, the dots and the quiet pill, each connection a compact row. | The page answers to the room it has, not to the window; the phone keeps what worked. |
| OVP-12 | **Pinned is the last section**, its widgets as they were (OSC-07). Read states (OSC-08, OSC-09) are unchanged; the loading placeholders have the new page's shape. | Out of scope; nothing lost. |
| OVP-13 | **Copy** is `OVERVIEW_WORDS` in `@whombat/client`; what a row and a tile say is made in `home/overview.ts`, pure and unit-tested, for the web and the phone alike. | One place for the words and the rules. |
| OVP-20 | **Old means older than five minutes** (`STALE_READING_MS`). An offline server says “Last seen 2 d ago” and no reading time; a server waiting for its first report says neither. | Five minutes is several missed reports; an offline server's last-seen already says how old its figures are. |
| OVP-21 | **A child's line names its parent where its uptime was**: “Ubuntu 24.04 · on pve-02” (the mock). Its uptime stays in its accessible name and on its page. | The column is narrow; what runs where is the point of setting it in. |
| OVP-22 | **The Servers line counts a server that needs a look as running**; offline and waiting ones are said apart (“1 offline”, “1 waiting”); the noun is machines, apps (Docker only) or “machines and apps” by what the servers run. | It reads as the mock's “5 running · 1 not working”; a warm server still runs. |
| OVP-23 | **Running links to the server's machines tab** (`?tab=guests`); “Everything running” in the heading to every machine and app (`?view=guests`). Past 60 dots they are drawn unwell, running, stopped in that order and the rest is “+N”. | The count leads to the list it counts; the amber dots are never the ones cut. |
| OVP-24 | **A tile's line has no full stop**: a reason reads as sent with its first letter up; without one, the card's fallback words (“Whombat can’t reach it right now”). Not working is red, needs a look amber, offline and learning grey. Learning says “<assistant> is still learning it”. | A tile's line is a label, not a sentence; offline connections were already grey. |
| OVP-25 | **Jump links count what the section shows**: with the hosts fleet, Servers counts its servers and its dot is red when one isn't working or is offline, amber when one needs a look. An old `?tab=` (`servers`, `services`, `connections`, `pinned`) scrolls to its section, anything else opens at the top. | The count beside the link must match the rows under the heading. |
| OVP-30 | **Connections is drawn empty for whoever may add one**: its heading with “Add a connection” and “Nothing connected yet.”; for anyone else an empty Connections isn't drawn. The empty page (nothing at all) is unchanged. | Without it a person with servers but no connection loses the way to add one. |
| OVP-31 | **Jump links only when there are two sections or more.** | One link to the only section is noise. |
| OVP-32 | **The servers' list says its own states in place**: a server waiting to be added is the list's last row (on a narrow page a dashed card) with Review; “No servers yet. Add a server” is a row of the card; “Servers aren’t reachable right now.” stands where the card would; the fleet's own failed refresh note sits above the card, only when Overview's note isn't shown. | Each state stays where the person looks for servers. |
| OVP-33 | **One failed-refresh note above the sections** (“Couldn’t refresh Overview.”, each server keeps its own reading time); Pinned's own “readings from …” note went with its tab. | One page, one note. |
| OVP-34 | **“Add a server” in the Servers heading is for whoever may add a connection** (`canAdd`, a person); Review and “No servers yet. Add a server” stay for everyone, as before. | The heading's action follows the same rule as Add a connection. |
| OVP-35 | **A tile's edge follows its line**: amber when it needs a look, red when it isn't working, none when it is offline (grey line, OVP-24). | An offline connection is grey everywhere else on the page. |
| OVP-36 | **The web draws the shared marks**: the jump link's dot is `StatusMark` at the mock's 7 px (not working keeps its ring), the server pill is `LevelPill` in the mock's sans face. | One status scale by shape across the app. |
| OVP-37 | **An old `?tab=` lands without motion and moves focus to its section; the address is left as it came.** A jump link scrolls smoothly unless the person asked for less motion. | Arrival shouldn't animate; a link the person pressed may. |
| OVP-50 | **On the phone a server's card is one button** that opens the server; its figures aren't tapped apart. A screen reader hears the card as `row.label`. | The phone's server page opens on its Summary, where the charts and machines are; a figure would open the same page, and one large target suits a finger. |
| OVP-51 | **The phone leaves out “Everything running”** in the Servers heading; “Add a server” stays. | The phone has no list of every machine and app; the link would open Overview again. |
| OVP-52 | **The phone's block bars are the mock's**: 20 blocks 2 apart, 7 tall, lit in `chart1`, amber past the limit, unlit `line2` (the earlier neutral ten-block meter goes). | The mock wins on look, and the web draws the same bar. |
| OVP-53 | **The phone keeps its own pill and title**: `LevelPill` (mono 11.5/500) with the mark by the pill's tone — red ring and dot, amber dot, a grey hollow ring offline or waiting — and the 32/700 large title at 22 like the other tabs; the jump chips sit at the page's 16 under the title and the status sentence. | One pill and one title size across the app; an offline server isn't red on Overview (OVP-24). |
| OVP-54 | **A live update keeps the servers' order in groups**: a server and those that run on it move together; a pull to refresh sorts worst first again (phone-refresh-status.md). | A child must never leave its parent, and nothing moves under the finger. |
| OVP-55 | **Old addresses land on their section on the phone**: `/connections?tab=…`, `/home?tab=…` and `/things/servers` open `whombat://connections?tab=<section>`; Overview scrolls there once the section is drawn and forgets the request. | OVP-01's `?tab=` on the phone too; coming back to the tab later opens where the person left it. |
| OVP-56 | **On the phone what isn't fine has a 4dp edge** (the mock's hairline and 3px inset as one border): a server not working in `bad` on a 6 % `bad` tint, a connection in `bad` or `warn` by its line's tone. An offline server's figures are dimmed and its clock line says when it was last seen. A connected tile's mark is an 8dp `ok` dot. | React Native has no inset shadow; the border draws the same edge. |
| OVP-57 | **Running's word stays grey** when some are unwell (“2 unwell”); the amber dots carry it, as in the mock. | The mock leaves the count and word plain; amber twice is noise. |
| OVP-58 | **The phone shows a server waiting to be added as the Servers section's last card**: a dashed `line2` card with the server glyph, “A server is waiting to be added” (or “N servers are…”), the web's line, and **Review**, which opens Add a server with its waiting list; with no servers yet it stands alone. | OVP-32's narrow form; seen on the iPhone simulator, the phone had left it out, so a waiting server only showed in the Inbox. |
