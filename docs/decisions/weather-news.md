# Weather and local news (WN)

John, 2026-11-26: “I realized that we need to improve more capabilities in Whombat by default, for example weather
information which will be useful for Dusty in both dashboard view (morning page for example), but also for Dusty's "Look
ahead" feature. Perhaps we should also add local news (country / state)? I realize that this will become a bigger task,
but I am sure it's worth it.”

The services were chosen by their terms (checked 2026-11-26): MET Norway's forecast is open data (NLOD 2.0, CC BY 4.0)
that a sold product may use with its credit; Nominatim may be used within its policy, which counts every Whombat
together as one application; most broadcasters offer their RSS for personal, non-commercial use, so Whombat reads news
the way a feed reader does, only from sites the person turned on.

| Id | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WN-01 | **Where this Whombat is** (`Place`, `Burrow.place`): a name, a latitude and longitude kept to two decimals, a country (ISO 3166-1), and where Nominatim gives them its region (ISO 3166-2 level 4), area (level 6) and the region's name. The place the person chose (`burrows.place`), else the one its time zone suggests (`placeFromTimeZone`: the zone's city in `zone.tab`'s country, `guessed`), else none (`UTC`, a standard time's name). `zone.tab`, not `zone1970.tab`, which merges the Netherlands into Brussels. | It works the day a Whombat is set up, without asking anyone; two decimals (about 1 km) are enough for a forecast and no closer to the house. |
| WN-02 | **Looking a place up** (`place.find`): OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, a search by words or a reverse by the device's location rounded to two decimals, `format=jsonv2` with `addressdetails`; up to five places. One request a second per box (a look-up waits up to 5 s for its turn, else “Many look-ups at once”), each answer kept a day, only when someone asks. Settings shows “© OpenStreetMap” by the results. `place.set` keeps one, or null goes back to the guess; signed as `burrow.updated` with the place's name, never its coordinates. | Nominatim's policy: an identifying User-Agent, at most one request a second for the whole application, no background look-ups, credit (ODbL). |
| WN-03 | **The forecast is MET Norway's** Locationforecast 2.0 `complete`, one kept per place in memory. A place is asked again only after MET's `Expires`, never within 30 minutes, 2 to 8 minutes later at random and never on the hour; `If-Modified-Since` with the kept `Last-Modified`, and a 304 keeps it. Asked only while something needs it (a shown widget, the assistant, Looks ahead); a failed read keeps the last forecast and tries again after 10 minutes. Every place shown with MET's data says “Data from MET Norway”; no Yr name or logo. | MET's terms: an identifying User-Agent with a real contact, at most four decimals, respect `Expires`, no requests on the hour, credit, and nothing that looks like Yr. |
| WN-04 | **Sunrise and sunset are worked out on the box** (`sunTimes`, NOAA's equations, about a minute), polar day and night included. **Units**: Celsius and metres per second, Fahrenheit and miles per hour in the United States; rain in millimetres everywhere; no setting. Symbols are MET's `symbol_code`, drawn by the screen. | No second request for what a formula gives; one rule instead of a setting nobody asked for. |
| WN-05 | **News is a feed reader**: Whombat suggests sites for the place's country and region (`NEWS_SUGGESTIONS`), and reads nothing until the person turns a site on or adds one of their own (an https address: a feed as it is, or a page by the `<link rel="alternate">` feed it names, through up to three redirects while adding; the feed's final address is kept). Up to 12 sites. Only the headline, its site, its time and its link: no pictures, no article text. The assistant may turn sites on or off as the person asks; adding a site asks first (its words reach the assistant every 15 minutes, and an address stays the person's to give). | Most broadcasters' feeds are for personal use: the person choosing the sites, as in any feed reader, is the closest to that. |
| WN-06 | **Reading a feed**: through the outbound rules (https only, public addresses, no redirects, 1 MiB, 10 s), with Whombat's User-Agent, kept 15 minutes per feed. RSS 2.0, RSS 1.0 (RDF) and Atom (htmlparser2, XML mode); headlines without markup; an item without a date or a link is left out; a time ahead of now is now; at most 30 per feed; all sites together newest first, each once by link and by the same words. A site that can't be read keeps its headlines and says why (“The site moved its feed”, “Couldn’t read it just now”), and is tried again after 5 minutes. | Some feeds list sections among their articles, or write local time as UTC; a moved feed must not lead the box somewhere new by itself. |
| WN-07 | **The suggestions** come from `scripts/weather-news/news-feeds.psv`, each row a feed checked to answer over https with recent items (188 on 2026-11-26): one “all news” feed per broadcaster for a country, and regional ones by ISO 3166-2 level 4 (`regions`: a province, Land, county or state; several where one site serves several) or level 6 (`areas`: Belgium's provinces). Nearest first: the area's, the region's, the country's. Ireland has RTÉ News and RTÉ Regional. | Region codes are what Nominatim gives, so the right regional broadcaster comes first without a map of our own. |
| WN-08 | **Two widgets** of Whombat's own (desk-display.md DK-200, DK-201): `weather` with its own `weather` view, and `news` as a `list`. Neither is private on a locked screen. Each is read once per burrow, the views made again every few minutes from what the forecast and the feeds keep. | Weather and headlines are public; a screen full of widgets mustn't multiply requests. |
| WN-09 | **Older apps** (session minor below `WEATHER_NEWS_MINOR`) get layouts, views, *Add a widget* and a screen elsewhere's grid without the two widgets and the `weather` view; when such an app saves or tries a layout, the widgets it couldn't see go back on the same page and shape at the same place while it is free, else they are left out. | Their contract's enums are closed: one unknown widget would make the whole layout unreadable. |
| WN-10 | **Session minor 16** (`WEATHER_NEWS_MINOR`): `place.find` and `news.sources` (reads), `place.set`, `news.setSources` and `news.addSite` (changes), the burrow's `place`, and the widgets. In the assistant's settings, on This Whombat's Overview: finding and reading are reads, setting the place and turning sites on or off are changes, adding a site always asks. | “We live in Breda” works in Chat; a site of the person's own stays their yes. |
| WN-11 | **The assistant's tools** `weather_forecast` (here, or a town it names, looked up first, in that town's own hours) and `news_headlines` (the sites that are on; `query` filters here, nothing goes out): read-only, no key, served in the person's own turns (desk Talk is one), in automation steps and in Looks ahead checks; never in outsiders' turns or a look through new mail. | A morning automation may well say the weather; strangers' turns get nothing they didn't need. |
| WN-12 | **Looks ahead** (looks-ahead.md): both tools are a check's (`AHEAD_TOOL_NAMES`, the Hermes policy). The weather of another place only in a calendar check while its web is open, by public words (`aheadQueryRefusal`); here's always. A calendar check gets the forecast where the Whombat is for the plan's day when it is within the week (`AheadDispatchContext.weather`). A weather-only finding names `{ weather: true }`, which Whombat writes as “Data from MET Norway”; a headline's link may be read and named as a source. Weather findings use the kinds there are (travel, other). | The forecast is the check's own context, not a stranger's page; `AheadSource` is only the report's input, which the box serves, while `AheadKind` is read by the apps. |
| WN-13 | **Privacy**: the privacy policy (version 2026-11-26) and privacy-and-security.md name MET Norway, Nominatim and the news sites the person chooses among the services a Whombat talks to itself; each request names Whombat (`Whombat/<version> (+https://whombat.com)`), never the person. Tests never reach the network (`useOpenDataForTests`). | They see the box's address and what it asks for. |

## Weighed and left

- **Open-Meteo**: its free tier is for non-commercial use only; a paid plan for a sold product.
- **Google News RSS**: its feeds are “solely for … a personal feed reader for personal, non-commercial use”.
- **Yle**: its terms rule out paid services and apps.
- **Weather warnings (MeteoAlarm)**: later. Matching a place needs the warning's polygons or EMMA ids, and its terms'
  extra rules for redistribution weren't read in full.
- **The Overview's tiles**: later; its tiles take only things and accounts.
- **BBC's English, Scottish and Welsh regional feeds as suggestions**: their regions have no ISO 3166-2 code a place
  keeps; the person can add theirs as a site of their own.
- **MET's Sunrise API**: a formula on the box gives the same to a minute.
- **A units setting**: metric, Fahrenheit and mph in the United States, until someone asks.
- **The place and the news sites in a backup file**: the box's own backups keep them with the database; a backup file
  doesn't yet.

Open for John: MET Norway's terms ask that a responsible person read and accept them once.
