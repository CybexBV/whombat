# No conversation membership (MB-01…MB-14)

John, 2026-10-26 (through the lead): a Whombat is one person's device with one agent (one-person.md OP-01, one-agent.md
OA-01), so every conversation is the person's with the agent, and the server's multi-person membership model
(`conversation_members`, `isMember`, `memberConversationIds`, `addMembers`, `removeMembers`) is a leftover to remove.
Whombat is not released: nothing is kept for compatibility except John's Home, which migrates in place, and old backup
files, which must still restore.

**What membership did** (2026-10-26, `apps/server/src`, call sites outside tests): 88 access checks (commands, reads,
routes, files, realtime, the turn's context, the MCP tools), 19 behaviours (whom a message wakes and notifies, unread
badges, live activity, plan step notices, `files.changed`, failure cards, requeues and retries, automations' agent
steps and recipes, checks finishing, background jobs, the feedback assessor's room), 17 bookkeeping writes and reads
(making rooms, the GitHub mirror, backups, the seed, `dev:ask`, the guide's last activity, the bus's
`membership.removed`) and 2 of the person's own settings (the notification level, written and read); plus the frames'
per-room audience (41 references in 28 files) and the test fixtures.

## The rule

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| MB-01 | **Membership decides nothing.** The person and the agent act in every conversation of the burrow; Whombat's own signers (GitHub, an automation's, Feedback, the tracker, Servers) act where their own code puts them; an outsiders' turn is confined to its own conversation (MB-04). `conversation_members` and the helpers around it are gone. | OP-01: nobody to keep a room from. |
| MB-02 | **Access checks become burrow checks.** Wherever a command, a read or a route asked “is the actor in this conversation”, it now asks whether the conversation (or the message, plan, canvas, run, file, report or turn behind it) is one of the actor's burrow, and answers as it did for a stranger's id (404, or the same refusal). The person is still the only one with devices, and the agent only acts through `/mcp` with its turn's token, so nobody else reaches these paths. | The burrow is the boundary that is left; the old checks always passed for the person and the agent. |
| MB-03 | **Realtime is the person's devices'.** A subscription is still for an enrolled account and is taken as that account's person principal in the topic's burrow (`principalFor`): an account without a person there (anyone but the person) is refused as before. `conv:`, `run:` and `canvas:` topics need only the thing to be in that burrow (`authorizeTopic`); the per-conversation audience of burrow frames (`outbox.audience_conversation_id`, `session_events.audience_conversation_id`, the publish option `audienceConversationId`), the subscription's membership gate and the bus's `membership.removed` go. | Membership was a second filter for the person's own devices; the account's person in the burrow is the real one. |
| MB-04 | **Superseded in part by outsiders-turns.md OT-04 (its own thread, not its whole conversation) and OT-01 (served only an allowlist).** **An outsiders' turn works only in its own conversation** (`mcp/tools.ts` `withinTurn`, `keepInTurn`): it reads, posts, replies, posts cards, asks for approval, performs actions, uploads files, starts and reports runs, and reads or edits plans and canvases only in the conversation its dispatch is in, reads no canvas without a room (the burrow's), and searches nothing. Before, posting was confined only by the agent's membership, and the agent was in all of the person's rooms; a canvas without a room was readable. Its context never holds the room's window, in any kind of room (`runtime/context.ts`: the window needs the turn's person), where before a branch room's window could reach it when the agent was a member there. | OP-17: what strangers send must not pull the person's rooms into an answer or put words in them. The tool layer is where the turn is known; the commands it runs don't see the dispatch. |
| MB-05 | **The agent is woken in every conversation of the burrow**: a mention, the person's message in their Chat or Feedback room, a plan step a person starts, its pull request's checks finishing, a background job's notify, a quiet wake (Vault and approval answers), a failure card, a turn carried on after a usage limit, Try again, an automation's agent step, a recipe and a described automation. Its search covers every room too (less the branch rooms of switched-off repositories); “Search messages, canvases and runs” no longer widens it, as there is no room it isn't in. **Behaviour change:** an `@Dusty` in a pull request's room that the agent wasn't in (one opened on GitHub by someone else) now wakes it, with that room's window. | The person asked for it by name; the room is the person's (MB-01). Nothing in a branch room is private from the agent. |
| MB-06 | **Notifications and badges are the person's.** Who hears about a message is the burrow's person (`thePerson`), never the agent or a signer, by the person's level there, and nobody for a message in a former person's Chat; unread and mention badges are counted and sent for the person (`personUnreadCounts`); a run's live activity the agent asked for goes to the person; a plan step that needs someone tells the person when the plan is theirs or in their Chat (as before); `files.changed` goes to the person's `me:` topic; any message of the person's settles a waiting agent's status, which reads again whether a question is still open. **Behaviour change:** pull request rooms the person wasn't in now count their unread badges and notify them, at the branch rooms' default (mentions only). | Only the person reads; agents have no inbox. |
| MB-07 | **The person's level per conversation is a column of the conversation** (`conversations.notify`, null = the kind's default, `defaultNotifyLevel`): `conversations.set_notify` sets it, lists and details read it. A member row's `position` (member order) and `joined_at` meant nothing to anyone and go. | It was the person's setting, kept per member only because there were several. |
| MB-08 | **`read_states` stays per principal.** The person's markers, keyed by the person's principal, as before. A restore moves the person's (the restorer's) marker in each conversation it touched. | Keying it by conversation alone would rewrite the read model and its indexes for no gain: one row per conversation either way. |
| MB-09 | **Lists show the person's rooms and every branch room.** The sidebar, `conversations.list` and `dev:ask` list conversations of the burrow that are the person's own Chat, Feedback room and task chats (`dm_person_id` the person) plus every branch room; a former person's Chat (OP-S02: history without an account) isn't listed, though it may be opened by id. **Behaviour change:** the pull request rooms the person wasn't in now show in their sidebar, read (MB-12). Activity, plans, canvases, runs, Files, search, Home's automations and the mail digest read every conversation of the burrow. | The person sees their agent's work; another person's chat from before one-person was never theirs to browse. |
| MB-10 | **What crossed rooms by membership is one rule.** An automation's rooms are checked only to still be the burrow's (`conversationsCheck`: “That room is gone…”); copying files and content between rooms needs no audience comparison (`audienceWithin`, `roomSees` go): every room has the same reader. The feedback assessor no longer needs to be “in” its room (`agent_not_member` goes); the person who set an integration up must still be the burrow's person (`personHere`). | OP-03; the room's audience was always the person and the agent. |
| MB-11 | **Nobody joins a room.** Making the person's Chat, Feedback room, a branch room (opening a pull request, the GitHub mirror, a review), a restored conversation, the seed's rooms and the tests' rooms writes no membership. `postMessage` loses `skipMembershipCheck`: every post checks only the burrow (signers and the agent post where their code says). The test fixtures `joinRoom`, `leaveRoom` and `makeRoom`'s member list go (`makeRoom` makes a branch room); tests that proved someone outside a room is refused go, as one-person.md OP-S01 did for people. | One rule, one place. |

## Migration and backups

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| MB-12 | **Migration `0007_no_membership`, a contract with a checkpoint** (MD-03, MD-06; floors itself). It picks each burrow's person as `thePerson` does (the claimer, else the first person with an account by position), copies that person's notify level from their member row onto `conversations.notify`, and moves the person's read marker to the latest top-level message of every conversation they weren't a member of (forward only, never backwards), so the rooms that now show for them (MB-09) don't light up as unread. Then `conversation_members`, `outbox.audience_conversation_id` and `session_events.audience_conversation_id` go. What it drops is membership (who was in which room, in what order, since when) and frames' audience; nothing a person made. Its test (`db/no-membership.int.test.ts`) migrates a box where the person wasn't in a branch room and had levels of their own. | Home migrates in place; a checkpoint because it drops a table older code reads. |
| MB-13 | **Old backup files restore.** Contracts 3.0's `conversation` record lists no members and Zod drops an older file's `members`; a restore makes rooms without membership and moves the restorer's markers (MB-08). A file made now carries no membership references: a conversation refers to its person (`dm.person`) and its repository, the directory to the authors. | backup-file.md BF-03, BF-04: records survive schema changes. |

## Remaining

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| MB-14 | **Not part of this:** the bus's `principal.removed` (nobody sends it since OP-S08; its listeners stay until someone removes offboarding's last traces), and the contracts' own words about members (`files.summary`'s “the viewer is a member of it”, attachment download “for members of its conversation”, live activity's “People (not agents) who are members of the run's conversation”, `TopicDenialReason`'s `not_member`, MCP's “membership / ownership checks”): their shapes are unchanged and their meaning now reads “the person”. Those are for the contracts when they move into this repository. | Wire contracts stay at 3.0.0 here. |
