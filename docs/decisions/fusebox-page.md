# Fusebox's page (FP)

John, 2026-10-21 (main checkout): “We have Fusebox connected, but it doesn't show up at Services in the sidebar and I
think it deserves a dashboard.” Research and a mockup first (`~/.cache/wb-tmp/mockups/fusebox/`), then “Implement this
fully”, with the four recommendations as given: keep the API-price figure, show every client's use, read only with
“Open Fusebox”, and leave John's Fusebox (0.7.0) as it is.

Builds on ai-gateway.md (AIG-*), connection-pages.md (CP-*), dashboards.md (DS-*) and connections-page.md (CN-*). No
contract change: the page is server-built ThingPage panels (CP-04), so web and phone draw it as they are.

## Server details

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| FP-S01 | `things/kinds/fusebox.ts` reads (as the connection, `getJson`) `/api/overview`, `/api/accounts`, `/api/faults` and, each optional, `/api/faults/dismissed` (still counted, AIG-11; unreadable, the poller's last faults stand in), `/api/clients/usage`, `/api/usage/status` and `/api/usage/dashboard` for this month (start of the month to tomorrow, the burrow's zone; kept 5 minutes, it is Fusebox's heaviest read). Cached 30 s like the other kinds, the page uses `latestRead`. `things/kinds/fusebox-pool.ts` is the pure part: pools, run out, plan words, point keys, glance, status. | The kinds' shared reader (CP-S07); pure logic gets unit tests. |
| FP-S02 | The health pass reads `/api/overview` and the stored accounts (`gateway_usage`, read every minute by the gateway poller) for the glance and points; it doesn't read `/api/accounts` twice. | The poller already holds the accounts. |
| FP-S03 | The weekly chart's forecast is drawn only when it runs out early and the moment is no further ahead than the range is long, so the past keeps at least half the chart. | A 1-hour range with a forecast two days out is a sliver. |
| FP-S04 | Agent turns are counted from `dispatches.gateway_provider` of the burrow over the last 7 days. | AIG-07 records the route per turn. |
| FP-S05 | **Watch** titles a gateway that answers but is bad with its reason (“Fusebox: Claude used up until Thu 09:00”, body “Its page says what is left and when it comes back.”), not “isn’t answering”; a refused management key's body is “Whombat can’t read its limits until then.” as in Needs you. | The other kinds' bad still means not answering; Fusebox's means used up. |
| FP-S06 | **The version without its build** (“0.7.0”, not “0.7.0+gui.81f9ff128ba6”) on the card and in the head; “Fusebox itself” shows it whole. | John's Fusebox runs a `+gui` build; the hash is noise on a card. |
