# Settings and linking the phone app (SPL)

John asked the council (GPT 6.1 Sol, Claude Fable 5.2, Muse Wonder Full 1.0) to rate the web's Settings and the
screen that links the phone app, then to implement what would raise the score (2026-11-10); SPL-15…SPL-20 are the rest
of Settings the council raised, which John asked for after (“you decide the open questions”). The round's scores were
Settings 7.5 / 7.5 / 7.5 and linking 7 / 8 / 6.5; all three named the same first problem: Settings never led to the
QR sign-in. These are the decisions taken to fix what they found. SPL-22 and SPL-23 are John's changes after the fourth
round; SPL-24…SPL-28 are his cleanup of Advanced (2026-11-10).
