# Workstream: spend (SP)

Workstream `agent-capabilities` (2026-09-28; assignment `docs/implementation/agent-capabilities/assignment.md` §6). A
read-only view of what agents' turns used, from the usage Whombat already stores (`dispatches.usage`:
`{inputTokens, outputTokens, costUsd?}` per finished turn, as the provider reported it).

Contracts 0.20.0 (CT-119…), app 0.11.0 (AP-195…). Informational only: no billing, no limits here (the terms forbid
intermediating usage). Binding and unchanged: DG-21… handoff budgets (their sums in `domain/handoff-usage.ts`),
C-10 (membership decides what a member sees).

## Details settled while building (SP-D…)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SP-D01 | **Arithmetic** (core `spend.ts`, pure, unit-tested): a turn counts in a window by `finished_at`; turns without usage count as turns with 0 tokens and raise `unreportedTurns`; `costUsd` is the sum over turns that reported cost, null when none did, and `costTurns` says how many did; tokens format as 1.2M / 340k / 900, cost as “about $3.40” (two decimals, “under $0.01” below a cent). | Honest about partial data. |
| SP-D02 | **Index** `dispatches (burrow_id, finished_at)` in `0048_capabilities` for the window sums. | Sums over 30 days stay cheap. |
| SP-D03 | **What counts as a turn**: a dispatch of the burrow that finished (`done`, `failed` or `cancelled`, with `finished_at`) and ran — claimed at least once (`attempts > 0`) or reported usage. A turn cancelled or refused before any runner took it is not one (no provider ran). Windows count by `finished_at`: **today** from the first instant of the burrow's calendar day to the first of the next (`zonedDayBounds`, found with core's `dayKey`, so it is exactly `spendWindowsOf`'s today, 23- and 25-hour days included); **week** and **month** `finished_at > now − 7 / 30 days`. | Turns that never reached a provider would only inflate “usage not reported”. |
| SP-D04 | **The sums run in SQL**: one query over `dispatches_finished_idx` (`burrow_id`, `finished_at > now − 30 d`), the dispatch's person resolved to its principal in the burrow (`on_behalf_of_account_id`), grouped by agent and person, each window a set of filtered aggregates (turns, input, output, cost, cost turns, unreported). A cost is rounded to micro-dollars per turn before it is summed (`round(numeric, 6)`), which is what `addUsage` does turn by turn, so SQL and the pure fold agree; core's `mergeTotals` folds the group rows into agents, people and totals. At most agents × people rows leave the database; the tests check every number against `spendWindowsFrom` over the same turns. | One indexed scan, arithmetic in one place. |
| SP-D05 | **Superseded by one-person.md OP-S73**: one view of every turn for the person. **Visibility as built**: people only (an agent, or a principal of another burrow: 403). An owner sees everything; by person ends with “Nobody” (turns for no one, or for an account with no person in the burrow). A member sees the turns whose person is them or whose agent is their personal agent: by agent sums only those, by person is their own row only (their personal agent's turns for nobody count in the totals and by agent, not in their row), and handoffs and plans only in conversations they are a member of, with the plan's own numbers (room members already see handoff usage in the plan pane). An owner sees every plan's numbers but its title only when they are in its conversation (SP-D10). `spend.agent`: an owner gets `all` for every agent, another person's personal agent included (the overview lists it too); a member gets `yours` — the turns done for them, or every turn of their own personal agent; another person's personal agent, a person's handle or a system principal is not found (404). | SP-01's rule, with its edges decided. |
| SP-D06 | **Handoffs and plans**: open handoffs are core's `handoffOpen` (not done or cancelled, not declined back); a budget is the tokens written into the handoff, else `WHOMBAT_HANDOFF_TOKENS_DEFAULT`; used is `stepTokens` (the owner's turns for the step, all time, as DG-11 counts). Plans are ranked by the tokens of their turns (`dispatches.plan_id`: plan-step and handoff turns; `handoff_result` turns carry no plan) finished in the last 30 days, then by turns; `usedTokens` is that 30-day number (the key of “most used first”), `budgetTokens` the plan cap `WHOMBAT_HANDOFF_PLAN_TOKENS` when the plan has handoffs (DG-11 caps its handoff turns), null otherwise; `title` is the plan's for a member of its conversation, else “A plan in a private conversation” (SP-D10). Agents, people and plans without a turn in 30 days are left out. | The contract's fields, filled from the numbers delegation already keeps. |
| SP-D07 | **Managed appliances need nothing extra**: the session allowlist is every `api.<area>.<action>` of core (`createOperationAllowlist`), and the dispatcher runs the REST handler in-process as the session's account, so both endpoints answer over a session with the same visibility (`managed/spend.int.test.ts`). | The web app reaches appliances through the dispatcher. |
| SP-D10 | **Superseded by one-person.md OP-S73**: plan titles always show. **Plan titles only for their conversation's members** (security review M8, C-10): `topPlans` names a plan only to a viewer who is a member of its conversation; an owner who isn't reads “A plan in a private conversation” (`SPEND_PRIVATE_PLAN`, `read/spend.ts`) with the plan's id and numbers (it still counts in their burrow view). Members see only their conversations' plans anyway (SP-D05). | A plan's title says what a private conversation is about; the owners' Work view already hides it. |
