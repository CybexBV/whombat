# UI refresh: status, shell and Overview (UIR)

From the 2026-11-04 design council (three reviewers, two rounds; the final spec and its mockup are the reference) and
John's “implement this fully”. This file covers one status scale (spec §1), the shell on a wide and a narrow screen
(§6) and Overview (§5). The Inbox rows, approval cards, Chat and service pages are other files' (they use the scale and
the two tokens below). Decisions in force that the spec met: the sidebar keeps Servers and Connections as tabs
(`sidebar-tabs.md` SBT-03), so the spec's “two labelled groups” isn't built; the status marks and the Settings row are.

Code: `packages/tokens/src/colors.ts`, `packages/client/src/home/statusLevel.ts` (and `things.ts`, `sections.ts`,
`greeting.ts`), web `components/StatusMark/`, `app/shell/` (`AssistantSidebar`, `ShellLayout`, `NeedsYouBarLink`,
`BurrowShell`), `features/home/` (`HomeScreen`, `useHandleIt.ts`). No contract,
schema or migration change.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| UIR-01 | **Two new colour tokens, `--warn-text` and `--ok-text`** (`statusTextColors`, apart from `ThemeColors` so the feedback widget's loader stays within its 8 KB budget): light `#765b27` and `#36694a` (warn and ok taken toward ink), dark the same as `--warn` and `--ok`. Both reach 4.5:1 on all four surfaces in both themes, tested in `contrast.test.ts`; `--bad` is tested on `sf2` and `side` too, since the “Not working” pill sets it there. A camel-case theme key becomes a dashed CSS name (`themeColorCssName`), digits stay attached (`--sf2`). | `--warn` and `--ok` fail 4.5:1 as text on `sf2` in light (D-A11Y-01); a status word on a pill needs a colour that passes everywhere. |
| UIR-02 | **One status scale: Not working · Needs a look · Learning · Connected**, with a reason of at most 60 characters, derived in `@whombat/client` (`statusLevel.ts`) from what the Whombat already sends: a thing's `status` and `reason` (bad and offline are not working, warn needs a look, learning learning, ok and not checked yet connected), a server's fleet status (waiting for its first report is learning), an Inbox item's severity (a `bad` problem is not working; asks, only-you lines and the rest need a look). | Web and phone read the same words without a new contract field: everything the scale needs is already there (the spec's server-side `level`/`reason` would have repeated it). |
| UIR-03 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-07** (a server that is fine has no pill on Overview). **Words**: the pill says “Not working”, or “Offline” for a thing that is offline (its own word); “Needs a look”; “Learning”; “Connected”. A server's card keeps “Running” for a server that is fine (CN-12). | The spec: the item's own words, else “Not working”; Connected kept. |
| UIR-04 | **Marks by shape** (`StatusMark`): not working a dot inside a 1.5px `--bad` ring, 12px across; needs a look an 8px filled `--warn` dot; learning an 8px hollow 1.5px `--mut` ring; connected none. The not-working dot is 6px with a 1.5px gap, as the approved mockup draws it: the spec's “8px dot, 2px gap, 1.5px ring” adds up to 15px, not the 12px it names. | Shape carries the level without colour; the mockup was what was approved. |
| UIR-05 | **Each mark has an accessible name**, the thing and why: “blackbox: disk array failed”, “pve-02: needs a look” without a reason (`statusMarkLabel`). A sidebar row's name is that label (just the name when connected); the row's tooltip stays “blackbox · Disk array failed”. | One short phrase a screen reader says, as the spec asks. |
| UIR-06 | **The sidebar never sorts by level**: rows stay in the server's order. Overview's connection cards sort worst first, keeping the server's order within a level (`sortByStatusLevel`); the Inbox may use the same function. | A list that reorders itself under the pointer is hard to use; a page of cards is read top-down. |
| UIR-07 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-01, OVP-36** (Overview's jump links carry the worst mark, not tabs). **The sidebar's “learning” word and the offline grey ring are gone**: a device being learned shows the learning ring, an offline server the not-working mark. The tab that isn't open (SBT-04) and Overview's tabs carry the worst mark's shape too (“something isn’t working” / “something needs a look”). | One scale everywhere; offline is the loudest state, not a quiet grey. |
| UIR-08 | **Superseded by final-shell-web.md FSW-06 (Settings is the ninth place; the person is not a link).** **The person's row is the way to Settings**: a link with their name and “Settings” under it (no title), labelled “Mara Okafor, Settings”, selected (`aria-current="page"`, the `sel` look) while Settings is open. The account menu (Mobile app, notifications, the theme, Sign out, the version) opens from a ⋯ button beside it, labelled “Account”; Settings left that menu. Replaces WS-10's “user row is a button that opens the account menu”. | The spec's labelled Settings row, without losing the menu's other entries (same functions). |
| UIR-09 | **The narrow screen's bar is one 56px row** (below 1024px, the drawer breakpoint): ☰ 44×44 with no border, the page's title, and what needs the person on the right. | Spec §6. |
| UIR-10 | **Superseded by final-shell-web.md FSW-07 (the bar holds Places, Search and the Inbox cue).** **The page's large title stays in the page; the bar shows it only after it scrolled away** under the bar (an `IntersectionObserver` on the page's first `h1`, found again when the page changes). Pages without an `h1` show no title. Not on a wide screen. | Big titles orient on arrival; the bar keeps you oriented once they're gone. |
| UIR-11 | **Superseded by final-shell-web.md FSW-07 (“3 need you ›”, red only when one isn’t working).** **“6 things need you” in the bar is a 44px pill** to the Inbox: the count amber, red only when one of the things that need the person isn't working (an Inbox item of level not working); hidden on the Inbox itself. | The red count was used for any `bad` item, approvals included; red means broken. |
| UIR-12 | **The drawer** is `min(85vw, 320px)` wide, a modal dialog named “Sidebar” while open (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`), focus kept inside and given back to ☰ when it closes; Esc, a tap on the scrim, a link in it and a visible 44px “Close the sidebar” button (top right, first in it, so it takes the focus) close it. | Spec §6; a phone has no Esc key. |
| UIR-13 | **Every target in the drawer is 44px**: places, servers, connections, the list tabs, the list's `+`, ⋯ and the account menu's items; the bar's pill and ☰ too. Rows stack without a gap (44px each); separate small controls side by side keep 8px. | Spec §6: 44×44 targets on a phone. |
| UIR-14 | **Superseded by overview-server-connection-final.md OSC-01.** **Overview's headline keeps its one red sentence; when it names an Inbox item it is a link to that row** (`/inbox?item=…`, `HomeStatus.itemId`), followed by a secondary “Ask {assistant} to handle it” button, the Inbox's own handle action (`looks.handle` on the thing it is about, else the question in the thing's thread; `useHandleIt`). No new panel. When the sentence names no item (something offline, a count), it stays text. | Spec §5; one loud place per event, the Inbox row, and the headline leads there. |
| UIR-15 | **Superseded by overview-one-page.md OVP-09** (a connection is a small tile with one line, its mark at the top right). **One card for every connection**: logo tile 44px, the name 17/600 that wraps and is never cut, what it is under it, the pill (mono, mark and word, `sf2`, the level's text colour) top-right, one `--ink2` line of why for anything not connected, its figures, and a left-aligned `--mut` foot. A device being learned says “{assistant} is learning what it can do. …” as its line and shows no figures. | Spec §5; the cards had three looks (with figures, without, learning). |
| UIR-16 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-24** (a tile's line has no full stop; the fallback words stand). **A card's why line** is the thing's `reason` as a sentence (capital, full stop); without one: offline “Whombat can’t reach it right now.”, a problem “It isn’t working. Open it to see why.”, needs a look “Open it to see what needs a look.” | The spec asks for a line on everything not connected; when the Whombat doesn't know why, say what the person can do. |
| UIR-17 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-09** (the foot says what it is on the left when the line didn't, and when it was last used on the right). **The foot says only “Used today”** (`usedAt`); no “Checked 13:55”. `ThingSummary` carries no time it was last checked; adding one is a contract change for the server's every kind and is left for when a page needs it. A card that was never used has no foot. | Built from the data that is there, as the brief asked. |
| UIR-18 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-01** (the words are the Connections section's summary line). **The summary words are the scale's**: “2 connected · 2 need a look · 1 learning”, with “1 not working” first when there is one (`statusSummary`), on Overview's Connections tab and its group head. `thingsLine` (“2 working …”) stays for the phone app until it moves. | Spec §5; “working” and “Connected” for the same thing was two words. |
| UIR-19 | **Superseded by overview-one-page.md OVP-09** (a tile says its first two figures in its line). **Figures on a narrow card**: three across while each has 110px (the card's content at least 346px, a container query), else two with the third under them across both; labels wrap between words and never inside one; tiles in a row share a height, their values on one baseline at the foot. The value is sans 600 20px with its rest in mono, as the mockup. | Spec §5; “INTEGRATIO” was cut at 390px. |
| UIR-20 | **Superseded by overview-one-page.md OVP-01, OVP-10, OVP-11** (sections with jump links; Add a connection in its heading; cards and tiles one across under 620 px). **At phone width** (Overview's own width under 560px): the pill goes under the name, the headline's button and “Add a connection” each take the page's width at 44px under the headline, Overview's tabs are 44px targets, and the tab summary sits on its own line under them. | Spec §5 and the mockup's phone frame. |
| UIR-21 | **Superseded by overview-one-page.md OVP-02, OVP-09, OVP-11** (servers one list; tiles in their own grid). **Connection cards are laid out `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(100%, 400px), 1fr))`**, 16px apart: two across at 1440, one on a phone. Server cards keep their grid (CN-12). | The mockup's grid; a server card's four tiles need more room. |
| UIR-22 | **Superseded in part by overview-one-page.md OVP-07** (the pill shows only when the server isn't fine). **A server's card uses the same pill** (`LevelPill`, its level from the fleet status), so Overview has one pill. | One scale, one look. |
