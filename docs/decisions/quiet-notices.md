# Quiet notices: what Whombat watches, and Ignore (QN)

John, 2026-10-22 (main checkout): UniFi said “Access point offline” for an access point he took offline on purpose, and
the only way out was “Look into it”, which asks the assistant to find a problem that isn't there. On the desk, Fusebox's
“Codex’s week runs out early” offered “Ask Neo to fix it”, for something that is information and nothing an agent can
fix. “I would like this to be much more smarter and also give me controls to ignore certain things Whombat is
monitoring.” My research and two mocks; he asked for the desk's “It’s on purpose” to be just **Ignore** (until it's back
online), for it to work for every service, not UniFi only, and then: “Implement this fully”.

The research catalogued every check Whombat runs today: 256, of which 117 are problems, 59 only a person can act on and
80 are heads-ups (`docs/implementation/quiet-notices/catalogue-*.md`, one row per notice with its check, subject, nature,
when Ignore lifts and noise notes). Contracts 0.122.0 (CT-1060…CT-1099), whombat-app 0.124.0 (AP-4800…AP-4899),
migration 0136, appliance console CG-330…CG-349, hosts HS-190…HS-209. Server details are QN-S* below; the brief
is `docs/implementation/quiet-notices/assignment.md`.

Changes earlier decisions: SD-05 (the dot comes from the active lines that aren't heads-ups), FP-05/FP-09 (a week that
runs out early is a heads-up, no “Look into it”), DK-96 (the desk asks the assistant to *look into* it, problems only),
DK-99 (*Not now* and *Ignore* for a look are built), ONE-24 (Ignore on a server alert or a connection is the shared one).

## What a line is

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| QN-01 | **Every line in Worth a look is one of three kinds** (`nature`): **Problem** (`problem`: something is wrong that the assistant can look into), **Only you** (`you`: only a person can do it: sign in again, pay, approve, replace a disk, decide), **Heads-up** (`fyi`: a forecast, an update, plain information). | “Look into it” and “Ask Neo to fix it” were offered for everything, also for a forecast or a sign-in only John can do. |
| QN-02 | **Superseded in part by needs-you-list.md NYL-03**: a server's heads-ups are in the Needs you list too, folded and never counted. **What each kind gets.** Problem: colours the dot, becomes the card's reason and a desk card, keeps “Look into it” (its question). Only you: colours the dot, reason and desk card, with the button to where it's done (the service's page, Sign in again) and no assistant button. Heads-up: on its page only, never a dot, never the desk, never Watch or Needs you. | The dot and the desk are for what needs someone; a heads-up is worth seeing when you look, not worth being told. |
| QN-03 | **Superseded in part by PWD-04, PWD-05 (plain-words.md): three lines per check, updates one line.** **One check, one subject.** Every line names its check (`unifi.device_offline`, `conn.signin`, `host.disk.space_high`) and, when it is about one item, that item (`device:<UniFi id>`, a container's name, an invoice's id). Lines that grouped several items (“2 access points offline”) become one line per item; past eight, the rest is one line (“and 4 more”) that Ignore takes as it is. | Ignoring one access point must not ignore the next one that goes down. |
| QN-04 | **Smarter defaults** (`catalogue-*.md` noise notes): checks that flap (a reboot after a firmware update, a camera reconnecting, a server starting) colour the dot only after two checks in a row; things done on purpose are heads-ups (a container stopped normally, an OPNsense gateway switched off by hand, a Pi-hole pause pressed on Whombat's own page, an indexer switched off for a while); late backups follow the job's own schedule; a busy gateway is a 30-minute average, not one reading; forecasts (a week that runs out early, traffic at 80 %, a disk full in weeks) are heads-ups until they are near; duplicate lines go. | Most of what made the sidebar yellow was not something anyone had to do. |

## Ignore, Remind me tomorrow, Stop watching

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| QN-10 | **Superseded in part by one-person.md OP-S07, OP-S50**: the ⋯ menu stands on every line that can be quieted, for the person. **A ⋯ menu on every line that can be quieted**, web and phone, for people who may manage the thing (owners and managers; servers: owners): **Ignore** (with a line under it saying when you'll hear again, in the check's own words: “Until it’s back online.”, “Until it’s renewed.”, “Until the week resets.”), **Remind me tomorrow**, and **Stop watching {item}** (or **Stop watching this** for a check about the whole thing). Look only and guests see no menu. | One way to quiet anything, where you see it. |
| QN-11 | **Superseded in part by on-purpose.md OPS-01** (gone for about an hour of passes, not twice in a row, ends an Ignore). **Ignore lasts until it changes**, one of three ways per check: *fixed* (the check saw it back to normal twice in a row, on checks where that part was actually read: an unread or capped part is never “fixed”), *newer* (a newer version, another failure, a different set of items), *period* (the window it is about resets: the week, the month). Whatever the way, it **ends early when it gets worse**: amber to red, or a higher band (75 % → 90 % full). Then the line is back, as new. | John's access point: ignored while it is off, and he hears when it goes off again later. |
| QN-12 | **Remind me tomorrow** quiets it until 09:00 tomorrow in the Whombat's time zone (Home's `SNOOZE_HOUR`), and also ends early when it gets worse. The desk's **Not now** for a look is the same. | One meaning for “not now” everywhere (DK-S19). |
| QN-13 | **Superseded in part by on-purpose.md OPS-03…OPS-06** (the assistant switches a check on the person's word; Needs you rows offer it). **Stop watching** turns a check off for one item, or for the whole thing: its lines no longer show at all and it never colours anything, until someone turns it back on. Kept until undone. | “I never want to hear about the attic access point.” |
| QN-14 | **Superseded in part by QN-27** (where a quiet line shows: folded with the heads-ups). **Shared for the whole Whombat**, with who and when: the line stays on the page, greyed at the bottom of Worth a look, “Ignored by John until it’s back online” / “Snoozed by John until tomorrow 09:00”, with **Undo**. It doesn't count for the dot, the card's reason, the page's badge, the desk, Watch or Needs you. The page's figures stay true (“3 of 4 access points online”). | The dot and the desk are shared; an ignore for one person would leave them amber for everyone else. |
| QN-15 | **What Whombat watches**: on every connection's and server's Settings tab, one switch per check in plain words (“Access points and switches going offline”), and under it what is excepted (“Not for: Garage AP until it’s back · Attic AP always”), each with Undo. The same list on web and phone. | Where you find, and take back, what was quieted. |
| QN-16 | **Ignore stays inside Whombat**: it never writes to the service (no TrueNAS *Dismiss*, no Pi-hole message cleared). What the service already dismissed stays respected (Home Assistant's ignored repairs, Grafana's silences), and a line's own buttons (Clear warnings, Dismiss, Mark seen) stay as they are. | Writing to a service changes it for everyone who uses it and would have to ask first. |
| QN-17 | **Recorded**: Ignore, Remind me tomorrow, Stop watching and their Undo are changes in the thing's Activity (“John ignored “Hallway AP is offline” until it’s back online”), kept out of Done recently (SD-08, everyday). An ignore ending by itself is not recorded; the line simply comes back. | Who quieted what is worth knowing; the end of it is not news. |
