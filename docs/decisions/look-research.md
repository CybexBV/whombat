# The assistant looks first (LR)

John, 2026-10-27 (main checkout): the desk said “Home Assistant needs a look” with *Ask Dusty to fix it* and *Not now*.
“We have no clue what's going on, because the message is not telling us anything. Instead of this we need Dusty to go
ahead and research it and create a description what's wrong. Based on that it can decide what options it has for the
user. For example if it is unable to fix it, it should not show a fix button.” My proposal (mock in that thread: the
card while looking, with a step, only you can, fixed) and his answers: research **automatically**; the step's button
**is** the go-ahead for that step; **Ignore instead of Not now** on the card; the assistant **may fix small things by
itself** and say so.

Contracts 3.5 (`LookResearch` in core `domain/look-research.ts`; `DeskLook.research`, `DeskHero.research`/`fix`,
`NeedsYouItem.research`, `ThingPage.research`, `looks.fix`, the desk's `look/fix`). The brief is
`docs/implementation/look-research/assignment.md`. Changes DK-96 (the desk's fix button), DK-113 (the hero's buttons)
and QN-20 (a look's buttons) for a look the assistant has looked into; without research a look stays as it was, but
for its hero's *Ignore* (LR-10).

John, 2026-10-29 (main checkout): “we are seeing lot's of ‘the-titan needs a look. I'm looking into it’ type of
messages. This is not only annoying, this is also not needed I think.” Home had opened 27 look threads in two days, 12
about the-titan, and 13 of them repeated an answer the assistant had already given: a check on the-titan failed for a
few minutes whenever a build ran, and each time it came back it was a new problem. He agreed to the four changes now in
LR-02…LR-04 and LR-S23…LR-S24: a look that comes back as it was gets its finding back, a thing keeps one thread, that
thread settles when the problem goes, and one thing is looked into at most twice a day (contracts 3.10:
`ThreadSettle.how` `resolved`; migration 0016).

## What happens

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LR-01 | **Every look is looked into by itself.** A server, connection or device that is down, in trouble or needs a look (the desk's looks: `deskLooks`, problems and only-you lines, never heads-ups) is looked into by the assistant without anyone asking, once it has been there for two of Whombat's passes in a row (a blip that clears at once isn't). | John: “Dusty go ahead and research it.” The card should say what is wrong before anyone has to ask. |
| LR-02 | **Once per problem.** One look is one research, kept while the look stays (its key, its line's check and item). A look that went and comes back as it was within a week gets that research back at once: the card says again what the assistant found, with its step, and nothing is posted and no turn runs. It is looked into again when it gets worse (needs a look → in trouble → down) or its line changes, or when it comes back and the last research is a week old or didn't end in a finding (fixed, failed). Quieted looks (Ignore, Not now, not watched) are never looked into. | The same problem shouldn't cost a turn every five minutes, nor a new thread each time a check comes and goes: on Home a cleanup check that failed while builds ran opened eleven threads in two days with the same answer. |
| LR-03 | **Superseded in part by handle-it.md HDL-01, HDL-07** (a research covers every line of a thing; the person’s ask doesn’t count against the day). **A budget.** At most 12 researches a day per Whombat, 2 of one thing, and one at a time; the rest wait their turn in the order the desk shows them, and past the budget a look shows as it did before (its reason, *Ask {assistant} to look into it*). | A noisy day must not use up the person's AI allowance, and one noisy thing must not use up the day's. |
| LR-04 | **Superseded in part by handle-it.md HDL-10** (a finding the person must say yes to pushes once; the thread stays quiet). **Where it happens.** In the thing's own thread of the person's Chat, started by the assistant with one plain line (“Home Assistant needs a look. I’m looking into it.”); a later research of the same thing says the same line as a reply there, which opens the thread again if it settled. *Ask {assistant}* in the apps opens it with everything the assistant found, and the person can carry on there. The look's thread never pushes to the phone or chimes: the look itself is what tells the person. When the look goes and the person didn't write in it, the thread settles by itself (“Settled when the problem went away”). | One place per thing for the whole story; the card is what interrupts, not the chat, and a room of open threads shows what is still going on. |
| LR-05 | **How it looks into it.** The person's turn (it reads what the person's turns read: the connection, the server, recent changes, memory), told what the look is and since when. It finds out what is wrong and what that means for the person, and ends with `look_report`: a title, one to three plain sentences, whether it fixed it, and the one step it offers, if any. Its last message in the thread says the same in its own words. | The person's turn is the only one that can read the connection and the server; a structured end is what the screens can draw. |
| LR-06 | **Small fixes by itself.** When the connection or server lets it manage (not *Look only*) and a step is small, safe and undoable — restart or reload one integration, add-on, container or service, retry a job — and nothing about it asks the person, it takes it, checks it worked, says so (`fixed`) and records it in Done today. Anything bigger, or anything that asks, it offers instead. | John: yes, let it fix small things by itself and say so. |
| LR-07 | **Superseded in part by handle-it.md HDL-03** (several steps are one plan with Go ahead; one step stays its own button). **One step offered, named on its button.** A step it can take but shouldn't take alone is offered as one button with the step's own words (“Restart Zigbee”, at most 32 characters). No step when it can't do it (only the person can: power, a cable, a sign-in, a payment) or when the connection is *Look only*: the summary then says what the person can do. | John: “if it is unable to fix it, it should not show a fix button.” |
| LR-08 | **The button is the go-ahead.** Pressing the step (`looks.fix`, the desk's `look/fix`) posts the person's own words in the look's thread (“Go ahead: restart Zigbee.”) and wakes the assistant to take exactly that step; what its tools always ask a person (deleting, stopping an array) still asks. It then checks the step worked and reports again: `fixed`, or what it found and the next step. | John: the button is the approval. The rules that always ask stay, they are the person's own. |
| LR-09 | **What the screens show.** While `looking`: the look as before with “I’m looking into it.” (the reason so far after it). `found`: the assistant's title and summary are the card's words; the step's button first, then *Ignore*, then *Ask {assistant}* (apps: the thread; desk: Talk about it) and *Open* for a server. `fixing`: “I’m on it.” `fixed`: the assistant's title (“Zigbee works again”) and summary in green until the look is gone. `failed` or none: as before. | Same story on the desk, Home and the thing's page. |
| LR-10 | **Ignore, not Not now, on the hero.** Every look's hero, looked into or not, offers *Ignore* (until it changes, QN-11) where it offered *Not now*; the card behind it keeps both. | John: “we also need an ignore button instead of Not now.” |
| LR-11 | **Older parts.** Every new field is optional: an older console or app shows the look as before; a newer one on an older Whombat gets no research and does the same. | The runtime, the console and the apps don't update at the same moment. |
| LR-12 | **Superseded in part by handle-it.md HDL-09, HDL-03** (a server’s page shows the research; a plan of several steps is one press). **Not now:** research on a server's own page and the Services tiles; a second opinion (“Look again”); the person choosing which looks are looked into or the budget; steps that need more than one press. | Smallest useful first. |

## Server details

Built on the runtime side (branch `looks-s`): migration 0012, `apps/server/src/looks/` (`research.ts`, `watch.ts`,
`threads.ts`), `home/jobs.ts`, `home/overview.ts`, `home/routes.ts`, `things/page.ts`, `desk/glance.ts`, `desk/calm.ts`,
`desk/service.ts`, `desk/socket.ts`, `mcp/served.ts`, `mcp/server.ts`, `mcp/tools.ts`, `runtime/context.ts`,
`domain/message-notify.ts`; core's triggers, `DispatchContext.look`, `look_report` and the calm hero; agent-runtime's
`lookTurnLines`.

| # | Detail | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LR-S01 | **Superseded in part by handle-it.md HDL-S01** (the row also keeps every line it covers, its plan, who asked, its card and its push). **`look_researches`** (migration 0012): the look's key, kind, name, its line (`check_id`, `subject`, '' for the thing), its rank (0 down, 1 in trouble, 2 to look at), Whombat's own words when it began (`reason`, the line's `question`), `since`, the state and the assistant's title, summary and step (`fix_label` shown, `fix_step` never), the thread (conversation, root), the turn on it now (`dispatch_id`), `started_at`, `state_at` (`LookResearch.at`), `reported_at` and `gone_at`. One live row per look (unique on burrow and key while `gone_at` is null); history goes 30 days after the look went, at the watcher's settle. Four commands write it through the pipeline (`look.research_start`, `look.report`, `look.fix`, `look.settle`); their events carry ids, never the words. | One row is one research of one problem (LR-02); the thread keeps the story, the row only what the screens draw. |
| LR-S02 | **The watcher is a home job every minute** (`home.looks`, `LookWatcher`): per burrow, the person (`thePerson`), `things()` and the servers' cards (`deskFleet`) through `deskLooks` with the line behind each, as the desk reads them, up to 50. A pass that can't read Home does nothing; one that can't read the servers keeps the servers' research (a missing server look is unknown, not gone). | The desk's looks are what the person sees; the job runs whether a screen is paired or not. |
| LR-S03 | **Two passes in a row** means the same look (key, rank, line and item) on the pass before and this one. What the pass before saw is kept in memory, so after a restart a look waits one pass more. | A blip that clears within a minute isn't looked into; nothing to store for it. |
| LR-S04 | **Superseded by handle-it.md HDL-02** (a new problem is a worse look or a line the research didn’t cover). **A new problem** is a look worse than the worst it was while its research lasted (its rank isn't raised when it gets better), or another line or item when both lines are known (a read that doesn't know the line says nothing). Its research ends (`gone_at`) and, once seen on two passes, a new one starts. A look that went and came back is a new row, unless its last research is given back (LR-S23). | Once per problem (LR-02) without flapping on reads that lose the line. |
| LR-S05 | **What is weighed**: every desk look except a heads-up (`nature: fyi`, which never makes a look anyway): problems, only-you lines and looks whose line isn't known. A quieted look is no look in `deskLooks`, so it is never weighed. | LR-01's list, with the server cards read some other way kept. |
| LR-S06 | **The budget** counts the rows started since the start of the burrow's day (failed ones too), in all and of each look's key (`RESEARCH_PER_THING`, 2); one at a time means no live row `looking`. Past it nothing starts: the look shows as before. A research given back (LR-S23) and a retry (LR-S21) aren't rows started. | LR-03. |
| LR-S07 | **Only when a turn could run**: the agent's provider must be ready for the person (`providerReadiness`: a runner, the sign-in, the licence); otherwise the look waits and nothing counts. | A research that can't run would only post “I can’t answer” threads and use the day's budget. |
| LR-S08 | **Settling**: a research whose `look` turn ended (done, failed, cancelled) without a report is `failed`; one `looking` for 2 hours (no runner took its turn) too; a `look_fix` turn that ended without a report offers its step again (`found`). | `failed` lets the screens say what they said before (LR-09); the person can press again when the step's turn broke. |
| LR-S09 | **A fix that didn't hold**: a look still there 15 minutes after the assistant said `fixed` (three health passes) is a new problem: its green words go and it is looked into again. | Green until the look is gone (LR-09) must not stay true when the look doesn't go. |
| LR-S10 | **The thread**: the assistant's own message in the person's Chat (`ensurePersonalDm`): “{name} isn’t answering.” for a down server, “{name} is down.” for another down thing, else “{name} needs a look.”, then “I’m looking into it.”; a reply in the thread of the thing's latest research while that thread's first message is there and newer than the room's Clear chats, else a new top-level message whose subject is the thing (CHT-05); its client id `look:<id>`; no dispatch, no mention, no push, no inbox row. The turn is `wakeQuietly` with trigger `look`, its own (`own: true`), for the person, in that thread, the opening its trigger message; it resumes the thread's session, so it knows what was found before. | LR-04: the person can carry on in the thread from *Ask {assistant}*. A thread the person cleared away isn't brought back. |
| LR-S11 | **The thread stays quiet**: the assistant's messages in a thread a research row names make no notification (`notifyForMessage`), so no push, no inbox row and no chime. | LR-04: the look itself tells the person. |
| LR-S12 | **Triggers**: `look` and `look_fix` are server and runner only; clients read `notify` and `mention`. `look_fix` follows the thread's model choice and takes the person's follow-ups as a person's turn does (`THREAD_CHOICE_TRIGGERS`, steering); `look` neither. | The press is the person's own message; the research is Whombat's. |
| LR-S13 | **`DispatchContext.look`** from the row whose `dispatch_id` is the turn's: key, kind, name, how bad, Whombat's words and line when it began, since (a down server's last-seen), the connection's handle and access (`read` is Look only), the server's id; a `look_fix` turn adds what it found and the step (label and its own words). | The person's turn reads the connection and the server itself (LR-05); the context only says which. |
| LR-S14 | **The turn's lines** (`lookTurnLines`, in the request, not the preface): about 260 tokens for a look (what Whombat saw as delimited data, where, read first, the small undoable steps where it may manage, `look_report`, then one short colleague's message), about 150 for the step. `look_report`'s rules are in its description, served only in those turns. | Short: the preface every turn pays for doesn't grow. |
| LR-S15 | **`look_report`** (contract `LookReportInput`, strict; no `fix` with `fixed`): served and run only when the turn is a `look` or `look_fix` turn with a research row (`looksInTurn`); elsewhere refused with `NOT_A_LOOK`. The last report counts. Words are made one line (`deskText`); the step is dropped on Look only. Found → `found` with or without a step; fixed → `fixed`, the step cleared. | LR-05…LR-07 checked where they can't be forgotten. |
| LR-S16 | **Fixed is Done today**: the same commit runs `report_done`'s command for the look's thread with the assistant's title (cut to 60). | LR-06: it says so where the person reads what it did. |
| LR-S17 | **`looks.fix`** (session op and route) and the desk's `look/fix` are one command, `look.fix`, as the person: the live research must be `found` with a step (else 404 `not_found`), the connection not Look only (else 403 `not_allowed`); a lapsed licence refuses it. The desk's needs the screen unlocked, acts as the paired person, and reads the glance again. | LR-08: the press is the go-ahead for that one step. |

So far: {the detail without its last stop}.”; `found` and `fixed` the assistant's title and summary (cut to 200 for an older console), `found` the step's label as `fix`; `fixing` the assistant's title and “I’m on it.”; `failed` or none as before. The tone stays the look's: `research` tells the screen to draw a fixed look green. | LR-09 in the one pure planner the runtime and the preview share. |
