# Chat rows, threads and cards from the final design (CTC-01…CTC-17)

John accepted the final design of the 2026-11 UI council (`docs/design-handoff/council-ui-2026-11/16-final/`,
`chat-list/` and `thread/`, both NOTES.md). This is how the web app and the phone app (iOS and Android) build it. The
approval card is another stream's; briefs are removed (`brief-removed.md`) and get no row or card here.

Supersedes, as John's acceptance says: council-web.md AP-7626 (the final card's visible score table) and AP-7718 (the
last answers as live rows), delegation-web.md AP-7704 (pictures under the task's row on the card), delegation-phone.md
AP-7730 and AP-7731 (the phone's folded line and row layout, now under Details). In part: ui-refresh-chat.md UIR-64 and
phone-refresh-asks.md AP-7417 (the waiting dot's colour), one-whombat-shell.md AP-1260 (a plan in a chat as the compact
step list), t3code-handoff.md AP-6612's words on a Chat row. UIR-65, UIR-66, UIR-67, AP-2408, TU-05 and card-moves.md
CM-01…CM-06 stay; the live-card strip (archived `live-card-strip.md`, AP-7760…AP-7766) stays gone.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CTC-01 | **A Chat or Feedback thread's stage comes only from its flags** (`threadStage`, `@whombat/client` `chat/stage.ts`): Waiting for you (`ChatThread.waiting`) > {assistant} is working (`working`, or the assistant's own live turn there) > Handed over (`handedOver`, or a T3 Code thread from it not ended) > Settled (`settled`). No flag is no stage, not Settled. The assistant's name is the name helper's. | A row must never guess from its replies; the flags are what the Inbox and Settle read too. |
| CTC-02 | **Every first message in Chat and Feedback has its replies row**, so the whole row opens the thread: “3 replies · Last reply 2 min ago”, or **“No replies yet”** before the first reply, then the stage's mark and word (an open ring for working, the branch arrow for Handed over with T3 Code's minutes, a tick for Settled). Its screen-reader name says each fact once: replies, “New replies” when the unread marker says so (TU-05), the stage, the question, “open the thread” (`threadRowLabel`). Replaces the “Neo is looking into it” / “T3 Code is working on it” work line on Chat and Feedback rows; other rooms keep it. | One place on every row says where the thread stands, in the same words everywhere. |
| CTC-03 | **The waiting dot is `chart1`**, not `warn`, on the web chip (UIR-64 keeps its place and Answer) and the phone's joined row (AP-7417 keeps “3 replies · ● Waiting for you”); the words are `warn-text` 600. Health dots keep warn and bad. | A thread waiting for an answer isn't a server running warm. |
| CTC-04 | **Superseded by CTC-17.** **Your own messages show no name and no face** in Chat, Feedback, a task chat and their threads, on the web and the phone; the time stays and the text starts where the faces do. Screen readers say “Your message, 13:52”; the ⋯ is “More actions for your message”. Other rooms (channels, branch rooms) keep names. | In a chat with your assistant your own name is noise; the mockups draw it so on every platform. |
| CTC-05 | **At phone width or with a touch screen the web's message actions are one 44px ⋯** on the author line, always there (Settle and Reply in thread are in its menu); the author line grows to 44px for it. Desktop keeps the hover toolbar. | A hover toolbar doesn't exist on a phone. |
| CTC-06 | **A Chat thread's head says its stage after its replies** (“3 replies · Waiting for you”), from the same flags. | The thread says where it stands without scrolling. |
| CTC-07 | **The phone's Chat head spaces Feedback, Settled and More 8pt apart**, outlined in `line2`, ≥44pt iOS / 48dp Android; the head grows instead of shrinking them. A message still sending is drawn in `mut`, not faded. | The mockups' spacing; nothing readable is faded. |
| CTC-08 | **Cards in a thread have one layout** (`CardFrame` on the web, `ui/cardFrame.tsx` on the phone; words from `cardFrame.ts`): a status line (a mark by shape, the status word, “· Council” / “· Tasks” / “· Run” after it, the card's own time at the end), a plain title, a headline in words when there is one, one sentence, the main button with at most two others in a fixed place under it, what opens in place, and one **Details** with every technical word. Plan, council, checklist (delegation) and run cards; the compact live run card and settled lines keep AP-730…AP-735 with the status line's mark. | The same card reads the same way whatever made it. |
| CTC-09 | **Marks by shape**: a filled dot waiting for the person, an open ring at work, the branch arrow handed over, a tick done, a square stopped, a ring with a bar where it didn't finish (warn, never red), a hollow circle not started. The word beside it always says the same. | Colour never says it alone; red stays for Not working. |
| CTC-10 | **A plan names its current or blocked step**: “{assistant} is working” with “Now: build the export endpoint.”, “Waiting for you” with the gate step's own note, “Blocked” with the blocked step's note; a callout “Current step” / “Blocked step” names it over the steps. Go ahead is the main button when a step waits only for the person's word, else Open plan; Open plan canvas beside it. The steps open in place with their state, a blocked step's reason and its work's links; the steps' count, due date and run or approval numbers are under Details. A plan in a chat is this card too (replaces AP-1260's compact list there). | The mockup's plan card; the person sees what is happening and what waits for them first. |
| CTC-11 | **A council's result is written in words** (`councilResultWords`): “All three agree.”, “Two of three agree.”, or how many reached its aim, from its last round. The final card's main button is **Read the answers**, which opens Details: each member's answer in full with its verdict in words, the plan, every round's scores and the pictures of what changed. A live council shows Working (Waiting for you at a go-ahead), where it is in one sentence and Stop; its members open in place, its earlier rounds, the plan and “Change who sits on it” are under Details. | Scores in a table aren't a result a person reads; the answers stay one press away. |
| CTC-12 | **A checklist keeps its ticks above Hand over N** (its arrow still chooses every task's AI), with Change something and Not now; **each task's AI chip is under Details**. Once handed over it says how far it is in words (“2 finished · 6 still in progress”), one short row per task (title and where it is), the finished ones folded into one line (DLG-28), Open tasks and Stop; Open tasks opens Details with each task's record: what it does now, its AI, how long, the agent's line, its pull request, its pictures and its ⋯ (Open in T3 Code, Stop this task). | The mockup's checklist; pull requests and model names are technical words. |
| CTC-13 | **A run card**: Working, Paused, Done, Didn’t finish or Taken over, its title, what it does now or how it ended as a sentence, its bar, why it needs a person; Watch run, Branch room and Try again where the run page offers it. Its number, branch, tests and changes are under Details. | One layout; the mono “RUN · LIVE” label was a technical head. |
| CTC-14 | **Markdown is rendered, never raw `**`**: a card's sentence, a council's answers and plan, a task's line go through the rich text renderer on both apps. | Raw markup reads as broken. |
| CTC-15 | **On a phone a long card starts folded** to its status, title, a two-line sentence and its main button; Show more opens the rest in place, Show less folds it. The web folds at ≤640px, the desktop starts open. Details is closed until pressed (or opened by Read the answers / Open tasks). Buttons and toggles are 44px (48dp on Android). | The main button stays in sight; the card opens where it is. |
| CTC-16 | **Quiet and failed rows are readable**: a council member that didn't answer, a stopped or skipped task, a step not started use `mut` / `ink2` (≥4.5:1 in both themes), never opacity. | A faded row can't be read; quiet isn't invisible. |
| CTC-17 | **Your own messages show your face, not your name** (John, 2026-11-07), in Chat, Feedback, a task chat and their threads, on the web and the phone: the same face as anywhere else, then the time; screen readers still say “Your message, 13:52”. Other rooms keep names. | John missed his face beside his replies: without it a thread reads as the assistant talking to itself. |
