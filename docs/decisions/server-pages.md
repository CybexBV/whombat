# Server pages: what a person needs first (SPG)

The “effortless” work of 2026-10-30 for John: a server's, a machine's or a service's page opens with what a person
needs, not what the machine knows. The review (`~/.cache/wb-tmp/ux-review/`, visual #4, #5, #9, #13, #22, #29, #30, #34,
#35; flows 2.6; copy §2 and §5) found server pages that read like a sysadmin console: forty facts in the head, raw log
lines on the System tab, CI machines named by hex ids at the top of every list, buttons cut off, a different set of
head buttons on every page and a dead end for a missing machine.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SPG-01 | **A verdict under the name** of every server and machine page, web and phone: one or two short sentences built from the same lines Worth a look shows (the banner's and the card's, their kind and whether they are quiet; `serverVerdict`, `machineVerdict` in `@whombat/client/hosts`). Offline: “pve-01 isn’t checking in. Whombat last heard from it 2 h ago.” Waiting: “… hasn’t checked in yet.” A problem: “blackbox has a problem: The disk array has failed. One more thing needs a look.” (the worst problem by title). A status the hosts service words (“running warm”): “pve-02 is running warm. 2 things need you.”; another note follows a colon. Otherwise “thetitan is fine: 28 apps and 4 machines are running.”, then “One thing needs you: {title}.” or “N things need you.”. Heads-ups and quiet lines never count. A machine: its state in words, then its own hints and its server's warnings about it. | The first thing a person asks is “is it all right, and do you need me?”; reading the card's own lines means the sentence and Worth a look never disagree. |
| SPG-02 | **At most four tiles in words**: Processor (Quiet under 40 %, Busy under 80, Very busy under 95, else Flat out), Memory (Plenty free under 70 %, Getting full under 85, Nearly full under 95, else Full), Space (Plenty free under 70 %, Filling up under 80, Nearly full under 95, else Full; under it the soonest forecast or “61 % free”; on Unraid the array, with Stopped, At risk or Not protected first), Temperature (Normal, Warm, Hot by the sensor's own limits). One plain line with at most one figure under each. The processor opens the CPU, network and disk traffic charts; the guests figure is gone (the verdict says what runs). | Words a non-developer reads at a glance; the raw figures are one click away in the charts each tile opens. |
| SPG-03 | **One “Technical details” fold** per page, closed: on a server's Summary its system and platform versions, kernel, processor, memory, uptime, address, Whombat's helper (when it checked in, its version), what Whombat may do, that the figures are live every 10 s and the connections that are part of it, then its storage and pools, the platform's own card (Incus, Unraid, QEMU/KVM) and the cluster. The head keeps the name, one badge, the verdict and its buttons. A machine's page folds its kind and number, cores, memory, uptime and address the same way; the System tab folds its internals (SPG-09). | The page opens with what a person needs; what only someone who runs the server reads stays one click away, in one place. |
| SPG-04 | **Temporary machines are hidden**: an Incus instance in any project but `default` (its id is `project/name`; CI and sandbox projects keep throwaway machines apart), a name starting `ci-job-`, an agent-resource lease's generated name (`agent-<32 hex>-…`, or a Docker Compose project of that shape) and a Docker container labelled `whombat.service=1` — never the machine this Whombat runs in (`isTemporaryGuest`). They are left out of the busiest list and every count (the verdict, the tab's count, a list's “32 of 49 running”) and out of the lists until a quiet “5 temporary machines hidden · Show” row (web Machines tab and All guests, phone list) brings them back. Worth a look's lines are the hosts service's and stay as it sends them. | Build machines named by hex ids crowded out the person's own things and took the top of the busiest list; the rule uses what the hosts data already carries, so it works without a hosts release, and Show keeps them reachable. |
| SPG-05 | **A server's words**: its tab is **Machines** (Proxmox, Incus, QEMU/KVM, plain Linux) or **Apps** (a Docker host, Unraid), by `guestsWord`; “Busiest machines” / “Busiest apps” (QEMU/KVM's few: “Machines”), the column “Name”, rows without type codes or numbers (“CT 131”) — only App or Machine where both run, This Whombat, a restart waiting —, “See all”; a line's way to a tab says **See …** (“See details” for System, “See temperatures”, “See disks”, “See storage”), never “Open System”. | The shared word list: machine and app, never guest; buttons go somewhere with See. |
| SPG-06 | **A problem's button asks the assistant by name**: “Ask Neo” on a server's Summary and banner (the builder's `assistant` input, from each app's agent words; “Ask about it” without a name). | One verb for asking everywhere; “Look into it” suggested nothing happens unless the person asks. |
| SPG-07 | **Every page head has one shape**: Ask {assistant} (primary) · the service's own page (“Open Home Assistant”) where there is one · ⋯ with the rest (a server's “Open a shell”, Restart and Shut down; a service's Restart, Pause, Update lists), what may not be done greyed with why (`HeadMenu`). | A shell beside Ask suggested it is for everyone; five buttons on Pi-hole's head buried the one most people want. |
| SPG-08 | **The busiest table has no button column**: its name opens the machine, whose page has the console. | “Console” was cut to “Conso” at 1440 px beside a long name. |
| SPG-09 | **The System tab says updates once**: the Health card's Updates and Restart lines (with Install and Restart) carry them, so the hosts service's “updates available” and “restart needed” lines aren't repeated above them (no more “4 updates available” beside “Installing updates…”). Its Health card is Updates, Restart and Background services (names without `.service`); the clock and load, the platform's operations and warnings (Incus's raw “Skipping AppArmor…”), Look inside, the network, what machines were given and the cluster are its Technical details. The helper's card is “Whombat’s helper”, “Checking in, last 4 s ago”, its version only in the Summary's details. | The System tab is where the update lines send people: it must answer, not contradict itself or show logs. |
| SPG-10 | **A machine that can't be read says so plainly**: “pve-02 doesn’t have “frigate” any more, or Whombat couldn’t reach it just now.” with **Back to pve-02’s machines**, Ask {assistant} and Try again. | A red line alone was a dead end. |
| SPG-11 | **Home Assistant's Controls lead its Summary**, above the figures (`place: top`). | The lights and the thermostat are what a person opens the page for. Supersedes SD-30's “at the top of the main column”. |
| SPG-12 | **The figures-open-their-charts rule is `openThingFigures` in `@whombat/core`**: the server lays out every page with it and the web's fixture pages too (their figure keys and chart ids are the server's), so the visual reference shows a page as a real Whombat draws it: Mail and Home Assistant open on their figures and Worth a look, the charts behind the figures. | The review's “simple services open on charts” came from fixtures that skipped the server's layout; one rule in one place keeps them equal. |

## Rows for DECISIONS.md

- SPG-01…SPG-03 — **A server's or machine's page opens with a verdict**, one plain sentence from Worth a look's own lines (“thetitan is fine: 28 apps and 4 machines are running.”), at most four tiles in words, and everything only an operator reads in one closed “Technical details” fold. (`server-pages.md`)
- SPG-04 — **Temporary machines are hidden** from server pages' lists and counts (Incus projects other than `default`, `ci-job-*`, agent-resource leases, Whombat's own `whombat.service=1` containers), with a “Show” row. (`server-pages.md`)
- SPG-05, SPG-06 — **Server pages use the word list**: Machines or Apps, See …, and “Ask {assistant}” on a problem. (`server-pages.md`)
- SPG-07 — **Every page head is Ask {assistant} · Open {service} · ⋯** with the rest. (`server-pages.md`)
- SPG-09 — **The System tab says updates once** and folds its internals. (`server-pages.md`)
