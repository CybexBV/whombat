# Service pages in slots

John, 2026-11-04: the Plex page didn't use the space it had (a tall head, four number tiles repeating the Libraries
table, Now playing mostly empty, a Worth a look column that ended early). He asked for a redesign for phones,
foldables and desktops that other services follow, approved the mockups and said “Implement this fully”.
Mockups: `~/.cache/wb-tmp/mockups/plex-redesign/plex-dashboard-redesign.html`.

| id | decision |
|---|---|
| SLT-01 | **A service's Summary is built from slots**, the same on every screen: Worth a look, Live, Inventory, Shelf, Trend, People, Machine. A service fills the slots it has; the rows close up around the rest. Every kind with a page is in slots, and so is the page of a connection without a kind (SLT-15…SLT-129); Plex was the first. |
| SLT-02 | **Live** is a `gallery` with `look: 'rows'`: one line per item with a small picture, its words, who it is about (`person`, drawn with their initial), a tag (`badge`) and a progress bar that takes the spare width (`note` at its start, `noteEnd` at its end, grey when `paused`). On a phone the rows are cards you swipe. |
| SLT-03 | **Inventory** is a `list` with `look: 'counts'`: each line's `figure` large on the right with what changed under it (“+9 this week”), its button (Scan) beside it. It replaces a service's row of number tiles and its table of the same things. A phone draws the lines as tiles two by two. Builders still give `right`, for clients without counts. New icons for a library's kind: film, tv, music, photo, video. |
| SLT-04 | **Shelf** is a `gallery` with `look: 'shelf'`: one row of pictures that fills the width with as many as fit, never a half-empty second row; a phone swipes it. |
| SLT-05 | **People** is a `bars` panel whose lines have `person`: who uses the service most, by name with their initial. |
| SLT-06 | **Trend** is a `chart` with `labels`: the words under each bar (“Thu” … “Today”) instead of times; such a chart keeps out of the page's range picker. |
| SLT-07 | **Machine** is a `bars` panel “The machine” (meta: the machine's name, linked to its page): Processor, Memory and Disk with how full each is, and the kind's own lines (Plex's version). A kind asks for it with `KindView.machineCard`; it then replaces the machine's CPU and Memory figures. Without a known machine there is no card. |
| SLT-08 | **Superseded in part by ui-refresh-chat.md UIR-70 (a plain panel, now first then SOON).** **Worth a look is a strip** on top on every connection page: `place: 'top'`, left out when it has no line (quiet lines count, so Undo stays reachable). The clients draw a top notices panel as a slim strip, one line per notice, the first three, “Show N more” after. |
| SLT-09 | **Slots sit in rows of three columns** by `span` (`place: 'chart'`, which any panel may take): Plex is Live 2 + Inventory 1, Shelf 3, Trend 1 + People 1 + Machine 1. The server packs every chart-placed panel, not only charts, so a short row widens its last card. Phones stack them in order; an open foldable or tablet (700 px and wider) puts the panels spanning two or three columns on the left and the rest (the strip first) on the right, each column in one half of the screen. |
| SLT-10 | **The head is two lines on the web**: icon, name, badge and the verdict on one line, the facts under it, buttons on the right; the phone shows the verdict and two full-width buttons (Ask the assistant, Open the service) under the name. |
| SLT-11 | **Plex reads its watch history** (`/status/sessions/history/all` with `viewedAt>`, `/accounts`, `/devices`), kept 10 minutes: plays per day for the last 7 days (Trend: plays this week and the change on the week before) and plays per person with the device they use most (People). What was added this week per library is a count with `addedAt>>=` (episodes for shows), kept with the counts. Each stream says its quality and bandwidth; Now playing's meta adds the bandwidth out. Unread history leaves Trend and People out. |
| SLT-12 | **What isn't built**: hours watched (Plex's history has no durations), library sizes in TB, and upload capacity; the mockups showed them as examples. |
| SLT-13 | **Builders make the slots, a check keeps them**: `kinds/slots.ts` has a builder per slot (`liveRows`/`liveList`/`liveControls`, `inventory` + `countLine`, `shelf`, `trend`, `people`) and `slotPanels`, which orders them and the kind's own panels (`more`, main column) and returns `slots`. `slotProblems` names what is out of place; every kind in slots tests its view with it and the page logs it. How to add a service: `docs/service-pages.md`. |
| SLT-14 | **A page in slots offers the range picker only when something on it follows the range** (a chart over time or a figure's small line; a Trend keeps its own days): Home Assistant and Plex show none, SABnzbd keeps it for its speed. |

A chart over the page's range goes in `more` (main column). `slotProblems` names a chart card in a view in slots and a chart-placed panel that fills no slot. |
| SLT-16 | **The card without a known machine**: when Whombat knows no machine the service runs on and the kind gives no figures of its own but does give machine lines (its version, “Up to date”), the page still draws the card, titled “<the connection's name> itself”, with only those lines (`itselfCard`). A known machine whose figures couldn't be read falls back the same way. |
| SLT-17 | **A desk keeps a count's colour**: `slotFigures` carries an Inventory or Live line's amber or red, from the line's `tone` or its count's, so a 92 % full pool stays amber on a desk. |
| SLT-18 | **People may mark a person**: `people()` takes a `tone` and a `sub` per person (a nearly full quota: amber, “Quota 300 GB”); the line under the bar takes the same colour. |

A Trend from fewer than 2 days with a value is left out (`daysWith`); a figure that is an estimate says “about”. |
| SLT-19a | **Superseded in part by connection-dashboards.md CDB-03 (“With Whombat”, with Last used).** **The page of a connection without a kind is in slots** (`things/generic.ts`): Inventory “How it answers” (id `answers`) — Answers, the share of Whombat's checks answered over the last 7 days (“99.7 %”, never 100 % while one went unanswered) with “All answered”, “2 didn’t answer”, or the last check's own word (“Answers with errors”, “Not answering now”, “Signed out”) so the line stays true while its notice is ignored; Answers in, the typical (median) answer time today with its change on the 7 days before. | SLT-19b | **A kind whose read gave nothing gets the same page**: a view with no figures, panels, charts or slots (what a kind returns when its read failed) is drawn as the page of a connection without a kind, so a failing service keeps the slots look instead of the old row of figures. |
