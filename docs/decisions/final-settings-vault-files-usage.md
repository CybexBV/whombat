# Final Settings, Vault, Files and Usage (FSV)

John accepted the final design (`docs/design-handoff/council-ui-2026-11/16-final/`: `SHELL.md`, `settings/`, `vault/`,
`files/`, `usage/`) and asked for it to be built into the web (desktop and phone width) and the phone app (iPhone and
Android). This file covers those four places; the shell (sidebar, Places, tabs, Back) is another task's. The briefs are
gone (`brief-removed.md`): no brief row, card or setting is built, though the drawings show one.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| FSV-01 | **Superseded in part by settings-phone-link.md SPL-01 (Often used is Notifications · Phone app · Theme) and SPL-22 (Connections is a group on the home, between the assistant and This Whombat).** **Settings opens on a home**: “Settings”, “You, {assistant} and this Whombat.”, then **Often used** — Notifications (where the account keeps them) and Theme — then **All settings**, five groups in this order on every platform: **You · {assistant} · This Whombat · Screens · Advanced**, each with a distinct line icon (person, the assistant's face on the phone or a bot on the web, box, display, sliders) and one line on what it holds. On the web `/settings` alone is the home and each group is a page of its own with “Back to Settings” and the group's name as its title (`?part=profile` is You; old addresses that meant You now name `part=profile`). On the phone the rows keep their live line (what the assistant runs on, This Whombat's news) and a row in trouble keeps its colour. Screens shows where this Whombat has a screen or screens of widgets, as before. | The accepted design; the person finds the two settings they change most without knowing the groups. |
| FSV-02 | **Superseded by settings-phone-link.md SPL-22 (Connections is a group on the home again; Advanced no longer lists it).** **Connections leaves Settings' groups** and stays one tap away: Advanced's CONNECTIONS fold has a **Connections** row (`accounts`) that opens the Connections page (`?tab=connections`, the phone's Connections screen), and the Overview keeps its Connections. The page and its address are unchanged. | Five plain groups, and nothing the app has today disappears. |
| FSV-03 | **Theme on the web** opens its Light / Dark choice in place under its row (the same preference as the account menu's). On the phone it stays the one-tap System · Light · Dark switch in its own row. | The phone's switch already is one tap; a sheet would add one. Superseded in part by `docs/app/decisions/account-menu.md` ACM-04 (the web offers Light, Dark and Auto). |
| FSV-04 | **Settings' read states keep the menu working.** The web home reads your saved preferences (`me.preferences`), the phone home This Whombat's status; while it loads a “Loading settings…” card shows under Often used, and when it can't be read “Couldn’t read your settings” with Try again (in the warning colour). Every row stays usable either way. | A failing read must not hide the way to fix it. |
| FSV-05 | **Usage is the Spend report's one name and page**, at the shell's address (`final-shell-web.md` FSW-05: `?tab=advanced&part=spend`, `USAGE_HREF`; the phone's `Spend` screen, titled Usage, in Places): the page is `UsageScreen` without Settings' groups around it. Settings' older Spend addresses still lead there. | SHELL.md: Usage is the display name for the existing Spend content. |
| FSV-06 | **Usage leads with plain numbers**: Today · Last 7 days · Last 30 days (Today first), then “Estimated cost” in dollars with “Cost reported for 4 of 5”, “Times {assistant} worked”, a sentence on what one time is, the window (“23 Sep 00:00–11:22 · Europe/Amsterdam”) and when it was prepared. A window with no finished work is empty (“No usage reported for this window”), never $0. **More** holds provider coverage (all providers combined; the report doesn't name them), the text read and written with how many times sent no usage report, and what saved plans used. No “tokens” or “turns” anywhere: a finished turn is a time {assistant} worked, tokens are “pieces of text”. The words are shared (`@whombat/client` `usageView`). | The accepted design and John's no-jargon rule; coverage stated honestly. |
| FSV-07 | **A Vault entry's row is one line**: its lock, the name, then how its check went and when it was last used (“Sign-in reached · Last used 09:12”, “Not checked yet · Not used yet”, “Secret · {assistant} is off”). Only a failing entry is loud: “Not working · The password stopped working” in the Not working colour. What it is and where it points move into the opened entry, first. Secrets are never shown, only replaced (unchanged). The words are shared (`vaultRowLine`). | One loud mark for the one thing that's broken; the rest reads calmly. |
| FSV-08 | **Add to Vault** is the page's primary action beside the title, wrapping onto its own full-width line at phone width or large text; on the phone it is a full-width button under the intro. Under the list: “Secrets stay closed. Replace one here when it changes.”, then what saving from chat does and the home network line, as before. | The 200% collision in the earlier drawing; nothing that was on the page is lost. |
| FSV-09 | **Files has one home per platform** (`/files`, the phone's Files screen) listing each conversation with files in one card: its mark (the assistant's head for Chat, a folder for a branch room), its name, and “4 files · 136 MB · Last added 23 Sep 11:20” (`filesSpaceLine`); the whole row opens the conversation's Files. Loading, “No files yet” with Go to Chat, and “Couldn’t read your files” with Try again each have a card of their own. | The accepted design; a row reads on its own. Superseded on the web by final-shell-web.md FSW-15 (the home is the Chat's files). |
| FSV-10 | **The four places share one look**: a 960px column centred in the workspace (wider for Settings → Screens), a 36px title (32 on a phone), cards of rows with an icon tile, a 15/600 name over a 13 line and a chevron, rows at least 64 tall on the phone (44pt / 48dp targets hold). A read's failure uses the warning colour; red is only for Not working. Web: `features/settings/Place.tsx`; phone: `ui/place.tsx`. | One pattern across the four screens and platforms. |
