# UI refresh: Chat, threads, composer and service pages (UIR)

From the 2026-11-04 design council (the final spec's §4 “Chat and thread” and §7 “Service page header”; its mockup was
built from the real tokens) and John's “implement this fully”. This file is the web's Chat room, threads, the one
composer and the head of a service's page; the sidebar, Overview, tokens, the Inbox and the approval card are the other
streams' (UIR-01…59, UIR-90…).

Code: `apps/web/src/features/messaging/` (`Composer`, `MessageRow`, `MessageList`, `ConversationRoom`, `ThreadView`,
`ThreadPane`, `replyFocus.ts`), `features/chats/` (`AskBox`, `ChatRoomScreen`, `runtime/RanOn.tsx`),
`app/shell/threadOrigin.ts` (with `BurrowShell`, `hosts.tsx`, `hooks.ts`), `features/things/dashboard/` (`PageHead`,
`NoticeStrip`, `slots.ts`) and `features/things/page/` (`ThingPageScreen`, `headFacts.ts`); the shared words
`CHAT_WORDS.waiting`, `answer`, `replyTo` in `@whombat/client`. No contract, server or migration changes: everything is
drawn from what the server already sends.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| UIR-60 | **One composer** for the Chat room, every thread's reply box and Automations (`Composer` variants `chat` and `reply`, `place="page"` for Automations): one row of the field, a labelled **File** button with its paperclip (accessible name “Attach a file”), the model's mark, the mic, a round send. Buttons 36px, 44px at ≤640px or a coarse pointer. Send is a transparent circle with a 1px `--line2` ring and a `--mut` arrow until there is text or a ready file, then the filled `--btn` (`data-ready`); `aria-disabled` only while an upload runs or the text is too long, as before. Placeholder in `--mut`; the field's border goes `--brand` on focus. Other rooms (a branch room's own composer) keep the card with `/ command` and `+ file`. | The spec's one composer; three different boxes for the same act read as three products. |
| UIR-61 | **The model's mark sits after File** (field · File · mark · mic · send), not before the paperclip as chat-runtime.md CR-12 placed it; the mic, which the spec doesn't place, stays in every composer (dictation.md) between the mark and send. | The spec's order; File is the labelled, more used control and the mark is a setting. |
| UIR-62 | **Only the words differ by place**: “Ask {assistant} anything” (Chat), “Reply to {assistant}…” (a Chat or Feedback thread and a thing page's Ask pane; `CHAT_WORDS.replyTo`), “Tell {assistant} what should happen on its own…” (Automations, with its suggestion chips under the box). Threads of other rooms keep “Reply in thread”. The phone app keeps “Reply…” (out of this stream). | The spec's words; the box says who answers. |
| UIR-63 | **Automations' Ask box is the one composer.** Words alone start the thread with `chats.start` as before; with files the question goes as Chat's own first message (`messages.send` in the Chat room, which carries files) and that thread opens. A box with a subject (`chats.start` carries no files) has File greyed with its reason. | Keeps every behaviour of the old box and gives Automations the File the spec asks for without a contract change. |
| UIR-64 | *Superseded by chat-inbox.md CIB-01 and CIB-04: the room is a list of rows; “Waiting for you” is on the row, without Answer.* **Superseded in part by chat-threads-cards.md CTC-03: the dot is `--chart1`, the words `--warn-text` 600.** **“Waiting for you” in Chat**: under a Chat or Feedback thread's first message while `ChatThread.waiting` is true (a pending approval, a key or Vault request, a question card, an unanswered question of the assistant's, an open guide card; chat-settle.md CHS-S03), one `--sf2` chip with an 8px `--warn` dot and “Answer” beside it; gone once nothing waits. One per thread, however many cards wait in it. It fits status-line.md STL-10 (no status sentence, “See them” or Done today in Chat's head): nothing is added to the head, the Inbox row stays the loud place, and the chip is a quiet mark beside the question itself. | The spec's “only while it is in Needs you” is the same binding (`waiting` is what Needs you reads for threads); a question asked last night shouldn't scroll away unseen. |
| UIR-65 | *Superseded by chat-inbox.md CIB-01: the room has no Answer; the thread holds the card.* **Answer goes to the card that waits**: a card drawn in the room (on the first message) is scrolled to and its first button focused; otherwise the thread opens (`requestCardFocus`) at the newest card with a button to press, focused, or with the reply box focused when the question is the assistant's words rather than a card. | “Answer” should land on the answer, not on the top of a thread. |
| UIR-66 | *Superseded by chat-inbox.md CIB-01: one two-line row per thread.* **The reply row is one full-width row** (a link: it navigates): the faces overlapping with a 2px `--bg` ring, “3 replies” `--brand` 600, “Last reply 10 hours ago” `--mut`, a chevron always at the right end; `--sel` with a `--line` border on hover, radius 10, 40px tall, focus ring as everywhere. At ≤640px it keeps to one line with the time alone (“10 hours ago”, “3 Oct”). The hover swap to “View thread” is gone. | The spec's reply row; a target that shows itself before hover. |
| UIR-67 | **A room thread has one way out**: its head is a back link that names where the thread was opened from — Chat, Feedback, Inbox, Overview, Automations, Vault, Files, Settings, What {assistant} did, Search or a thing's name — else its room; the subject chip; the question as the pane's heading, shown once the first message has scrolled out of view (until then invisible but read and focused); “3 replies” in mono (not at ≤640px, as the mock); Settle. No close button. Escape goes to the same place. The origin is kept in the shell while the thread (or another thread opened from it) stays open, in memory only: a reload or a shared link goes back to the room. Threads beside a room or in a branch room keep “Thread”, expand and ✕: their back link only returns to the split view. | Two exits (back and ✕) went to the same place; one that says where it goes is calmer, and a thread opened from the Inbox should go back there. |
| UIR-68 | **The model's mark only where it changed**: the provider's mark after the time shows on a reply that ran on something else than the thread's reply before it (`ranOnSwitches`), still only where more than one AI can answer (plain-words.md PWD-13); a first message never has it. The whole line (“Claude · Opus · High effort · Fast”) is the hover title of every assistant reply's time. The “Switched to …” divider stays. Supersedes chat-runtime.md CR-07 in part (a mark on each reply). The approval card's Technical details don't carry the model today and the card is the approval stream's; nothing was added there. | The spec drops the model from messages; the change is the one moment it tells the person something. |
| UIR-69 | **A service page's head on one line** (every connection's and device's page, `ThingPageScreen`; `PageHead` `statusFirst`, `detail`): the status pill leads the line under the name, then “Runs on {host}” with Change (or “What does it run on?”), the address as a button that copies it (“Address copied.”), and when it was last checked. Every other fact — version, Docker, database, uptime, what Whombat may do, what else it is connected through — is under **More detail** at the end of that line (a button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, the facts on the line below when open). The “Checked …” fact is found by its words (`headFacts`); the server words it. A server's page keeps its own head (server-pages.md). | The spec's §7: what the person needs at a glance first, the technical facts one press away. |
| UIR-70 | **Superseded in part by overview-server-connection-final.md OSC-10: every heads-up folds behind “Show N heads-ups”, uncounted; no SOON label.** **Worth a look is a plain panel**: `--sf` with a `--line` border and no tint of its tone, its label in mono capitals on an `--sf2` band (not a heading: the region is named by it), rows between hairlines. What is wrong now first (problems and only-you lines), the worst first (red, then amber); then heads-ups (forecasts, updates, advice; `fyi`) after a mono “SOON” label, still in sight; quiet lines last. Folded it shows three of now and two of soon, quiet lines in the room they leave, the rest behind “Show N more”. Supersedes service-slots.md SLT-08 in part (its order and its tinted strip). | The spec's §7; a forecast two months off isn't a problem today, but it shouldn't vanish into a fold either. |
| UIR-71 | **A line's way forward says only “Ask {assistant}” or “Show”**, whatever its own label (“Look into it”, “Repair”, “Show them”): ask lines “Ask {assistant}”, a tab, a page or an outside page “Show”. A line's own button that does something (Retry, Clear the warnings; `ThingButton`) stays, and so does the panel's “Ask {assistant} to handle these”. | The spec's two verbs; removing a button that acts would remove a function, which the refresh keeps. |
| UIR-72 | **Visual suite**: the harness no longer hides `data-composer-file` (the old thread `+ file` chip is gone; the one composer's File is part of the design and shows in every baseline). Baselines that change by intent are updated: the voice spec's Chat composer (with File) and the looks spec's Home Assistant Summary. No mock comparison changed, so nothing is masked and no budget raised. | Only documented deviations may be hidden. |
