# On purpose: stop watching what is set up so, and an Ignore that outlasts a blip (OPS)

John, 2026-11-02 (main checkout): “Plex can’t be reached from outside the house” was back in Needs you a day after he
ignored it. Plex is kept off the internet on purpose. On Home the Ignore (1 Nov 10:32, “until it’s reachable again”)
had ended by itself: Plex stopped reporting its port mapping as failed for at least two 5-minute passes, Whombat took
that as fixed, and the next failure was a new problem (`notice_states.first_seen` 2 Nov 09:09). Dusty had looked into it
twice and even restarted Plex to retry remote access, without asking whether it was meant to be closed. John: “we
should be offered this by Whombat / Dusty and he should be able to toggle it for me … I should have the option to
disable that check or stop warning me about this immediately.” Then: “Yes fully”.

Changes earlier decisions: QN-11 (an Ignore ends after about an hour of passes without its line, not two), QN-13 and
QN-S10 (the assistant may switch a check on the person's word), QN-21 and NYL-07 (Needs you rows offer *Stop watching
this*), LR-07 (a look that may be on purpose offers *Stop watching …* as its step).

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| OPS-01 | **Ignore outlasts a blip.** Any Ignore ends once 12 passes in a row (about an hour at one pass every 5 minutes) read its check without the line (`FIXED_PASSES`), not two. Getting worse, a new episode and a period's reset still end it at once. | A service retesting something (Plex its port mapping, a device rebooting) clears its line for a few minutes; two passes turned that into “fixed”, and the line came back as new. |
| OPS-02 | **The assistant asks before it fixes what may be on purpose.** A look turn (not Look only) is told: when it may be how the person set it up (remote access off, a port kept closed, a machine or service stopped, something unused), don't try to change it; ask in the summary whether it is on purpose and offer as `fix` “Stop watching …” (“Stop watching remote access”), whose step switches the check off with `watch_checks` and reports it fixed. The step names the check and the thing (a connection by its handle). | Dusty restarted Plex to “retry remote access” the person had closed on purpose; the honest step is the question and one press to stop hearing about it. |
| OPS-03 | **`watch_checks`, the assistant's tool for What Whombat watches.** A personal tool (`ONE_MCP_TOOLS`), not in outsiders' turns or an automation's own step: `thing` (a page id, or a connection's handle) lists its checks (on/off, the items left out, ignored or snoozed); with `check` and `watched` (and `subject` + `subjectName` for one item) it switches that check off or on through the same path as the page's switch (`setThingWatch`, a server's through the hosts service). | One way to change what Whombat watches, whoever asks for it; the agent needs the list to name the right check when the person says “stop telling me about X”. |
| OPS-04 | **Only on the person's word.** A switch is refused unless the person's own message started the turn (`onBehalfOf.principalId` is the person: their words in a chat, or the go-ahead their press of the step posts). A turn the assistant started itself (a look, a schedule, an automation) gets “ask them first, or offer it as your step”. | Switching a check off hides a real outage too; the person decides, the assistant carries it out. |
| OPS-05 | **Recorded as the assistant's change for the person, with Undo.** The switch commits as the person (they decided; servers' rules need an owner's actor), with the assistant on the change (`agentId`) and kind `things.watch` instead of an everyday switch, so it is in what the assistant did (Done recently, “See everything {assistant} did”) and the thing's Activity, with an Undo that keeps until used (a direct undoer that switches the check back as the person who pressed it). | “He should be able to toggle it for me”, and the person sees afterwards what was switched and takes it back in one press. |
| OPS-06 | *Superseded in part by inbox.md IBX-05: it is in the row's More, and Settle took Ignore's place.* **Stop watching this in Needs you.** A line's item carries the check behind it (`NeedsYouItem.watch`, `WatchItem.watch`: the thing, the check, its item when the check is per item, the toast's name): a connection's worst line (not while it needs signing in again), a server's alert or hint (not the hub's own checks), a device that doesn't answer. The row's ⋯ (the phone's sheet) offers *Stop watching this* (or *Stop watching Garage AP*) after Ignore, also on an ignored row; it calls `things.setWatch`, the row leaves at once, and the toast “Whombat stopped watching …” has Undo. | “Stop warning me about this immediately”, where the warning is, without a trip to the thing's Settings. |
| OPS-07 | **No contracts release.** The new fields and tool are additive (optional fields, a new agent tool); nothing outside this repository reads them. | Additions are minors and only matter to other repositories when they use them. |
