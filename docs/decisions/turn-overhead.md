John, 2026-10-24 (main checkout), after the research `~/.cache/wb-tmp/research/2026-10-24-turn-overhead/report.md`: a
short Claude turn on Home sent ~52 k tokens per model call in two calls (tool definitions ~78 %, Hermes's prompt ~17 %,
the turn ~6 %). His call: keep Whombat's tools rather than drop them for Hermes's, don't turn on Claude Code's tool
search, and “Implement your suggested approach”: one call per short answer, then trim what is served.

Token figures are Claude tokens estimated as in the report (o200k counts × the ratios measured on Home's exact call:
1.40 for prose, 1.75 for tool definitions), for a Chat turn on a Whombat like Home (one agent in the assistant shell,
no repository, no GitHub, the computer with its browser, the connection tools, the Vault's two).
