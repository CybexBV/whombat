# Overview, arranged (OVA)

**Superseded by `servers-connections-places.md` SCP-18** (2026-11-28): there is no Overview; OVA-01…OVA-12 are history.

John found the Overview plain (2026-11-24) and accepted the mockup `docs/design-handoff/overview-arranged/overview.html`
(switch State, Theme and Width at its top; press Arrange, then a gear; the mock wins on look, copy and layout). He asked
that a person choose what each connection's tile shows (Home Assistant's isn't fixed to power), remove tiles, make some
bigger and move them; a gear on each tile does it, the gears only while arranging, which a gear at the top starts. One
Overview per Whombat. Web (desktop and phone width) and the phone app (iOS, Android).

Code: `packages/core` (`domain/overview-layout.ts` the layout, `contracts/home.ts` `overviewEndpoints`),
`apps/server/src/home/arrange.ts`, `packages/client` (`home/arrange.ts` the arranged order, the edits, the figures and
`ARRANGE_WORDS`; `react/overview.ts` the hooks), `apps/web/src/features/home/`, `apps/mobile/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx`
and `home/`.

| Id | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OVA-01 | **One layout for the whole Whombat** (`OverviewLayout`): the tiles in their order with what each shows, its size and whether it was removed; the four figures at the top; the pinned charts' widths and which were removed. Kept on the burrow row (`burrows.overview_layout`, null until first arranged) and sent with `home.overview` (`arranged`), so every device draws the same page and takes a change with `home.changed {things}`. An older Whombat sends no `arranged`: the page offers no Arrange there and draws its tiles as before. | One person per Whombat (one-person.md OP-01): the person's computer and phone show one Overview. Riding on `home.overview` keeps it live without another read or event. |
| OVA-02 | **Arrange is the person's.** `overview.arrange` writes the whole layout through the commit pipeline (a receipt, nothing signed: it changes only how a page looks). The assistant may arrange it through its settings (`change`, the Overview's group); screens never. A layout naming a thing twice is refused. | The whole layout in one write can't be half applied, and two devices' edits end as the last one saved. |
| OVA-03 | **Arranging is a mode.** A button with a gear, **Arrange**, sits at the end of the page's heading (after the jump links; on a narrow page beside the title, the gear alone). Pressed, it reads **Done** with a check, a dashed bar under the heading says “Arranging the Overview.” and what to do, and only then does every tile, figure and pinned chart get its gear, tiles can be dragged, and the last tile is **Add to Overview**. Done or Esc ends it (Esc first closes an open panel). Outside arranging a tile is one link to its page and nothing else shows. | Gears on every tile all the time are noise on a page read many times a day and arranged rarely. |
| OVA-04 | **A gear opens a panel** (from the right; a sheet from the bottom on a narrow page): the thing's logo and name, a live preview of the tile, then **What it shows** (its main figures, or one panel of its page, each with what it says now), **Time span** (1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days) when what it shows has a chart, **Size** (Small, Wide, Large), **Place** (Move earlier, Move later) and **Remove from Overview**. Every choice applies at once and is saved; there is no Save. A figure's gear offers Whombat's own four and every thing's figures, grouped by thing, the ones already at the top greyed. A pinned chart's offers **Width** (Half, Full width), Place and Remove. | What a tile can show is exactly what its desk-screen widget can (`overview.figures` is *Add a widget* for things and accounts), so a connection's page, the box's screen and the Overview agree. |
| OVA-05 | **What a tile shows is read as the screens read it** (`overview.views`, the desk's `WidgetResolver` and the things' and accounts' widgets), every minute while the page shows, each widget at its own pace behind that. Until its view arrives, or where it has none, a tile says its line from Home as before (OVP-09). A view that can't be read says why in the tile's line, amber. | One reader for screens and Overview: the same values, the same caching, no second set of reads per service. |
| OVA-06 | **A tile**: logo, name, mark (the status level's), then the figure large with its unit and what it is, one line, a chart where the view has points (a line with its wash and a dot at the newest reading), and the foot (what it is, when it was used). **Small** is one cell, **Wide** two across, **Large** two across and two down with a larger figure and chart. A tile that isn't fine keeps its amber or red edge wherever it sits. Supersedes OVP-09's look and its worst-first order: the person's order holds; a broken thing still leads the page (OVA-08). | The figure is what the person came for; the size says how much it matters to them. |
| OVA-07 | **The page**: the summary card (Live, the greeting with the date and how long ago it was read; the broken thing with Look into it and how many other things need the person, else “Everything’s fine” and what was checked; the four figures under it), **Today**, **Connections**, **Servers** (OVP-02…OVP-08, unchanged), **Pinned**. Supersedes OVP-01's order. | What changed today and what the person chose to watch come before the server table they open less. |
| OVA-08 | **Removing a tile never hides a problem**: a thing that breaks still leads the summary card and is in Needs you, removed or not. | Arranging is about looks; it must never be a way to stop hearing about something. |
| OVA-09 | **The starting layout**: every connection, device and account a small tile with its main figures, in Overview's order (worst first); the four figures Servers running, Connections working, Needs you, Done today. A thing connected later is a small tile at the end; an entry for a thing that is gone is dropped at the next save. | Nobody has to arrange anything to get a useful page. |
| OVA-10 | **Today** is the day as a line from 00:00 to 24:00 with **Now** on it: what the assistant finished and the automations that ran or failed so far, the automations still planned after Now (hollow), and the same as chips under it, each with its time and where it came from. “N things so far · M planned”; *All activity* opens Inbox. Nothing today: the card isn't drawn. | The day at a glance, from what Home already has (`doneToday`, `automationsToday`). |
| OVA-11 | **The phone app**: the same order; Arrange in the header row beside the title; while arranging a gear on each tile, figure and pinned chart opens a sheet with the same choices, and *Move earlier*, *Move later* stand for dragging. Small tiles are two across, Wide and Large the full width. | Dragging in a scrolling list fights the scroll; two buttons always work. |
| OVA-12 | **Copy** is `ARRANGE_WORDS` in `@whombat/client` (with `OVERVIEW_WORDS`); the arranged order, the edits and what a figure says are `home/arrange.ts`, pure and unit-tested, for the web and the phone alike. | One place for the words and the rules. |
