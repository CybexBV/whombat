# One Needs you list (NYL)

The “effortless” work of 2026-10-30 for John (stream needs-you, branch `ef-needs-you`): a problem has one home. Before
it one server alert could show in eight places with different buttons and counts, and Watch and Needs you were split
by severity instead of by who has to act (the review's flows.md Journey 2, 2.1, 2.5, 2.7, 2.9, 6.3, 7.2, 7.3; visual.md
#1, #3, #8, #15, #24). Contracts: `NeedsYouItem` gains `group`, `href`, `state`, `snoozedUntil`, `fixable` and the kinds
`hint`, `automation_run`, `device`; `HomeOverview.needsYouList`; `StartChatBody.reuseThread`; core's
`needsYouGroupOf`, `needsYouCount`, `NEEDS_YOU_HREF`, `needsYouItemHref`. No migration.

Replaces one-whombat.md ONE-07 and in part ONE-10, ONE-24; quiet-notices.md QN-22 and in part QN-02; sidebar.md SB-02,
SB-08; connections-page.md in part CN-03; and the apps' Watch, tabs and alert-push rows (app one-whombat-shell.md
AP-1259, AP-1271; one-whombat-mobile.md AP-1360…AP-1362, AP-1376, in part AP-1353; one-layout-android.md AP-2640;
connections-page.md in part AP-2550, AP-2556; sidebar.md AP-2601, AP-2607; look-research-mobile.md AP-5927).
