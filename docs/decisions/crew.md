# The crew: new art and names for the five agents (CRW-01…CRW-11)

Source: John's handoff “Whombat crew” (2026-10-21), kept verbatim in `docs/design-handoff/whombat-crew/` (`PROMPT.md`,
`agents.json`, `svg/`, `png/`, `expressions/`, `reference/crew-reference.html`, `reference/roster.png`). It replaces the
art of `docs/design-handoff/five-agents/` and builds on five-agents.md (FA-01…FA-18) and assistant-name.md
(AN-01…AN-10). The prefix CR is also chat-runtime's (chat-runtime.md CR-01…), so references name the file:
“crew.md CRW-06”.

John's answer (2026-10-21), the one choice the brief left to him: personal assistants named after an old character
**keep their face and take the new name** (option A, CRW-06).
