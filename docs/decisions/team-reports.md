# Reports to the Whombat Team (workstream `team-reports`)

John, 2026-11-25: Whombat Link people tell the team behind Whombat about a problem or an idea from inside Whombat,
seamlessly, the way a product's customers send reports to a report tracker; the reports arrive on the team's own
Whombat (“Home”) and take the same flow as such reports (report-trackers.md): the assistant checks whether it is a real
bug, T3 Code fixes it, another model reviews, Whombat merges on its checks and tells the person when the fix reaches
them. Planned with the Council (three rounds); John's decisions the same day: no encryption (a quiet warning on the form
is enough), a pilot for two invited people, replies signed “Whombat Team (AI assistant)”, 90 days after closing, the
assistant drafts reports.

Code: `packages/core/src/domain/team-reports.ts` (shapes, vocabulary, words), `packages/core/src/contracts/team-reports.ts`
(the apps' REST), `packages/control-contracts/src/device/team-reports.ts` (device ops), `apps/server/src/team-reports/`
(the box), `appliance/manager/internal/broker/teamreports.go` (the manager), the apps' Reports pages. Control's side and
the team's tracker plugin are Cybex's own (whombat-cloud `control/docs/decisions/team-reports.md`, `reports-plugin/`).

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| WTR-01 | **Who**: a person whose Whombat runs Link (`on` or `grace`) and whose Link account is on control's invitation list; control says whether they may send (`team.reports.list` `available`, `reason` `not_invited`, `off`, `link_off`) and refuses a send with its own code (`not_invited`, `link_off`, `too_many_open` at 20 open reports; `team_reports_unavailable` only for the team's switch), each in its own plain words on the box. A refusal the box doesn't know reads as “didn’t take it”, never as Link being off. The entry shows only where sending is possible or the person has reports; nobody else sees it. | A pilot for two people first; calm for everyone else; the person learns why. |
| WTR-02 | **Readable at control.** Control keeps the reports as the record, readable by the team, unlike everything else that crosses it (control CL-02, contracts CT-10). The form says so in one quiet line: “The Whombat Team and its AI assistant read what you send here. Leave out passwords and private details.” | John: encryption isn't needed, a quiet warning is enough. |
| WTR-03 | **The way through**: the apps call the box (`teamReports.*`, session minor 10); the box calls control through the manager (`team.reports.list/get/create/reply/act`), the words as base64url JSON checked with core's shapes on both ends; the manager forwards them unread, whenever control knows the box (WTR-17). The box adds its own version and channel to a new report, the app its own version, device and language; the form names the box's part too (“and this Whombat’s version and update channel” until it knows them). | One way for every Link feature; the cross-language schemas stay ASCII. |
| WTR-04 | **The box keeps a copy** of each of its reports as last read (the whole report and the revision the person read): the list shows it while control can't be reached (`stale`), and a report is unread while control's `revision` is past the one read. A withdrawn report's copy goes at once. An answer older than the copy (a lower revision, or the same one changed earlier: answers that crossed) leaves the copy as it is; a list that says nothing new writes nothing. A malformed answer from control reads as control not taking it, and the list falls back to the copies. | What is new must be known on the box; a list that vanishes offline looks broken; a late answer mustn't undo a newer one. |
| WTR-05 | **Following the answers**: while the box has an open report, it asks control's list every 10 minutes (and when an app opens the list); the first look comes 20 to 60 seconds after start, at a moment of each box's own, so a box that just started knows soon whether the person may send. A team change marks the report unread. A question (`needs_information`) or a fix to try (`available`) is an Inbox item and a push in fixed words, never the team's: “The Whombat Team asked about your report” / “A fix for your report is ready to try”; tapping opens the report. | Inbox only when the person is needed; push previews pass through control. |
| WTR-06 | **The team's words stay out of the person's assistant.** They never reach Chat, the assistant's context, Search, memory or automation triggers; they show only on the report's page, as plain text (links as text, no pictures). | Outsiders' words are never instructions (OP-17, OT-25). |
| WTR-07 | **The assistant drafts.** When the person says something in Whombat doesn't work or wishes it did something, the assistant may call `report_to_whombat_team` with the kind and its draft of the words. The box keeps the draft and asks the person on a card (always Ask, whatever the keys say, like a shared link) that shows exactly what is sent; Send sends it as the person; Edit opens the form with the draft (`teamReports.draft`); a sent or declined draft is done. The assistant can read the person's reports with `whombat_team_reports`: number, kind, the start of the person's own description as the title (`Report #N` when the box has no copy of it; never control's title, which the team may rename), status in words and dates, never the team's words. | Seamless (John's choice), and only the person's press sends; a title is words the team can write. |
| WTR-08 | **Statuses** are the tracker's vocabulary; the person reads `TEAM_REPORT_STATUS_WORDS` and a four-step track (Received → Looking into it → Being fixed → Fixed). **It works** and **Still happening** show once a fix is available to them or the report was closed. | The team's tracker automations read them as they are. |
| WTR-09 | **Withdraw** deletes the report at control at once (control tells the team's tracker) and its copy on the box, in every Link state control answers (WTR-17). | The person's words are theirs to take back. |
| WTR-10 | **Kept** until 90 days after the report closes; when the Link account is erased or the box leaves Link, control deletes its reports, and the box drops every copy once the manager says it is out of Link (`team_reports_drop`). A Whombat restored onto new hardware starts without its earlier reports: before it takes the new device id, the box drops every copy and the assistant's drafts not sent yet. | John, 2026-11-25. |
| WTR-11 | **Not in the pilot**: screenshots, diagnostics and logs (a report is text and versions), email about reports (Link's own notification email covers the Inbox item for those who chose it). | Small first; size limits and privacy each need their own decision. |
| WTR-12 | **The team's side** is Cybex's: control keeps and serves the reports to the team's Whombat through an API of its own, and a private tracker plugin there feeds the report-tracker automations, a Beta and a Production connection by the reporter's channel, replies signed “Whombat Team (AI assistant)” (a connection's own signature, WTR-13). Nothing in this repository names the team's Whombat. | The tracker flow is the one every report tracker's reports take (report-trackers.md). |
| WTR-13 | **A tracker connection may sign its replies itself** (`signature`, the connection's own words, up to 60 characters): Whombat adds it after a blank line instead of “<agent> - AI Assistant”. Without one it signs as before (TA-26). | John: “Whombat Team (AI assistant)”; a reporter's own assistant may be called Dusty too. |
| WTR-14 | **The card's Edit.** While the assistant's card waits, Chat's card and the approval's dialog on the web and the phone's approval screen put Edit after the answers; it opens the form with the draft (`/reports/new?draft=<id>`, the phone's `TeamReportNew`). The card goes now or not at all: Yes, do it · No, never Yes, tonight (the Inbox's item too; the server refuses a yes for later). The card's approval carries the draft's words, so wherever it can be answered they show exactly: Chat's card, the approval's details on the web and the phone (`REPORT`), and the Inbox's item, whose body is the words and which offers Edit on the web; a draft too long for the Inbox's item (600 characters) takes its yes only on its card, which holds all of it. Sent from the form, the card is withdrawn: declined by the person with a signed `approval.declined` (`withdrawn`), nobody woken. The card's yes and the form's send of a draft go with one request id made from the draft's id, so control answers the same report whichever path or device sends first; the form's send of a draft already sent is refused (409, “This report was sent already.”) and nothing goes. | Changing the words first must be one press away; a report waiting for the night helps nobody; a card left asking after the report went would send it twice or fail. |
| WTR-15 | **One request id per send.** The web and phone forms, the reply box and It works / Still happening make a request id when they open (a button its own) and send it with every try of that send, a new one once it went (`requestId` in the REST bodies); the box passes it to control, which does a request it did already only once. Without one (an older app) the box makes one per call, and the call is not safe to repeat. `teamReports.act` is destructive and never resent by a client by itself, like `create` and `reply`. | A retry after a lost answer must not send the words, or count a press, twice. |
| WTR-16 | **A whole report fits one answer.** An answer travels in one session CONTROL message of at most 64 KiB, but a report may hold 200 messages of 6 000 characters: every answer with a whole report (get, create, reply, act) keeps the report's own words and the newest messages within three quarters of that (UTF-8 bytes of the JSON) and says how many of the oldest it left out (`earlier`). The report pages show one quiet line above the conversation then (“Earlier messages aren’t shown here.”). The box's copy keeps the whole report. | A long conversation must still open, on the phone too. |
| WTR-17 | **Link ended: read and withdraw.** The manager forwards the team routes whenever control knows the box (MG-193, like claim reports); the box lets the list, a report and a withdrawal through then too, and keeps sending (a report, a reply, It works, Still happening) for Link on or in grace, saying plainly otherwise (“Sending to the Whombat Team needs Whombat Link on. You can still read and withdraw your reports.”). Control decides what each Link state allows. | What the person sent stays theirs to read and take back when Link stops. |
| WTR-18 | **The words stay out of receipts.** `teamReports.create`, `.reply` and `.act` are secret ops on the session: their receipts keep neither a digest of the input nor the answer (the person's and the team's words), and nothing of either is logged. A retry of one with the same session request id gets “check the current state”. | Withdrawing must take the words back from the box as well, not leave them in a week of receipts. |
