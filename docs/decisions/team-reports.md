# Reports to the Whombat Team (workstream `team-reports`)

John, 2026-11-25: Whombat Link people tell the team behind Whombat about a problem or an idea from inside Whombat,
seamlessly, the way a product's customers send reports to a report tracker; the reports arrive on the team's own
Whombat (“Home”) and take the same flow as such reports (report-trackers.md): the assistant checks whether it is a real
bug, T3 Code fixes it, another model reviews, Whombat merges on its checks and tells the person when the fix reaches
them. Planned with the Council (three rounds); John's decisions the same day: no encryption (a quiet warning on the form
is enough), a pilot for two invited people, replies signed “Whombat Team (AI assistant)”, 90 days after closing, the
assistant drafts reports.

Code: `packages/core/src/domain/team-reports.ts` (shapes, vocabulary, words), `packages/core/src/contracts/team-reports.ts`
(the apps' REST), `packages/control-contracts/src/device/team-reports.ts` (device ops), `apps/server/src/team-reports/`
(the box), `appliance/manager/internal/broker/teamreports.go` (the manager), the apps' Reports pages. Control's side and
the team's tracker plugin are Cybex's own (whombat-cloud `control/docs/decisions/team-reports.md`, `reports-plugin/`).

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TR-01 | **Who**: a person whose Whombat runs Link (`on` or `grace`) and whose Link account is on control's invitation list; control says whether they may send (`team.reports.list` `available`, `reason` `not_invited`, `off`, `link_off`). The entry shows only where sending is possible or the person has reports; nobody else sees it. | A pilot for two people first; calm for everyone else. |
| TR-02 | **Readable at control.** Control keeps the reports as the record, readable by the team, unlike everything else that crosses it (control CL-02, contracts CT-10). The form says so in one quiet line: “The Whombat Team and its AI assistant read what you send here. Leave out passwords and private details.” | John: encryption isn't needed, a quiet warning is enough. |
| TR-03 | **The way through**: the apps call the box (`teamReports.*`, session minor 10); the box calls control through the manager (`team.reports.list/get/create/reply/act`), the words as base64url JSON checked with core's shapes on both ends; the manager forwards them unread. The box adds its own version and channel to a new report, the app its own version, device and language. | One way for every Link feature; the cross-language schemas stay ASCII. |
| TR-04 | **The box keeps a copy** of each of its reports as last read (the whole report and the revision the person read): the list shows it while control can't be reached (`stale`), and a report is unread while control's `revision` is past the one read. A withdrawn report's copy goes at once. | What is new must be known on the box; a list that vanishes offline looks broken. |
| TR-05 | **Following the answers**: while the box has an open report, it asks control's list every 10 minutes (and when an app opens the list). A team change marks the report unread. A question (`needs_information`) or a fix to try (`available`) is an Inbox item and a push in fixed words, never the team's: “The Whombat Team asked about your report” / “A fix for your report is ready to try”; tapping opens the report. | Inbox only when the person is needed; push previews pass through control. |
| TR-06 | **The team's words stay out of the person's assistant.** They never reach Chat, the assistant's context, Search, memory or automation triggers; they show only on the report's page, as plain text (links as text, no pictures). | Outsiders' words are never instructions (OP-17, OT-25). |
| TR-07 | **The assistant drafts.** When the person says something in Whombat doesn't work or wishes it did something, the assistant may call `report_to_whombat_team` with the kind and its draft of the words. The box keeps the draft and asks the person on a card (always Ask, whatever the keys say, like a shared link) that shows exactly what is sent; Send sends it as the person; Edit opens the form with the draft (`teamReports.draft`); a sent or declined draft is done. The assistant can read the person's reports with `whombat_team_reports`: number, kind, title, status in words and dates, never the team's words. | Seamless (John's choice), and only the person's press sends. |
| TR-08 | **Statuses** are the tracker's vocabulary; the person reads `TEAM_REPORT_STATUS_WORDS` and a four-step track (Received → Looking into it → Being fixed → Fixed). **It works** and **Still happening** show once a fix is available to them or the report was closed. | The team's tracker automations read them as they are. |
| TR-09 | **Withdraw** deletes the report at control at once (control tells the team's tracker) and its copy on the box. | The person's words are theirs to take back. |
| TR-10 | **Kept** until 90 days after the report closes; when the Link account is erased or the box leaves Link, control deletes its reports. A Whombat restored onto new hardware starts without its earlier reports. | John, 2026-11-25. |
| TR-11 | **Not in the pilot**: screenshots, diagnostics and logs (a report is text and versions), email about reports (Link's own notification email covers the Inbox item for those who chose it). | Small first; size limits and privacy each need their own decision. |
| TR-12 | **The team's side** is Cybex's: control keeps and serves the reports to the team's Whombat through an API of its own, and a private tracker plugin there feeds the report-tracker automations, a Beta and a Production connection by the reporter's channel, replies signed “Whombat Team (AI assistant)” (a connection's own signature, TR-13). Nothing in this repository names the team's Whombat. | The tracker flow is the one every report tracker's reports take (report-trackers.md). |
| TR-13 | **A tracker connection may sign its replies itself** (`signature`, the connection's own words, up to 60 characters): Whombat adds it after a blank line instead of “<agent> - AI Assistant”. Without one it signs as before (TA-26). | John: “Whombat Team (AI assistant)”; a reporter's own assistant may be called Dusty too. |
