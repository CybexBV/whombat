# Looks ahead (LKA)

John, 2026-11-25: ChatGPT’s “Dots” posted unasked that a concert starts at 20:45, not 20:00 as in his calendar, that a
football match is next to the venue, and that a booked event is full with no confirmation in his mail. The first version
checked tomorrow’s location-filled calendar events once against mail and plain HTTP pages. Later the same day John
asked to expand Looks ahead to work much closer to Dots while keeping Whombat’s stronger security isolation (no memory
poisoning from untrusted mail or web text): multi-stage checks across the week ahead, events without a filled-in
location and real all-day trips, same-day schedule context for conflict detection, JS-heavy web reading, read-only file
and document lookups (Drive/OneDrive, Paperless-ngx, Notion), incoming mail as a proactive trigger, one-tap next-step
chips, user-owned standing rules, active “Not useful” calibration, structured per-event stage notes, and Settings
visibility with “Check now”.

The morning and evening brief stays removed (`brief-removed.md`): Looks ahead posts only a finding, never a summary.

## What it does

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-01 | **Multi-stage calendar checks plus incoming mail sweeps.** A home job every five minutes (`looks-ahead/watch.ts`) reads the person’s enabled calendars up to 7 days ahead (`AHEAD_READ_MS`) and recent inbox messages across Google, Microsoft 365 and Email/IMAP (`looks-ahead/mail.ts`, when `mailOn` is on and `mail.read` is Allowed), then starts the highest-priority due check (`planAheadPass`). Calendar checks run in three stages (`aheadStage`): `early` (2–7 days ahead, catching missing tickets/bookings while there is still time), `eve` (afternoon before from 14:00, or today > 4 h before: door times, crowds, closures, bookings) and `morning` (today 90 min–4 h before: cancellations and travel disruptions). Whombat wakes the assistant (trigger `ahead`); it never wakes itself. | Finding missing tickets 5 days ahead leaves time to rebook; checking again the afternoon before and morning of catches late time changes and disruptions. |
| LKA-02 | **Which events and emails**: timed or real all-day plans (generic placeholders like “Out of office” or “Verjaardag” with no place/notes stay skipped), one-off events or modified occurrences of a repeating series (`recurringException`), not online-only meetings (`ONLINE_PLACE` or `meetUrl` with no physical place), not declined, unanswered (`needs_action`) or cancelled (`aheadSkip`). Events with a real title and an empty `location` field are checked. Incoming mail runs through a cheap deterministic pre-filter (`aheadMailCandidate`, English and Dutch booking/deadline signals plus user rule keywords, skipping own mail and newsletters) before any AI turn runs. | People write “Coldplay” or “Weekend Rome” without a location field or as all-day events; modified recurring events often moved; cheap pre-filtering keeps newsletters from spending tokens. |
| LKA-03 | **Which calendars and mailboxes**: every calendar of the person’s Google and Microsoft 365 accounts they can add to and show (their own and a shared Family calendar; subscriptions out), toggled per calendar in Settings (`calendarIsOn`); plus every active Google, Microsoft 365 and Email/IMAP mailbox when `mailOn` is on (on by default). | Family plans live on a separate calendar; bookings and deadlines arrive across every connected inbox. |
| LKA-04 | **Once per version and stage, prioritized**: an event’s stage key is `<version>` for `eve` and `<version>:<stage>` for `early`/`morning` (`aheadStageVersion`), keeping `ahead_checks_version_idx` atomic. A version+stage is never checked twice, while later stages (`eve` after `early`, `morning` after `eve`) still check and carry structured prior-stage notes so nothing is repeated. Priority order (`planAheadPass`): stage urgency (`morning` > `eve` > `mail` > `early`), then signal score (`aheadPriority`: physical place, travel/venue keywords, modified occurrence, all-day trip, notes), then soonest start. | Routine lunches no longer crowd out flights or concerts, and later stages catch fresh disruptions without repeating earlier alerts. |
| LKA-05 | **Budgets**: one check at a time, at most 8 checks a day and 4 findings a day (`AHEAD_CHECKS_PER_DAY`, `AHEAD_FINDINGS_PER_DAY`); checks begin 08:00–20:30 and tell only until 21:00 in the Whombat’s zone; only while a turn of the person’s could run (`providerReadiness`). Calendars and mailboxes are cached for 15 minutes unless `looksAhead.checkNow` asks for a fresh pass. | Stays calm and bounded even with multi-stage calendar and mail sweeps. |
| LKA-06 | **On by default, with rules, recent checks and Check now.** Settings → {assistant} → Looks ahead (`looksAhead.get`, `looksAhead.update`, `looksAhead.checkNow`; session minor 12, `LOOKS_AHEAD_V2_MINOR`) shows the main switch with “Check now”, calendars, incoming mail toggle, user-owned standing rules (“What to watch for”, up to 20), stopped kinds, 7-day count and the 15 most recent checks. The assistant reads and changes it as the person asks (`ASSISTANT_SETTINGS` page `agent.ahead`). | Gives Dots-style Custom Rules and Activity-view visibility without cluttering Chat. |

## The quiet check

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-10 | **The person’s turn, on the background model, in its own quiet context.** `ahead` never merges with Chat turns, takes no follow-ups, and reads no conversation history. Calendar checks receive the same day’s other accepted plans (`daySchedule`, excluding declined/unanswered/cancelled events); mail checks receive the next 14 days of accepted calendar plans. | Lets Dusty spot cross-calendar conflicts, tight travel gaps and bookings missing from the calendar without exposing `calendar_events` to stranger invites. |
| LKA-11 | **Nothing shows it at work**: no “is thinking”, its final text is never posted, it never makes Chat look busy on Home, and failures are silent. Settings’ Recent checks shows the outcome calmly. | Most checks find nothing; quiet execution prevents noise while Settings remains transparent. |
| LKA-12 | **What it may use, in code** (`AHEAD_TOOL_NAMES`, enforced by both the server and Hermes policy): `mail_search`, `mail_read`, `files_search`, `files_read`, `ahead_read_docs` (strictly read-only Paperless-ngx and Notion search, listed only when connected), `search_web`, `read_web_page`, and `ahead_report`. No writes, sends, memory, settings, Vault, general `connection_call`, servers, general browser, commands or helpers. Private tools run only when their key is Allowed (`mail.read`, `files.read`, `web.browse`). | Read-only access to mail, Drive/OneDrive, Paperless and Notion finds PDF tickets and meeting docs without exposing any write surface. |
| LKA-13 | **No general memory writes (preventing memory poisoning)**: Hermes memory/skill tools stay blocked, `review: false`, and every ended `ahead` session is dropped (`hermes_session_drops`). Continuity across stages of the *same* event uses a tiny, scrubbed `note` column on `ahead_checks` passed only back to later stages of that `eventKey` as delimited data. Lasting preferences come exclusively from user-owned `rules` and recent `"Not useful"` verdicts (`feedbackNote`). | Gives Dots-style continuity and tuning without Dots’s memory-poisoning vector. |
| LKA-14 | **The web first, enhanced for modern sites, and closed once any private data is read**: `search_web` runs Brave Search (with `extra_snippets`) using public words only (`aheadQueryRefusal`). `read_web_page` extracts Schema.org JSON-LD event data before stripping HTML, and falls back to an ephemeral, incognito headless Chromium `--dump-dom` routed through a local SSRF-checked public-IP proxy (`isPublicAddress` + DNS pinning, zero cookies/Vault) on JS-only shells or 403/429/503 bot-blocks. Once a check reads mail, files or docs — or from the start of a mail-triggered check (`mailReadAt` stamped on start) — the web is permanently closed. | Venue and ticketing sites work reliably while untrusted mail or private files can never steer outbound web traffic. |
| LKA-15 | **It ends with `ahead_report`**, once: `found: false` (with optional factual `note` for later stages), or a finding with primary `kind`, optional `extraKinds` (up to 2 extra when the same plan has two real issues), plain text (up to 900 chars, scrubbed of links/markup/phones), verified `sources` (`url` from `read_web_page` or attempted-and-blocked search fallback labeled `site (search)`, `mailId`, `fileId`, `docId`, or `{ calendar: true }`), optional one-tap `action` chip label (up to 52 chars), and optional `note`. Whombat names every source itself. | “Sourced or silent” across web, mail, files, docs and calendar conflicts, supporting multi-issue alerts and actionable next steps. |

## A finding

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-20 | **One thread per event (or email)**, posted in the person’s Chat with push and no link previews: if an earlier stage already posted a finding for the same event, a later stage’s new finding replies in that existing thread rather than opening a second one. | Keeps one tidy thread per plan across early, day-before and morning-of checks. |
| LKA-21 | **One-tap action chip plus feedback replies**: when `ahead_report` proposes an `action` (scrubbed plain words like “Update event to 20:45” or “Add to calendar”), it becomes the first quick-reply chip ahead of “Not useful” and “Stop checking for this” (3 total, within `SUGGESTIONS_MAX`). Tapping it posts the person’s own words into the thread, waking their normal trusted turn (which verifies details and asks for approval via normal keys/cards before changing anything). “Not useful” records `feedback` plus an optional `reason` (`feedbackNote`) fed into future checks’ `recentDismissed`, and Dusty may offer to save a lasting preference as a standing rule (`addRule`). | Works on every existing web and mobile app immediately, keeps background checks 100% read-only, and turns “Not useful” into real calibration. |
| LKA-22 | **Not told**: kept quiet when Looks ahead or its calendar/mail toggle is off, every reported kind is stopped, the daily finding cap (4) is reached, night hours, < 30 min before a timed event, or a calendar event changed/went meanwhile. | Keeps alerts accurate and calm. |
| LKA-23 | **Settling**: calendar finding threads settle 2 hours after the event ends (and mail finding threads 48 hours after posting) when the person didn’t write in them (`settleResolvedThreads`). Rows expire 30 days after `endAt`. | Keeps Chat clean once the plan or window has passed. |
