# Looks ahead (LKA)

John, 2026-11-25: ChatGPT’s “Dots” posted unasked that a concert starts at 20:45, not 20:00 as in his calendar, that a
football match is next to the venue, and that a booked event is full with no confirmation in his mail. Looks ahead
checks the week’s calendar plans in stages against mail, files and the web. The same evening he asked for it to work
like Dots where Dots is stronger, while keeping Whombat’s isolation (nothing a stranger wrote steers a tool or stays as
free memory): Dots starts from everything new, not from a plan; it follows a matter over days; and it watches what the
person asked it to. So Looks ahead also looks through new mail as a whole a few times a day (the triage), keeps a
short list of matters it follows (“Keeping an eye on”), and takes “tell me when the notary replies” from Chat.

The morning and evening brief stays removed (`brief-removed.md`): Looks ahead posts only a finding, never a summary.

## What it does

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-01 | **Calendar stages plus a look through new mail.** A home job every five minutes (`looks-ahead/watch.ts`) reads the person’s enabled calendars up to 7 days ahead (`AHEAD_READ_MS`) and, when `mailOn` is on and `mail.read` is Allowed, the newest inbox mail of every Google, Microsoft 365 and Email/IMAP mailbox (`looks-ahead/mail.ts`), then starts the one most urgent thing due (`planAheadPass`): a calendar check, a mail check, a follow-up of a matter, or a triage pass. Calendar checks run in three stages (`aheadStage`): `early` (2–7 days ahead), `eve` (the afternoon before from 14:00, or today more than 4 h before) and `morning` (today, 90 min–4 h before). Whombat wakes the assistant (trigger `ahead`); it never wakes itself. | Missing tickets found days ahead leave time to rebook; mail holds most of what waits on a person, and nothing there is tied to a plan. |
| LKA-02 | **Which events**: timed or real all-day plans (generic placeholders like “Out of office” or “Verjaardag” with no place or notes stay skipped), one-off events or modified occurrences of a repeating series (`recurringException`), not online-only meetings, not declined, unanswered (`needs_action`) or cancelled (`aheadSkip`). Events with a real title and an empty `location` are checked. Which mail is chosen by the triage (LKA-30), not by keywords. | People write “Coldplay” or “Weekend Rome” without a location; keywords miss “can you answer me by Friday?”. |
| LKA-03 | **Which calendars and mailboxes**: every calendar of the person’s Google and Microsoft 365 accounts they can add to and show (their own and a shared Family calendar; subscriptions out), toggled per calendar in Settings (`calendarIsOn`); plus every active Google, Microsoft 365 and Email/IMAP mailbox when `mailOn` is on (on by default). | Family plans live on a separate calendar; what waits on a person arrives in every inbox. |
| LKA-04 | **Once per version and stage, the most urgent first.** An event’s stage key is `<version>` for `eve` and `<version>:<stage>` otherwise (`aheadStageVersion`); a mail is checked once (`mail:<connection>:<message>`); a matter is followed up once per reason (`matter:<id>`, version `due` or `no_answer`); a triage pass is its own row (`triage:<start>`). Order (`planAheadPass`): `morning`, `eve`, `mail` (one that matches a matter first), `matter`, `triage`, `early`; within a stage the signal score (`aheadPriority`), then the soonest. | Nothing is checked twice, and what can’t wait goes first. |
| LKA-05 | **Budgets**: one thing at a time; at most 12 checks a day (`AHEAD_CHECKS_PER_DAY`) and 3 triage passes (`AHEAD_TRIAGE_PER_DAY`, at least 3.5 h apart, `AHEAD_TRIAGE_GAP_MS`, not counted as checks); at most 4 findings a day (`AHEAD_FINDINGS_PER_DAY`). Checks begin 08:00–20:30 and tell until 21:00 in the Whombat’s zone, and only while a turn of the person’s could run (`providerReadiness`). Calendars and mailboxes are read at most every 15 minutes unless `looksAhead.checkNow` asks for a fresh pass. | Calm and bounded, whatever the inbox does. |
| LKA-06 | **On by default, visible in Settings.** Settings → {assistant} → Looks ahead (`looksAhead.get`, `looksAhead.update`, `looksAhead.checkNow`) shows the switch with “Check now”, calendars, the incoming mail switch, “Keeping an eye on” (the open matters, each with Remove; session minor 14, `LOOKS_AHEAD_MATTERS_MINOR`), the person’s standing rules (“What to watch for”, up to 20), stopped kinds, the week’s count and the 15 most recent checks. The assistant reads and changes it as the person asks (`ASSISTANT_SETTINGS` page `agent.ahead`). An app older than a minor keeps working: the view only gains optional fields, and a recent row keeps the `source` and `stage` values older apps know (a triage pass or a follow-up is a `mail` row with `of`). | The person sees what it does and what it remembers, without clutter in Chat. |

## The quiet check

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-10 | **The person’s turn, on the background model, in its own quiet context.** `ahead` never merges with Chat turns, takes no follow-ups, and reads no conversation history. A calendar check gets the same day’s other accepted plans (`daySchedule`); a mail check, a follow-up and a triage pass get the next 14 days of accepted plans. | It can spot conflicts and bookings missing from the calendar without the calendar tool or the chat. |
| LKA-11 | **Nothing shows it at work**: no “is thinking”, its final text is never posted, it never makes Chat look busy on Home, and failures are silent. Settings’ Recent checks shows the outcome calmly. | Most checks find nothing. |
| LKA-12 | **What it may use, in code** (`AHEAD_TOOL_NAMES`, enforced by the server and by the Hermes policy): `mail_search`, `mail_read`, `files_search`, `files_read`, `ahead_read_docs` (read-only Paperless-ngx and Notion search, listed only when connected), `search_web`, `read_web_page`, and its ends `ahead_report` and `ahead_triage`. A triage pass is served only `ahead_triage`: it reads nothing but what Whombat gave it. No writes, sends, memory, settings, Vault, general `connection_call`, servers, general browser, commands or helpers. Private tools run only when their key is Allowed (`mail.read`, `files.read`, `web.browse`). | Read-only all the way; the pass that sees most strangers’ words has no tool to reach anything with. |
| LKA-13 | **No free memory.** Hermes memory and skill tools stay blocked, `review: false`, and every ended `ahead` session is dropped (`hermes_session_drops`). What lasts is only: a scrubbed `note` (300 characters) for later stages of the same event, the matters list (LKA-40: fixed short fields, scrubbed, shown and removable in Settings), the person’s own `rules`, and their recent “Not useful” verdicts. All of it reaches a later check as delimited data. | Continuity and tuning without a place where a stranger’s text could settle unseen. |
| LKA-14 | **The web first, and closed once any private data is read**: `search_web` runs Brave Search with public words only (`aheadQueryRefusal`); `read_web_page` reads a page the check saw (JSON-LD first, a headless Chromium through the SSRF-checked proxy for script-only or blocking sites). Once a check reads mail, files or docs the web is closed for good; a mail check, a follow-up and a triage pass start with it closed. | Untrusted mail or private files can never steer outbound traffic. |
| LKA-15 | **A check ends with `ahead_report`**, once: `found: false` (with an optional factual `note`), or a finding with a primary `kind`, up to 2 `extraKinds`, plain text (up to 900 characters, scrubbed of links, markup and phone numbers), verified `sources` (a page it read, a mail, file or doc it read, `{ calendar: true }`, or in a follow-up `{ matter: true }`), an optional one-tap `action` label and an optional `note`. A mail check or a follow-up may add `matter` (LKA-41). Whombat names every source itself. A triage pass ends with `ahead_triage` instead (LKA-31). | “Sourced or silent”, and the model’s words never carry a link or a name of a source. |

## A finding

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-20 | **One thread per plan, mail or matter**, in the person’s Chat with a push and no link previews: a later stage of an event replies in the thread an earlier stage began; everything about one matter goes in that matter’s thread (for one the person asked for, the thread they asked in). | One tidy thread per thing. |
| LKA-21 | **One-tap action plus feedback replies**: a proposed `action` (“Update event to 20:45”, “Draft a reminder”) is the first quick reply, ahead of “Not useful” and “Stop checking for this”. Tapping it posts the person’s own words, which wake their normal turn (it verifies and asks before changing anything). “Not useful” records `feedback` and an optional reason for later checks (`recentDismissed`); “Stop checking for this” stops the finding’s kind, or, under a finding about a matter, stops keeping an eye on that matter. | Checks stay read-only, and one tap is enough to act or to tune. |
| LKA-22 | **Not told**: when Looks ahead or its calendar or mail switch is off, every reported kind is stopped, the day’s findings are used, at night, less than 30 minutes before a timed event, or the event changed meanwhile. News on a matter the person asked for is told all the same (not counted, not stopped by a kind), still never at night: it waits for the morning. | Calm, and a thing the person asked for is never dropped. |
| LKA-23 | **Settling**: an event’s thread settles 2 hours after it ends, a mail finding’s 48 hours after posting, when the person didn’t write in it (`settleResolvedThreads`). Rows go 30 days after `endAt`. | Chat stays clean once it has passed. |

## Incoming mail: the triage

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-30 | **A triage pass instead of keywords.** Up to three times a day, when new mail came since the last pass, one quiet turn (`source: 'triage'`) sees the new inbox mail as a numbered list (`m1`…: sender, subject, date, a short snippet; at most 60, `AHEAD_TRIAGE_MAIL_MAX`), the next 14 days of plans, the open matters (`k1`…), the rules and the recent “Not useful” verdicts. It is told what needs a person: someone waiting for their answer or decision, a deadline, payment or choice waiting on them, a booking or change of plan, a problem with an account or a payment, news on a matter. | “Can you answer me by Friday?” has no keyword; one cheap pass over the headers replaces one check per keyword hit. |
| LKA-31 | **It only picks.** A triage pass ends with `ahead_triage`: at most 3 picks (`AHEAD_TRIAGE_PICKS_MAX`), each the number of a mail and optionally the matter it belongs to. No words of its own, no other tool. Whombat maps the numbers to the mails it listed. Each pick becomes an ordinary mail check (LKA-10…15) on a later pass, which reads that one mail and reports or stays silent. | A stranger’s subject line can at most get a mail picked or passed over; each mail is then read on its own, so one can’t speak for another. |
| LKA-32 | **Nothing is missed for being late.** A pass covers the mail since the last pass began (at most 48 hours; the first one 24 hours), read as the newest 50 per mailbox (`AHEAD_MAIL_SWEEP_LIMIT`), whenever it runs: mail that arrived at night or while the day’s checks were used is in the next pass. The person’s own mail and plain bulk mail (`aheadMailNoise`) are left out before the turn; a mail with a booking or deadline word or a word of a rule is listed first (`aheadMailSignal`) when there are more than 60. | The newest 15, read only while the watcher was free, lost mail on a busy day. |
| LKA-33 | **Picks wait their turn.** A pass’s picks stay on its row (`event.triage.picks`) for 48 hours; the watcher starts them one at a time as mail checks within the day’s budget. “Check now” may run a pass before the 3.5 hours are over. | One thing at a time holds here too. |

## Keeping an eye on (matters)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| LKA-40 | **A short list Whombat owns** (`ahead_matters`): what it is (`title`, 120 characters), who (`who`, 80), whether it waits on the person or on them (`waitingOn`), an optional date (`dueAt`), a short `note`, and how a mail is matched to it (`match`: mail threads, sender words, subject words). At most 30 open (`AHEAD_MATTERS_MAX`). Every word in it is scrubbed like a finding’s (`findingWords`: no link, site, address, phone number or markup). It goes after 30 days without news (`AHEAD_MATTER_KEEP_MS`). | Following a refund or a notary over days needs something kept; fixed short fields the person can see and remove are not a free memory. |
| LKA-41 | **Who opens one.** The person, in their own words in Chat (LKA-45). Or a mail check, with `ahead_report`’s `matter` (`title`, `who`, `waitingOn`, `due`), when the mail starts something that goes on after today: a case with a company, a question the person waits on, a request with a date. At most 3 a day from checks (`AHEAD_MATTERS_OPENED_PER_DAY`). A matter from a check is matched only by what Whombat knows of that mail (its thread and its sender’s address), never by words the model chose. A check may also update or close the matter it was given (`ref`, `note`, `close`). | A stranger’s mail can open a line in a list; it can’t decide what later mail is read because of it. |
| LKA-42 | **Mail that matches goes first.** New mail in a matter’s thread, from its sender, or (for one the person asked for) with its words becomes a mail check at once, without a triage pass, and the check is given the matter and what was told about it before. It reports the news in the matter’s thread, or stays silent when the mail isn’t about it. | “The notary replied” within minutes, not at the next pass. |
| LKA-43 | **News the person asked for is always told** (LKA-22), in the thread where they asked. A matter is closed when the check says it is done, when the person removes it (Settings, or “Stop checking for this” under its finding), or when it runs out. | They asked. |
| LKA-44 | **One follow-up, by the clock.** Whombat, not the model, decides when (`aheadMatterDue`): a matter that waits on the person, the day before its date; one that waits on them, at its date or after 5 days without news (`AHEAD_MATTER_NO_ANSWER_MS`). Once per matter and reason. The follow-up (`source: 'matter'`) reads the mail to see whether it was handled: handled, it closes the matter silently; not, it tells (“They asked for an answer by Friday”, “Still nothing from the notary since the 19th”) and may offer “Draft a reminder”. | The deadline nobody answered and the answer that never came are what a list is for. |
| LKA-45 | **“Tell me when the notary replies.”** In the person’s own turn the assistant has `ahead_watch` (`start`, `list`, `stop`): what to watch, who, the sender words and subject words to match, waiting on whom, an optional date. Only a turn the person began themselves is served it: never an `ahead` turn, an automation’s or an outsider’s. It needs Looks ahead and incoming mail on. The assistant answers in one line that it will tell them in this thread. | Only the person’s own words make a watch, as with a Find My watch. |
| LKA-46 | **Seen and removed in Settings.** “Keeping an eye on” lists each open matter with who, what it waits on and its date, and Remove (`looksAhead.update` `{ removeMatter }`). The assistant lists and stops them too. | Unlike notes nobody can read, the person owns this list. |

## Weighed and left

- **Reading the mails in the triage pass itself** (one turn, up to three findings): cheaper, but one mail’s text would sit
  beside another’s finding, and a check row is one thread. Picks that become ordinary checks keep each mail alone.
- **A free note per matter that grows** (as Dots keeps): left for fixed fields; a stranger’s text gets no room to settle.
- **Sent mail, Moneybird, Todoist as sources**: after a week of findings shows what is missed.
- **A new kind for news on a matter**: older apps read the kinds as a closed list; such a finding is `deadline` when it
  waits on the person and `other` otherwise, and its “Stop checking for this” stops the matter, not a kind.
