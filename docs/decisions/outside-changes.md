# What changed before it broke, and settings copies (CHG)

The look-after build, 2026-11-28: when something breaks at 03:15 people don't know what changed. Done recently
(`done-today.md`) keeps only what Whombat did, and a thing's History (`monitoring.md` MON-51) its outages and alerts.
What changed outside Whombat — an image Watchtower pulled, an unattended kernel update, new firmware, a proxy host
edited by hand — was gone by the next read. And when a router or Pi-hole dies, its settings go with it.

Everything here stays calm (`watch-defaults.md`) and plain (`concise-replies.md`).

## The change log

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CHG-01 | **Whombat compares what it already reads; it reads nothing new for it.** A connection's health pass gives what its kind read (`KindSeen.tracked`): the service's own version (Plex, Jellyfin, the *arr apps, SABnzbd, qBittorrent, Seerr, NZBHydra2, Home Assistant, UniFi Network, OPNsense, Pi-hole and AdGuard, ESPHome, Frigate, UniFi Protect, Proxmox, TrueNAS, Synology, QNAP, Nextcloud, Immich, Paperless, PBS, Portainer, Grafana), UniFi's devices' firmware by name, Nginx Proxy Manager's proxy hosts and Cloudflare's tunnel routes by host. A 10-minute home job reads each online server's detail from the hosts service once: a restart (the boot time moved more than 2 minutes), its kernel, its system's own version, waiting updates that were installed (the count fell to zero), and its containers' images. | People need what changed, not more polling; every source is a read Whombat already makes or a page already shows. |
| CHG-02 | **Left out as noise**: metrics, health, DHCP leases, a container or device that came or went (not a change of it), a part one read couldn't see (it keeps the read before), Whombat's own containers (`whombat.service`) and Unraid's Docker list (it has no image id). Cloudflare tunnels: only those managed in Cloudflare (`remote_config`) have routes Whombat can read; at most 10 tunnels, cached 15 minutes. | A change log that lists churn is one nobody reads. |
| CHG-03 | **A first read only sets what the next is compared with** (`change_baselines`, one row per thing and source: `service`, `server`, `containers`, `settings`). Nothing is told on an update or a new connection. | Without a before there is no change. |
| CHG-04 | **Bursts collapse.** More than 3 changes of one kind in one read are one change with up to 5 names (“5 containers got new images: …”). The same item changed again within 30 minutes merges into the first change (from the first value to the last); one that went back is gone. A new image pulled under the same tag (`app:latest` to `app:latest`) never undoes another: two of them are one change. | A rolling update or a flapping route is one event to a person. |
| CHG-05 | **By Whombat or outside Whombat.** A change is Whombat's when a `changes` row (Done recently) about the thing, or about the server a connection runs on, came between 2 hours before the read before and this read, and wasn't undone; else it is outside Whombat. A merge keeps “by Whombat” once either part was. | Whombat's own updates are the most common change; saying so stops a false alarm. The window covers a care plan's update that takes a while to show. |
| CHG-06 | **Kept 90 days** (`thing_changes`, pruned by the servers' pass), like incidents. | Same reach as History's 90 days. |
| CHG-07 | **Facts are kept, words are made when read** (`changes/words.ts`), in the reader's language: “Immich went from 2.3.0 to 2.4.0”, “Kernel updated to 6.12.1 and the server restarted”, “Proxy host photos.example.com now points to http://10.100.0.12:2283”. Names, versions and hosts are the service's; every other word is Whombat's (EN and NL). | A Dutch reader sees Dutch, and a wording change reaches old rows. |
| CHG-08 | **Silent.** A change never alerts, pushes, lands in the Inbox, starts an automation or a look. An open page hears `home.changed {things}` so its History reads again. | It is context for when something breaks, not news. |
| CHG-09 | **History lists changes beside outages**, newest first, on the web and the phone (`ThingHistory.changes`, session minor 22): the change's words, when Whombat saw it, and “Changed by Whombat” or “Changed outside Whombat”, in grey (no red or amber dot). An older app ignores the field. | One place to see what happened and what changed before it. |
| CHG-10 | **A container's image id comes from the agent** (`Guest.image_id`, additive in the agent's report and the hub's guest; the hub passes it through for Docker only). Without it a new image pulled under the same tag (`:latest`, `:release`) looks the same; an older agent or hub sends none, and Whombat then compares the image reference only. This is a newly signed agent. | Watchtower-style updates keep the tag; the image id is the only fact that changes, and no agent reported it. |

## Looks and the assistant

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CHG-20 | **A look reads what changed just before.** When the assistant looks into a thing, its turn gets up to 8 changes of the thing and the server it runs on from the 48 hours before the trouble began (`LookDispatchContext.changes`), as data, with: say it “changed just before” when it fits, never that it caused this unless it checked. | The likely trigger, without a claim nobody can prove. |
| CHG-21 | **`what_changed`** (thing optional, hours 1–2160, default 48): the assistant reads changes of one thing (a connection also covers its server) or of all it may see, newest first, up to 60, with who made each. Read-only, offered wherever `watch_history` is. | “What changed on blackbox this week?” is a question people ask. |

## Settings copies

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CHG-30 | **Three services get a nightly settings copy**: OPNsense (`/api/core/backup/download/this`, its `config.xml`), Pi-hole 6 (Teleporter, `/api/teleporter`, a zip) and Nginx Proxy Manager (its proxy hosts, redirects, streams, 404 hosts and access lists as one JSON file, without passwords; certificates aren't in it). Each through the connection and key Whombat already has. Not UniFi (its backup isn't in the integration API Whombat signs in to) and not Home Assistant (its own backups already exist and are watched). | Their settings are precious, small and exportable through what Whombat already holds. |
| CHG-31 | **When**: the first within the hour of connecting, then one a night between 02:00 and 05:00 at the box's clock (any time once the last try is a day and a half old); a failed try again after an hour, three times, then the next night. A 15-minute home job looks for what is due; one try has 60 seconds and 20 MB. | Nightly is enough for settings, and the night is quiet. |
| CHG-32 | **Kept on the box, sealed** with the master key like Vault secrets (`settings_copies.sealed`, `custody.sealBytes`, its own label per copy), so it rides in the box's own backups and nowhere else. Not in the person's backup file. The newest 10 different copies are kept. | Settings hold addresses and keys; the box's seal is what guards those already. |
| CHG-33 | **Same is not kept twice.** Bytes the same as the newest only move when it was checked; a copy whose settings compare the same (a Teleporter zip's own times) replaces the newest. Otherwise it is kept beside it. | Ten copies of the same night are worth one. |
| CHG-34 | **Consecutive copies are compared into a change** (“OPNsense’s settings changed: firewall rules (2 added, 1 changed)”), by part, from a summary of hashed items (`changes/copies/summary.ts`). Secrets are cleaned out before hashing (passwords, keys, tokens, certificates, SNMP communities, an LDAP bind password, OpenVPN's TLS key, shared keys), and so are fields that change by themselves (revisions, times). Only the summary's counts reach the change log, and only the change's words reach the assistant; a copy's bytes never do. NPM's routes are left out of its summary: its proxy hosts are told as routes already (CHG-01). A failed summary read keeps the last one. | What changed, in a line, without handing the settings to the AI. |
| CHG-35 | **On the service's page**: a card “Settings copy · last night” (or today 03:12, or a date) with how many are kept, one line on what it holds (“Restore one in OPNsense itself.”) and **Download** of the newest. Download reads `things.settingsCopy` through an export stream (it is larger than a message), only the person. Before the first copy: “No settings copy yet”. | The copy is there when the router is dead and the person needs it. |
| CHG-36 | **Restoring is the service's own.** Whombat offers Download only; the person imports it in the service (OPNsense's Backups page, Pi-hole's Teleporter, NPM by hand). | A restore that goes wrong takes the network down; that is the person's step. |
| CHG-37 | **One check, “Settings copy failing”** (`settings.copy`, `nature: 'problem'`, starts on). A backup not happening is one of the very important ones (`watch-defaults.md` WD-01), and Whombat can say what to do (sign in again, a key without the right). Its line shows only once a copy has worked and none has for 48 hours, so one missed night or a service just connected says nothing. The card says the last try's problem at once. | Calm by default and still loud when the copies stopped. |
