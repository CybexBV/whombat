# A service's settings, changed from its page or by the assistant (SST)

John, 2026-11-04 (main checkout): Whombat should become more of a server and services dashboard, with sub-views (how
the disks are used) and full service pages (Plex) with useful information and settings, and the assistant one click
away for each part. He liked the idea that the assistant makes the changes instead of Whombat building forms for them.
Two other models reviewed that against the code at the highest effort and both came back with the same middle way:
the settings that matter are declared per service as typed changes that a switch, a Change sheet and the assistant all
run through one path, every change is checked with the service and can be put back, and only the long tail goes to the
assistant's free-form calls. John agreed and saw the mockups (`~/.cache/wb-tmp/mockups/service-dash-v2/`), then:
“Implement this fully”.

Builds on service-dashboards.md (SD-*), server-pages.md (SPG-*), assistant-settings.md (ASG-*) and home-redesign.md
(HR-*). It narrows SD-02 and SD-08. No contracts release (the appliance, relay and API use none of it, as ASG-16).

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|----------|-----|
| SST-01 | **A service declares the dozen settings that matter** (`KindModule.settings`, `things/kinds/settings.ts`): each with its group, name, one plain sentence, its value in words, and either a switch or a list of choices, and how to read it from the service and write it back. The page shows them on a tab of their own as `settings` panels (one card per group), and every switch and Change runs `things.action` with the kind's `setting` action (`target` the setting, `value` the new value). The assistant runs the very same action (SST-04). | One typed path for the person's tap and the assistant: the same checks, the same words, the same record; the page works without the AI. |
| SST-02 | **Done means checked**: the action reads the setting first, refuses a value that isn't one of its choices, writes it, then reads it back from the service. Only when the service reads back the new value does Whombat record “Changed” with Undo and say so (“Plex now allows up to 20 Mbps outside your home. Checked with Plex.”). If the service still reports the old value, it refuses (“Plex didn’t take the change. It still says 8 Mbps.”). If the read-back fails, drops the setting or shows some other unexpected value, Whombat records an uncertain outcome in plain words (“Asked Plex to set …”) with no “Changed” and no Undo, and does not mark a Put-it-back complete. Choosing what it already is says so and changes nothing. | A change the service quietly ignored or Whombat couldn't verify must never look done or offer Undo. |
| SST-03 | **Every declared change can be put back** for 7 days: it is recorded as a change of the thing (kind `thing.setting.<setting>`, title “Changed Quality outside your home in Plex from 8 Mbps to 20 Mbps”) with an undo recipe (`thing.action`: the action, the setting, the old value and the value it set). **Put it back** — in the toast, on the setting's row and in Activity's Undo — runs the same action as the person with the old value, and refuses in plain words when the setting was changed since (“Quality outside your home was changed again since, so Whombat won’t put it back.”). The row says who changed it last and when (“Dusty changed this yesterday from 4 Mbps.”, on a phone “Changed by Dusty yesterday”). Supersedes SD-08's “No Undo” for declared settings; other buttons keep it. | A person tries a setting when they can take it back with one tap. |
| SST-04 | **The assistant changes a declared setting through the same action**: it reads the page (`things.page`), whose settings tab lists each declared setting with its id, value and choices, then `settings_change` with `things.action {action: 'setting', target, value}`. That always asks on a card (`things.action` is an `ask` entry, ASG-06), and the card's words are Whombat's, from the kind (`describe`): “Change a Plex setting”, “Quality outside your home: 8 Mbps → 20 Mbps”, with the connection's label when they don't name it (“Change a Plex setting · Plex upstairs”, approved-connection-calls.md APC-05). After the yes it runs once as the person and the change records the assistant as the one who asked (`agentId`), so it also shows in Done recently with Undo. A setting that isn't declared goes through the connection's own request tool, which asks first for Plex's `/:/prefs` (unchanged). | The person approves exactly what runs, in words they read, and finds it again on the page. |
| SST-05 | **What stays in the service** (narrows SD-02): updating or upgrading it, deleting anything, accounts, sharing and sign-in, and anything that could lock the person out. Such a row shows its value and “In Plex” with a link to the service's own settings page; it has no switch or Change. Everything else SD-02 lists stays as it was. | Settings are now something a page may change, but only the reversible ones. |
| SST-06 | **The tab names**: the service's settings are a server-built tab after the Summary, named “{Service} settings” (key `service-settings`). The clients' own Settings tab (the connection's sign-in, Look only or Manage, the checks) is now **Connection**; its key stays `settings` so old links still open it. | Two tabs both called Settings would be a guess every time. |
| SST-07 | *In part superseded by SST-18: Remote access, Secure connections and Empty the trash stay in Plex.* **Plex's settings** (from `/:/prefs`, written with `PUT /:/prefs?<id>=<value>`): *Watching away from home* — Remote access (`PublishServerOnPlexOnlineKey`, a switch), Quality outside your home (`WanPerStreamMaxUploadRate`: Original quality, 20, 12, 8, 4, 2 Mbps), Secure connections (`secureConnections`: Required, Preferred); *Converting video* — Use the graphics chip (`HardwareAcceleratedCodecs`, shown only when Plex offers it), Conversion quality (`TranscoderQuality`: Automatic, Faster, Better picture), Conversions at once (`TranscodeCountLimit`: No limit, 1–4, 6, 8); *Libraries* — Scan when files change (`FSEventLibraryUpdatesEnabled`), Scan on a schedule (`ScheduledLibraryUpdatesEnabled` with `ScheduledLibraryUpdateInterval`: Off, every hour, 2, 6, 12 hours, every day), Empty the trash after a scan (`autoEmptyTrash`); *Upkeep* — Quiet hours for upkeep (`ButlerStartHour`/`ButlerEndHour`, pairs from 00:00 – 03:00 to 04:00 – 07:00), and Updates (In Plex, SST-05). “Secure connections: Off” isn't offered: it can only make Plex less safe. A setting Plex doesn't report is left out; when `/:/prefs` can't be read the tab says so with Open Plex. The tab starts with one line (“The settings that matter most, in plain words. Change one here, or ask {assistant} to do it for you.” · All settings in Plex) and ends with “Something else? {assistant} can change any other Plex setting for you. It asks you first.” · Ask {assistant}. | The dozen people actually look for; the names are what a person calls them, not Plex's ids. |
| SST-08 | **Ask about a card**: a panel may carry `ask` with one to three questions; its head gets a small **Ask** that opens “Ask {assistant} about {title}” with them as chips and “Or ask something else…”, each opening the Ask pane with that question. Plex's Now playing (why something converts, who watched most), Libraries and each settings group carry them. It is the only part of a page that needs the AI. | One click from any part of a page to the assistant, about that part. |
| SST-09 | **Changing a setting**: a switch flips at once (no question; flipping back and Put it back undo it). A setting with choices opens a sheet — a dialog on the web, a bottom sheet on the phone — with its name, a sentence, the choices with “Now” on the current one, a hint when Whombat knows one, and **Ask {assistant} instead** · Cancel · **Change it**. “Ask {assistant} instead” opens the Ask pane with “Help me choose {setting} for {service}.” | One way for every setting; the assistant stays one tap away for “I want the iPad to stop converting”. |
| SST-10 | **A service's verdict**: a page may carry one plain sentence under its name (`ThingPage.verdict`), like a server's (SPG-01). Plex's while it is fine: “Plex is fine: 2 people are watching and every library is up to date.” (or which library is being scanned); otherwise the page's own words from the same line as the badge: “Plex has a problem: …” or “Plex needs a look: No remote access.” (`verdictField`). | The first question is “is it all right?”, on a service's page too. |
| SST-11 | **What uses a server's space** (Storage): built in `@whombat/client/hosts` (`spaceUse`) from each machine's and app's own disk (`diskUsedGb`), biggest first, and — on a server with one volume — the rest of its used space as “Everything else”, under tiles in words (how full, when it fills up, disks to watch). When a recent on-request disk breakdown exists (measured within 24 hours by the hosts agent's `disk_usage` stream — through the assistant's `server_disk_usage` tool during a Look or chat, or on demand from the Storage tab on sessions at protocol minor 3 or later, `DISK_USAGE_MINOR = 3`), it replaces “Everything else” with the measured categories (container images, build cache, volumes, logs, system journal, package caches, VM disks and snapshots where known) and largest non-overlapping folders, together with when it was measured; disks that are spun down or whose sleep state cannot be confirmed are skipped and never woken. | Gives both the assistant and the person a real breakdown when a disk fills up without adding scheduled disk walks or waking sleeping drives. |
| SST-12 | **Generic `KindModule.settings` wiring**: `KindModule` carries an optional `settings?: KindSettings` built with `declaredSettings(declared)` (`things/kinds/settings.ts`). `connectionPage` inserts its `service-settings` tab right after Summary when the service was read, `actionFor` dispatches `setting` to `kind.settings.action`, and `describeThingAction` asks `kind.settings.describe` for the approval card's words. Plex moved onto it with no change in behaviour. | Adding settings to another service is one module and one field, with no duplicated tab, action or approval-card plumbing. |
| SST-13 | **Verdicts and Ask chips on core service pages**: Home Assistant, TrueNAS, Synology, Immich, Paperless-ngx, AdGuard Home, Pi-hole, Sonarr and Radarr each carry one calm plain verdict sentence (`ThingPage.verdict`) built from data the page already reads (naming real operational issues such as stuck downloads, paused blocking or failed jobs even when their checks start off) and two to three contextual Ask chips (`ThingPagePanel.ask`) on their key Summary cards. | The same instant “is it all right?” answer and one-click assistant entry Plex has, with no extra service calls. |
| SST-14 | *Superseded by SST-18.* **Declared settings for AdGuard Home, Pi-hole, Sonarr, Radarr and Immich**: each declares only everyday reversible settings that cannot cut off access, the network or delete stored data (SST-05), reading and writing full config objects where the service's API requires it so untouched fields (auth, upstream DNS, ports, storage templates) stay intact — AdGuard Home (`home/adguard-settings.ts`: block lists, update interval, safe browsing, parental control, query log on/off, anonymise IPs, statistics on/off; safe search engine selection, query-log retention and statistics retention stay in AdGuard Home), Pi-hole (`home/pihole-settings.ts`: blocking mode, DNSSEC, query log, privacy level via `/api/config/{dns,database,misc}` and `PATCH /api/config`, never `/api/config/webserver`; query-history retention stays in Pi-hole), Sonarr and Radarr (`media/arr-settings.ts`: rename on import, hardlinks, extra files, minimum free space, completed download handling, retry failed, unmonitor deleted, RSS sync interval via `/api/v3/config/{mediamanagement,naming,downloadclient,indexer}`, never `/api/v3/config/host`; Propers & Repacks stays in Sonarr/Radarr because replacing a file can permanently delete the old one), and Immich (`storage/immich-settings.ts`: video transcode mode and resolution, smart features, automatic database backups on/off, version checks via `/api/system-config` with 404 fallback to `/api/admin/config`, and a plain 403 non-admin message; trash retention and database-backup retention stay in Immich). OPNsense and UniFi Network keep no declared settings (lock-out risk). | Covers the common day-to-day settings safely across five more services while keeping lock-out and data-deleting retention settings in the service itself (Undo can restore a number, not deleted photos, backups or history). |
| SST-15 | *Superseded by SST-18.* **DNS validation and filtering switches stay in the service** (refines SST-05 and SST-14): Pi-hole's DNSSEC switch (`dnssec`, `home/pihole-settings.ts`) and AdGuard Home's DNS filtering switches (`filtering_enabled`, `safebrowsing`, `parental`, `home/adguard-settings.ts`) are read-only `elsewhere` rows (“In Pi-hole” / “In AdGuard Home”). Writable settings on Pi-hole are blocking mode for already-blocked domains, query log and privacy level; on AdGuard Home, block-list update interval (preserving `enabled`), query log, IP anonymisation and statistics; Sonarr, Radarr and Immich keep their existing writable settings (none touch auth, listeners, network or lookups). | Turning on DNSSEC against an upstream that lacks DNSSEC records, or enabling DNS block lists, custom rules or external filtering feeds that match Whombat’s own address, breaks name lookups after the service saves the flag — cutting the person off before they can reach Undo. |
| SST-16 | *Superseded by SST-18.* **No setting Whombat changes can delete, replace or stop keeping stored data** (refines SST-05, SST-14 and SST-15): any setting whose change — on, off or to another value, now or on the service's next run — can make the service delete, replace or stop keeping files, history, backups, photos, logs or monitoring state is a read-only `elsewhere` row (“In {Service}”). What stays writable across the five services is: **AdGuard Home** (`home/adguard-settings.ts`) — block-list update interval (`filter_update_interval`, preserving `enabled`; query log on/off, IP anonymisation and statistics on/off stay in AdGuard Home); **Pi-hole** (`home/pihole-settings.ts`) — blocking mode for already-blocked domains (`blocking_mode`; query log on/off and privacy level stay in Pi-hole); **Sonarr and Radarr** (`media/arr-settings.ts`) — rename files on import (`rename_files`) and RSS sync interval (`rss_sync_interval`, read-only “In Sonarr” / “In Radarr” when off at 0; completed download handling, retry failed downloads, unmonitor deleted items, minimum free space, hardlinks and import extra files stay in Sonarr/Radarr); **Immich** (`storage/immich-settings.ts`) — video conversion mode and resolution (`video_transcode`, `video_resolution`), smart features (`machine_learning`, `smart_search`, `face_recognition`, `duplicate_detection`) and version checks (`version_check`; automatic database backups on/off stays in Immich). | Undo restores only the setting value: it cannot bring back a replaced media file, dropped subtitles, unmonitored episodes or movies, skipped database backups or unrecorded lookup logs and statistics. |
| SST-17 | *Superseded by SST-18.* **A setting write never overwrites a concurrent change** (refines SST-05, SST-14, SST-15 and SST-16): use a field-only update wherever the service offers one (`PUT /:/prefs?<id>=<value>` on Plex, `PATCH /api/config` on Pi-hole), or a conditional write (`If-Match`, with `412` refused as a conflict in `actAs`) when supported. Where an API only takes a whole object, read only that section fresh right before writing (`readFresh`), read it again after writing and verify every untouched field (`untouched`) still matches what was read — returning an uncertain outcome in plain words (never “Changed”, no Undo) if any untouched field changed. Whenever a whole-object payload carries sign-in, access, network or data-keeping fields (so a lost concurrent write could flip them), keep every setting on that endpoint in the service (“In {Service}”). Exact split across all six services: **Plex** (`media/plex-settings.ts`, field-only) — `remote_access`, `remote_quality`, `secure_connections` (Required/Preferred only), `graphics_chip`, `conversion_quality`, `conversions_at_once`, `scan_on_change`, `scan_schedule` and `quiet_hours` stay writable; `empty_trash` (can drop a library) and `updates` (restarts/upgrades Plex) stay in Plex. **Pi-hole** (`home/pihole-settings.ts`, field-only `PATCH /api/config`) — `blocking_mode` stays writable; `dnssec` (can break lookups), `dns_and_dhcp` (upstream DNS, DHCP and sign-in), `query_logging`, `privacy_level` and `max_db_days` (stopping/shortening lookup history) stay in Pi-hole. **Sonarr and Radarr** (`media/arr-settings.ts`, whole-object `PUT` with fresh single-section read and untouched-field read-back check) — `rename_files` (`/api/v3/config/naming/{id}`) and `rss_sync_interval` (`/api/v3/config/indexer/{id}`, read-only when off at 0) stay writable because neither section carries sign-in, access, network or data-keeping fields; all `mediamanagement` rows (`hardlinks`, `import_extra_files`, `min_free_space`, `propers_and_repacks`, `unmonitor_deleted` — both for their own file/monitoring impact and because `/api/v3/config/mediamanagement` carries `recycleBin` and `recycleBinCleanupDays`), `downloadclient` rows (`completed_download_handling`, `auto_redownload_failed` — can replace/delete files on upgrades) and `general_and_updates` (`/api/v3/config/host` — auth, API key, ports, TLS, updates) stay in Sonarr/Radarr. **AdGuard Home** (`home/adguard-settings.ts`, all read-only “In AdGuard Home”) — `filter_update_interval` stays in AdGuard Home because `POST /control/filtering/config` always resends `enabled` (the DNS block-lists switch, so a concurrent DNS filtering change could be overwritten); `filtering_enabled`, `safebrowsing`, `parental` and `safesearch` (DNS filtering/engines), `querylog_enabled`, `querylog_interval`, `anonymize_ip`, `stats_enabled` and `stats_interval` (log/statistics keeping and retention) and `dns_and_access` (upstream DNS, encryption, sign-in) stay in AdGuard Home. **Immich** (`storage/immich-settings.ts`, all read-only “In Immich”) — `video_transcode` (setting it to Off or Only when needed can make Immich’s next “All videos” transcode job delete converted copies, and it writes the whole server config), `video_resolution`, `machine_learning`, `smart_search`, `face_recognition`, `duplicate_detection` and `version_check` (Immich only takes a full-object `PUT` to `/api/system-config` or `/api/admin/config` without conditional writes, carrying `passwordLogin`, `oauth`, `server`, `trash`, `backup` and `storageTemplate`), `trash_days`, `db_backup_enabled`, `db_backup_keep` (retention and backups) and `auth_and_users` (sign-in, storage and users) all stay in Immich. When all rows on a tab stay in the service, the tab’s intro says so instead of “Change one here”. | A read-modify-write of a whole config object without conditional writes silently reverts a concurrent change made in the service; if that object carries password login, OAuth, DNS filtering or a recycle bin, Whombat could turn sign-in or safety back off while reporting success. |
| SST-18 | **One rule for every setting Whombat changes** (supersedes SST-14…SST-17; narrows SST-05 and SST-07): Whombat changes a setting from its page or through the assistant (with read-back verification and 7-day Undo) only when all three hold — **(a)** the service accepts a single-field write or a conditional write (`If-Match`, `412` refused as a conflict in `actAs`): no whole-object or whole-section write; **(b)** changing it, now or on the service's next run, deletes or replaces nothing already stored (turning a log or statistic off is fine when what is already kept stays); **(c)** no value can cut the person off (name lookups, sign-in, the web interface, the network, or blocking Whombat's own address). Every setting that fails any test stays read-only (“In {Service}”), and when every row on a tab stays in the service the tab's intro says so. Result across the six services (OPNsense and UniFi Network keep no declared settings): **Plex** (`media/plex-settings.ts`, field-only `PUT /:/prefs?<id>=<value>`) — writable: `remote_quality`, `graphics_chip`, `conversion_quality`, `conversions_at_once`, `scan_on_change`, `scan_schedule`, `quiet_hours`; in Plex: `remote_access` (turning it off cuts off access from outside the home in one click), `secure_connections` (setting Required immediately refuses plain HTTP on port 32400, cutting off plain-HTTP LAN access and Whombat's default HTTP connection), `empty_trash` (can drop a library when a drive is briefly away), `updates` (restarts/upgrades Plex). **Pi-hole** (`home/pihole-settings.ts`, field-only `PATCH /api/config`, reading `/api/config/{dns,database,misc}`, never `/api/config/webserver`) — writable: `blocking_mode` (`dns.blocking.mode`), `query_logging` (`dns.queryLogging`, toggles writing future lookups to `pihole.log` while keeping stored logs and DB rows), `privacy_level` (`misc.privacylevel` 0–3, applies only to new lookups while keeping stored rows in `pihole-FTL.db`); in Pi-hole: `dnssec` (can break lookups against non-DNSSEC upstreams), `dns_and_dhcp` (upstream DNS, DHCP and sign-in), `max_db_days` (shortening purges older rows from `pihole-FTL.db`). **AdGuard Home** (`home/adguard-settings.ts`, reading `/control/{filtering,safebrowsing,parental,safesearch}/status`, `/control/querylog/config`, `/control/stats/config`) — writable: `safebrowsing` (`POST /control/safebrowsing/{enable,disable}`), `parental` (`POST /control/parental/{enable,disable}`), each an empty-body single-field toggle checked against AdGuard's phishing/malware or adult-domain hash-prefix feeds only, disabled for private rDNS and separate from user block lists/custom rules; in AdGuard Home: `filtering_enabled` and `filter_update_interval` (whole-section `POST /control/filtering/config`; block lists can also block Whombat), `safesearch` (whole-object `PUT /control/safesearch/settings`), `querylog_enabled`, `querylog_interval` and `anonymize_ip` (whole-section `PUT /control/querylog/config/update`; shortening deletes history), `stats_enabled` and `stats_interval` (whole-section `PUT /control/stats/config/update`; shortening deletes history), `dns_and_access` (upstream DNS, encryption, sign-in). **Sonarr and Radarr** (`media/arr-settings.ts`, reading `/api/v3/config/{mediamanagement,naming,downloadclient,indexer}`, never `/api/v3/config/host`) — all ten rows stay in Sonarr/Radarr (`rename_files`, `hardlinks`, `import_extra_files`, `min_free_space`, `propers_and_repacks`, `completed_download_handling`, `auto_redownload_failed`, `unmonitor_deleted`, `rss_sync_interval`, `general_and_updates`): every v3 config endpoint takes a whole-section `PUT` with no `PATCH` or conditional write, and media-management, download-handling and host sections also carry file-replacement, recycle-bin, auth and network controls. **Immich** (`storage/immich-settings.ts`, reading `/api/system-config` with 404 fallback to `/api/admin/config` and a plain 403 non-admin message) — all eleven rows stay in Immich (`video_transcode`, `video_resolution`, `machine_learning`, `smart_search`, `face_recognition`, `duplicate_detection`, `trash_days`, `db_backup_enabled`, `db_backup_keep`, `version_check`, `auth_and_users`): Immich only takes a full-object `PUT` with no conditional write, and video transcode, trash, backups and auth also carry deletion or sign-in impact. | One clear rule applied consistently across every service prevents silent concurrent overwrites, data loss and lock-outs without blocking safe single-field settings. |
