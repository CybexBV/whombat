# The improvement review's features, server side (RF-01…RF-15)

Stream RF of the 2026-10-07 improvement-review build (`docs/implementation/improvement-review/assignment.md` §4.5): the
runtime half of new features 4 (Stop an agent's turn) and 5 (deploy record and environments board), and the server
pieces of the review's UI items the web and Android streams build on — thread pages, thumbnails, read state across
devices, the needs-you count, a canvas as Markdown and search answers reused. Contracts 0.55.0 (CT-295…CT-308,
`contracts-0.55.md`); migration `0085_review_features`. RF-09 came later from stream MN (report items D7/D8).

| # | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RF-01 | **Superseded in part by one-person.md OP-S07, OP-S08**: the person may stop any turn, whoever it is for, and nobody is offboarded. **Superseded in part by HN-T01 (hermes-native.md)**: a stopped turn whose runner still holds it holds the agent's place in its thread, not the whole conversation; the rest holds. **Stop on an agent's turn, `dispatches.cancel`** (`runtime/stop.ts`). People only (an agent: 403), in the turn's conversation (anyone else: 404, as for no turn), who are the person the turn is for (`onBehalfOfAccountId` is their account), may wake that agent there (core's `canStopTurn`: a team agent, or a personal agent's owner — the retry rule) or own the burrow. A turn that already ended answers with how it ended; a run's turn answers 409 “Pause the run instead.” (`details.code: pause_run`). A queued turn is cancelled at once; a claimed or running one too (state `cancelled`, `cancel_reason` `cancelled`, `error` `stopped`, `stopped_by_id`), its runner stops at its next beat through the existing `ProgressResponse.cancel`. **Until the runner says the turn ended it still holds the agent's place in that conversation**: the claim's one-turn-per-(agent, conversation) rule counts a turn a person stopped whose lease hasn't run out, and the runner's completion of a stopped turn records what it used and the session it ran and ends the lease (`finalizeIn`). Other ways a turn is cancelled (offboarding, an assessment's end, a deleted thread) keep their behaviour. The thinking line clears (`RunnerRuntime.stopThinking`, HRS-04's holder rule), what the turn was doing is interrupted (WS-15), `dispatch.updated` and the answer say “Stopped by Joris” (`turnStoppedText`, worked out by the command that knows who; later frames of the row say “Stopped”), a hidden signed `dispatch.cancelled` (`System`, `{dispatchId, agentId}`) records it, a plan step's turn moves its step (WS-05). The Work view offers `stop_turn` on queued and running turn items to the same people (`mayStopTurn`). Thinking frames carry `typing.dispatchId` (enqueue, wakes, retries, the runner's beats). | “Stop, do this instead” is the common case: marking the turn cancelled at once and letting the next one claim beside it would start a second provider process on the same session while the first is still being killed, and would lose the stopped turn's usage (spend) and session (the next turn wouldn't see what it did). Holding the place until the runner reports (a beat, seconds) and then resuming keeps both. |
| RF-02 | **Superseded in part by one-person.md OP-S69**: a recorded deploy carries its conversation and message in every read and frame; the rest holds. **Recorded deploys** (`deployments/`, table `deployments`). `record_deploy` (no key; the turn's agent; the MCP adapter passes the turn) records the deployment with names stored trimmed and collapsed and grouped by `deployGroupKey` (lowercase keys in their own columns), the run named (only the agent's own, else 404) or the turn's (`dispatchRun`), `previousId` = the newest succeeded deploy of the same environment and service, the `deploy` card in the turn's thread (quiet: no mentions, no dispatch; its text `deployLine`, which older clients show), a signed `deploy.recorded` (`Code`, text `deployLine` without the sha, `where` “Deploys”, `ref` `work/deploys`; payload ids, names, status, how, sha — never the summary or URL) and `deployment.recorded` on the burrow topic. A turn that posts nowhere (a search answer, an assessment, a reflection) records without a card. Reads: `deployments.list` (newest first, environment/service filters by key, a keyset cursor over `(created_at, id)`, a foreign cursor 400) and `deployments.environments` (per group the newest row and the two newest successes, by window functions, through core's `deployEnvironmentsFrom`). `conversationId`/`messageId` only for members of that conversation, in reads and frames. | The board is burrow-wide, the room isn't: a private channel's deploy is on the board without saying where it was talked about. The pure builder from contracts keeps the runtime and the fixtures the same. |
| RF-03 | **Superseded by stack-optimization.md OPT-01.** Thread pages use an indexed keyset range; core's `pageThreadReplies` remains the fixture reference. | Reading every reply id made a small page grow with the entire thread. |
| RF-04 | **Thumbnails** (`files/thumbnails.ts`). The thumbnail is the content-addressed preview the session's `preview` variant already served (`files/previews.ts`), now at `THUMBNAIL_MAX_EDGE` (720, was 512), made by the image worker **when the upload is stored and before it is answered** (REST PUT, session upload, an agent's `upload_file`) for PNG and JPEG larger than 720 on their longest side and within the worker's budget; its size goes on the row (`attachments.thumbnail_width/height`, operational bookkeeping, no event: the upload's `attachment.uploaded` is the record). `GET …/attachments/:id/thumbnail`: `readableAttachment`'s rule, 404 without one, its own type, `private, max-age=31536000, immutable`, an etag of the source and the edge. It goes with its blob (`removePreviews` in `removeIfUnused`; the hourly sweep also drops previews of another edge). Images within the edge are their own thumbnail (none made), others none. | Making it before the answer means the message it goes on carries `thumbnailUrl` from its first frame (no `message.updated` later); one worker job per large image at upload is what every viewer would otherwise download in full. |
| RF-05 | **`notification.read` from the server's mark-read paths**: `notifications.markRead` (ids: those that changed, 200 a frame; all: `ids: null` with `before`) and a skill proposal's decision, which settles its notices for every decider; with the unread count after, through the outbox, nothing when nothing changed (`domain/notification-read.ts`). Reading a conversation or a thread and opening a push are the clients calling `notifications.markRead` for the rows about that place (whombat-app AP-704), so they publish through it; the server doesn't mark rows read on `conversations.markRead` or `push.resolve`. | One write path for inbox read state keeps the event exact; the clients already know which rows a read covers. |
| RF-06 | **`work.needsYou`** and bootstrap's `sidebar.counts.needsYou` are one function (`read/needs-you.ts`). | They must never disagree; the needs-you suite checks both at every step. |
| RF-07 | **`canvases.content`**: `readableCanvas`'s rule (404 outside the canvas's room), the Markdown `canvas_read` gives agents (`collab.readMarkdown`), `updatedAt` the canvas's. | The phone shows exactly what agents read. |
| RF-08 | **Search answers reused**: `search.ask` answers with the asker's answer to the same words — trimmed, whitespace collapsed, lowercase, compared in SQL (`sameSearchWords`) — that is `pending` or `ready` and younger than 24 hours (`SEARCH_ANSWER_REUSE_MS`), and queues nothing; a failed answer, one without a researcher, someone else's and an older one ask again. The search's answer on record and a cursor's pinned answer match the same way, with no age limit (as before). | Asking on Enter or the button (WE-10) twice, or with other capitals, shouldn't wake the researcher twice; a day-old answer may be stale, so asking again then asks. |
| RF-09 | **A read thread leaves the phone** (`clearReadThreads`, managed only): when `notifications.markRead` reads a person's notifications, each thread they were about (NT-15's `thread:<conversationId>:<rootId\|main>`) gets a content-free `clear` to that person's phones — only for groups with a row that was unread until then and nothing unread left there, once per group and newest read row per process (NT-16's guard), after the commit; the clear's kind is the newest read row's. | NT-16 for threads: reading on the web shouldn't leave the phone's shade full. A clear per read-marker move would spam control; a thread with something still unread keeps its line. |
| RF-10 | **Turns Stop doesn't apply to**: a Feedback assessment, an automation step, a search answer and a reflection (`stoppableTurn`) answer 409 and their thinking frames carry no `dispatchId`. | Their owner ends them (the report's flow, the automation run, the search screen); stopping one would leave that owner waiting. |
| RF-11 | **`record_deploy` in the prompt**: the contracts' description carries the rule; agents with Deploy at On or Ask read one more sentence on the deploy line (“Record every deploy you do with record_deploy.”, no new line, PE-08); a deploy card in a window reads `deployCardText`. The tool is served to team agents' turns, not to search answers or personal agents (`toolFitsTurn`, PE-05). Its served entry is about 1 840 characters (≈ 460 tokens at /4). | The tool-schema budget: paid on every call of every turn that is served it. |
| RF-12 | **The deploy job's record** (`deploy.record_job`, from `DeployExecutor.onWorkflowRun` after the approval completes): once per approval (a unique index), `byId` the person whose yes ran it (`decided_by_id`, else the requester), signed and its card posted by the requester where the approval card is (its thread, or the top), `how` `github_actions`, `ref` from the action, `sha` only when the action named one (7–64 hex), `url` null, the summary naming the workflow and its run on GitHub. A failure to record never fails the poll. | The run's page is evidence, not “where people can see it”; the agent that asked is who says it in the room, the person who said yes is who deployed. |
| RF-13 | **Superseded by one-person.md OP-S69**: a recorded deploy's frame carries its conversation and message, with no per-recipient fill. **`deployment.recorded` is published without its place** and the hub (and the managed event log, both through `personalizeFrames`) fills `conversationId`/`messageId` in for that conversation's members from the row. | Fail closed: a frame that misses the personalizer never names a private room. |

At merge the journal gets 0084 then 0085. |
