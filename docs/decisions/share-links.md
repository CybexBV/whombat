# Shared links (SL-*)

John, 2026-11-05, after a look at ChatGPT's shared links: build it for Whombat, as an encrypted copy rather than a page
on Whombat's servers; a link works for **7 days** unless the person picks otherwise, **no title in link previews**,
**readers can't reply**. Everything else as proposed: share a thread or from one message on, a check before sharing,
expiry and how often it was opened, one list to stop them, the assistant only on the person's yes, never in an
outsiders' turn.

Related: [feedback-drop.md](feedback-drop.md) (the same shape the other way round: control keeps only ciphertext),
[one-person.md](one-person.md) OP-01 (nobody else signs in), managed-providers.md PV-11 (no plaintext customer content
in cloud ingress), [outsiders-turns.md](outsiders-turns.md), contracts CT-1720…CT-1729
(`docs/contracts/decisions/contracts.md`), control (control: the table, the device ops, the public read, CL-…),
the appliance (the manager's three routes, MG-…).

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SHL-01 | **A link is a sealed copy, never a live view.** The box builds a `SharedSnapshot` of the thread as it is now, seals it with a fresh random 32-byte key (XChaCha20-Poly1305, the share id as associated data, `WBS1` + nonce + ciphertext; `@whombat/core/share`) and hands control only the sealed bytes. The key is the link's fragment: `https://<hosted app>/s/<shr_…>#<key>`. | Browsers never send the fragment, so control, the relay and anyone reading control's database can't read a shared thread (PV-11 holds). A copy works while the box is off. |
| SHL-02 | **What a copy shows**: the person's and the assistant's messages of a thread of the person's **Chat** (never the Feedback room: outsiders' reports aren't the person's to pass on), from its first message or from one of its messages on; their words; pictures (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF up to 4 MB each, 24 at most) unless the person turns them off; the assistant's HTML pages and charts. Never cards (approvals, plans, runs), “More detail”, link previews, other files, deleted messages, messages with outsiders' provenance or Whombat's own lines. Commands and their output aren't in messages at all. At most 400 messages; the sealed copy at most 8 MiB. | Only what the person sees as the conversation; what a turn did stays in Whombat. |
| SHL-03 | **Have a look first.** The Share sheet counts what the copy holds and what is left out, and points out network addresses (IPv4, IPv6, `.lan`/`.local`/`.home`/`.internal`/`ts.net` names), email addresses and things that look like a key or a password (known token prefixes, private key blocks, `password: …`), with the first one found (a secret cut to four characters) and how many messages have it. Whombat never changes the words itself. | A Whombat chat is full of the person's servers and accounts; pointing it out is honest, silently redacting would change what the person meant to share. |
| SHL-04 | **A link works for 7 days** unless the person picks 1 day, 30 days or no end; control deletes the copy when it runs out. | John's choice; ChatGPT's links never end, which is how old links leak. |
| SHL-05 | **Control's view of a link is an id, a size, an expiry and a count.** `share.put` (the same id replaces the copy and keeps its count), `share.delete`, `share.views`, signed by the manager; `GET /v1/shares/<id>` without sign-in answers the bytes with `no-store`, `noindex`, `no-referrer`, CORS for the hosted app only, or 404 for unknown, stopped and expired alike, and counts each read. At most 500 working links per Whombat. | Nothing to index, nothing to preview: a link pasted in a chat shows only Whombat's generic card (no title, John's choice). |
| SHL-06 | **The viewer page is the hosted app's `/s/<id>`**, its own small page: it loads before and without anything of the app or a session, fetches the copy, opens it in the browser and draws it read-only with the app's message parts (rich text, pages in the sandboxed frame). It says plainly when the link is incomplete, has stopped or doesn't open. No reply box. | Readers can't reply (John's choice); the page needs no account and talks to no Whombat. |
| SHL-07 | **The link's row is the box's, through the pipeline**: `share_create` keeps the id, the key sealed with the box's custody key (`share:<id>:key`, so the person can copy the link again) and what it shows; the call to control happens after that commit; `share_published` records control's viewer URL with a signed `share.created` event (ids and counts, never the thread's words); `share_discard` drops a row whose copy never got there. Stopping marks the row `stopped` and `stop_pending` (`share.stopped`) until control deleted the copy (`share_stop_done`). | The call to control can't sit in a transaction; a link never shows as working before control has it, and a stop is never lost. |
| SHL-08 | **The share round**, every 5 minutes: deletes again at control what was stopped here but not there; stops links whose thread or chat is gone (no foreign key on purpose, so a deleted thread's row stays until its copy is gone); drops a row whose copy never got to control after 10 minutes (deleting it at control too, in case it did). | Deleting a thread stops its links, as ChatGPT's do; nothing is left working because the box restarted between two steps. |
| SHL-09 | **The assistant asks, the person says yes.** `share_link` (personal turns only; not in an automation's step or a brief; never served to an outsiders' turn) runs `share_create` as the assistant, whose key `share.link` is capped at Ask and forced to Ask whatever its grants say: a card says how many messages, whether pictures, until when, and that it can be stopped in Settings. On yes the link is made after the commit and the assistant is woken with it (`approval_granted`) to give it as it is; if it can't be woken, the link is posted as one line. | A link reaches people outside the Whombat; a prompt-injected turn must not be able to publish anything by itself. |
| SHL-10 | **Where people find it**: Share in a Chat thread's head and “Share…”/“Share from here…” in a message's menu (web and phone), on a box only (a link needs Whombat's servers); the link with Copy (and the system's share sheet on the phone); Settings → You → **Shared links**: each link with how long it works and how often it was opened, Copy, Stop sharing, and Stop all links. On a Whombat without the manager's share routes (development, the community edition) making a link says “Sharing needs Whombat’s servers, and this Whombat can’t use them.” | One place to stop them, as ChatGPT's Data controls, but with the count and the end date. |
