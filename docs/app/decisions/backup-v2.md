# Backups v2 in the apps: the passkey is the key, bringing back, honest status (AP-6880…AP-6939)

John, 2026-11-04, after a review of backup and restore: “The passkey is the key.” The backups page answers “am I safe?”
honestly (failing, behind, running, just now), being able to bring the Whombat back is part of being backed up, the old
recovery kit screens leave the main path, bringing back is passkey first, and the words are plain. These are the
screens on the web and the phone (Whombat [backup-passkey.md](../../decisions/backup-passkey.md) BPK-01…BPK-14,
[backup-options.md](../../decisions/backup-options.md) BKO-15…BKO-35; the wiring is
[backup-passkey.md](backup-passkey.md) AP-6865…AP-6870). Words and views are `@whombat/client/managed`
`backup-options.ts` (`BACKUP_OPTIONS_TEXT`, `backupHero`, `backupContents`, `backupDetailLines`,
`bringBackLaterOffered`, `backupPasskeyAsked`) and `kit-passkey.ts` (`paperCopyText`, `tapPasskeyForBackups`). There is
no mock for these screens: they follow the existing Backups part and the bring-back screens.
