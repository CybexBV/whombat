# Dashboards on the web (2026-10-18)

Stream W of Whombat `dashboards.md` (DS-01…DS-19): one Summary for every server, connection and device, drawn by one
renderer from the same `ThingPagePanel`s (contracts 0.89.0, CT-394…CT-398) — a connection's or device's built by the
server (`things.page?range=`), a server's built in `@whombat/client` (`serverDashboard`, stream K,
`dashboards-client.md` AP-2000…). The mocks John approved are `docs/design-handoff/dashboards/` (`server.png`, `ha.png`,
`hetzner.png`, `unraid.png`, `anatomy.png`). The phone is stream M's (`dashboards-mobile.md`, AP-2120…).

| Id | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-2050 | **One renderer, `features/things/dashboard/`**: `Dashboard` (layout through K's `placePanels`), `Panels` (one component per panel kind, the old `page/Panels.tsx` grown), `ChartCard`, `Spark`/`Meter`, `RangePicker`, `PageHead`/`TabRow`, `ServerSummary`. Every tab a connection's server sends is drawn by it (a 0.88 page too: its panels are placed from their widths). `page/Panels.tsx` and `page/PanelChart.tsx` are gone. | DS-01, DS-02: one look for every Summary, no per-kind page work. |
| AP-2051 | **Rows and columns.** A chart row of three cards shares the width evenly; a wide card beside a narrow one takes **1.85fr · 1fr**, the main and side columns' share, so its edge lines up with the main column under it (the mock's Network beside Disk I/O); a row that isn't full fills the width by its cards' spans. The cards of a row end together (the plot grows into what the card leaves it, at least 150 px, 140 px in a wide card). The renderer follows its own width (a size container), not the window's: under 1000 px the side column goes under the main one and chart rows hold two cards (a wide card or one left alone takes both columns), under 640 px one. A column whose every card had nothing to say (what Whombat changed, before it changed anything) is dropped and the other takes the width. | DS-04 and §2 of the brief; no holes, no stretched card beside a long list. |
| AP-2052 | **A chart card's legend shows only for two or more lines without a figure** (Network, Disk I/O, Errors in the log); a card with a figure (Temperatures) has none, as the mock draws it — the tooltip names every line, the foot names the sensors. A 0.88 card's `meta` sits after its title. | The mock wins on layout; a figure and a legend together crowd a third of the width. |
| AP-2053 | **One chart engine**: `components/charts/TimeChart` grew stacked bars per point, a forecast (a dashed line from the newest point to `forecast.value`, the time axis running on to `forecast.at`), limit words at the line's start (the mock's “85 °C limit”), each line's own words in the tooltip, its own padding and soft gridlines (`--line`). The connection page no longer uses `LineChart`. The card's model (`dashboard/chartModel.ts`, unit-tested) lives in the web: colours through `thingSeriesToken`, the left axis's three gridlines from the card's fixed ends and unit or round numbers over what is drawn (stacked tops for bars, the limit and the forecast included), a 0.88 card's second unit mapped onto the left scale with its words on the right, its wash under the first line; values in DS-15's words (one decimal under 10, none above, thousands separated). | §2 of the brief (“TimeChart grown … retire LineChart”). The phone draws its own charts; K's client has the axis rules of 0.88 (`thingChartModel`) without `axis`, bars or forecast, so the web's model does them rather than change K's package mid-build. |
| AP-2054 | **The time axis's words fall on round times in the person's zone** — quarter hours for an hour, hours for six, every six hours for a day, days for a week, weeks for a month — the newest point “Now”, and only what fits (`fitTimeTicks`). The words follow the timestamps' own span, not the page's range: the 30-day disk and database cards on a 24-hour page speak in days, and a forecast adds its dates after “Now”. | The mock's “Sat 12:00 · Sun 00:00 · Now”; quarter-of-the-range ticks gave “Sat 14:20”. |
| AP-2055 | **Figures** are one card of 3–6 cells divided by lines (drawn as shadows, so they stay right whatever wraps): five a row down to a 760 px card, three below, two under 560 px. Each: the label face, the value at 24 px with its rest, the line under it, then a 30 px small line of the range scaled between its own lowest and highest value (the mock's) or a 5 px meter; a warn or bad tone colours the value and the line. Small lines are named for screen readers by what they show (“CPU over the last 24 hours: between 1.2 and 4.2”), meters by their share (“Integrations: 98 %”). | DS-07; accessibility rule (labels for small lines). |
| AP-2056 | **Worth a look shows five** and folds the rest behind “Show N more”, a real button with `aria-expanded` that opens them in place and then says “Show fewer”. | DS-05. |
| AP-2057 | **Table cells**: a cell's `note` sits under its words (the mock's “CT 131” under the guest, “4 vCPU · 8 GB · 80 GB” under CX32); a small bar is 54 px before the figure; a small line is 150 px × 24 px, named by its column; a button cell is a small outline link (“Console”, “Open”) whose column has no visible head (a screen-reader word) and never drops as the card narrows — the other columns drop from the last back as before (AP-1754). | DS-08, CT-396. |
| AP-2058 | **List lines** carry a dot in their tone's colour, named for screen readers (“Needs a look”), or the item's icon when it has one; the right-hand words are mono. | ha.png's Backups and “Integrations not loading”. |
| AP-2059 | **A text panel without a title is a one-line note** with an info mark and its way out on the right (hetzner.png's “Hetzner can’t see memory or disk space … Add as a server”), its own card in the main column rather than inside the Servers card. | The contract has no note inside a table; a card of its own keeps the panels independent. |
| AP-2060 | **One `PageHead` for servers and connections**: the crumb, the icon in its 38 px box (plain for a server, the blue tint for a service, the warm one for a cloud), the name (a server's in the mono face), one badge, the builder's chips (a server's from `serverDashboardHead`; a connection's page has no chips in 0.89 — its facts carry the version), Ask {assistant}, then the kind's buttons as before: **Shell** and **Power** (a menu) for a server, **Restart** and **Open <service>** for a connection. No “···” menu. | The mock's “Console” for the host's shell would collide with the guests' “Console” (their screen); Power already folds its two serious actions into a menu. |
| AP-2061 | **A connection's fact line**: what it runs on first (“Runs in **CT 131** on **pve-02**”, the guest's and the server's pages as two links, “Change” after them), then the server's facts, the address pill before the last one (“Checked 2 min ago”), as ha.png orders them. A server's: its facts, the address pill, when the agent synced, “Also connected”. | ha.png, server.png. |
| AP-2062 | **The range** lives in the address (`?range=7d`; 24 hours is the bare address) and is offered on the Summary tab only, at the tab row's right end. A connection's page reads `things.page?range=` (only on a runtime with `things.dashboards`, and only when the page offers two or more `ranges`), keeps the old page on screen while the new range is read, and reads again every `refreshS`. A server's offers `SERVER_DASHBOARD_RANGES` with “Live · every 10 s” before them (left out on a narrow page). Without `things.dashboards`: no picker, one range, the page placed from its widths. | DS-03, DS-16, DS-17. |
| AP-2063 | **Every server kind's Summary is `ServerSummary`** (`useServerDashboard` → `Dashboard`); `tabs/SummaryTab.tsx`, `tabs/HintsCard.tsx`, `unraid/UnraidSummary.tsx` and the Incus server's summary are gone, `ServerPage` draws one tab row for every kind (Incus's tabs became `IncusPanel`, its row the page's), with counts after Guests and Storage. **`libvirt/LibvirtSummary.tsx` stays**: it is a libvirt VM's Summary (the guest page), not a server's. | DS-09, DS-17; the brief listed LibvirtSummary by mistake (K confirmed). |
| AP-2064 | **Unraid's updates (Unraid itself, plugins, containers) and its unread notifications move to the System tab** (`unraid/UnraidSystemCards.tsx`), before the System tab's own cards; the Summary's notices link there. | They lived only on the old Summary; “the Summary shows, the tabs do” (DS-09). |
| AP-2065 | **The web's `things.page` fixtures are 0.89 pages for every range** on the range's grid (1 h and 6 h at 5 minutes, 24 h at 15, 7 d at 2 h, 30 d at 6 h): Home Assistant as ha.png (figures, CPU · Memory · Disk space with its forecast · Errors in the log as bars · Database, the integrations not loading, backups, nine notices), Hetzner as hetzner.png, UniFi (DS-13, with the container it runs in), the generic page (DS-14), the pool (a chart card per reading). Hetzner keeps the world's name “Hetzner” (the sidebar's), and its whombat-prod isn't one of the world's servers, so it has the note and no Open button (the mock showed both). | Fixtures speak like the server will; one world, one name. |
| AP-2066 | **John's servers in fixture mode** come from K's `dashboardFixtures` through `withDashboardFixtures` when `VITE_FIXTURE_DASHBOARDS=1` (option `dashboards`): pve-02 and blackbox as the mocks draw them, the-titan (Incus), kvm-lab (QEMU/KVM), the-falcon and raspi (plain Linux) by their address. Off (the default, and the tests), the world's five servers as before, which the dashboard builds its panels from just the same. | The mocks' servers for the visual check without changing the world other screens and tests use. |
