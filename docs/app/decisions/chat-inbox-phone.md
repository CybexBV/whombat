# Chat as an inbox: the phone (2026-11-14)

The phone's side (Android and iPhone, one app) of Whombat `docs/decisions/chat-inbox.md` (CIB-01…CIB-13), against the
mockup John approved (the phone in `chat-inbox.html`). Where the mockup and the decisions differ, the decisions win: no
“Later”, no day headings in the list, the filter reads “Waiting”, the person is “You”, the assistant's own name. The
words are `INBOX_WORDS` (`@whombat/client`), verbatim.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-9330 | **Chat and Feedback are a list of their open threads** (`ChatInbox`): `chats.threads` with `state: open`, newest activity first, thirty a page, the next page as the list nears its end; pull to refresh. The room's messages aren't loaded and its read marker no longer moves: a thread is read where it is read, in the thread. The box (“Ask Neo anything”) stays at the bottom above the tab bar; what it sends opens its thread as before. With two pages Chat's list keeps New chat in place of the box. | CIB-01; marking the room read on sight would make a thread the assistant started look read before anyone opened it. |
| AP-9331 | Superseded by AP-9372…AP-9375. **A row is the mockup's phone row**: at least 64dp, the whole row one button, a hairline under it, no face, preview, file, card or button. Line one: the title (14, one line, cut with “…”) and the time (DM Mono 11.5). Line two: a status chip, then “Who: line” (who at 600; 13, one line) and, when something is new, the count. Something new: an 8dp `chart1` dot in a 16dp column at the left, the title 700 in `ink`, the time 500 in `chart1`, the line in `ink2`, the count a `chart1` pill (18dp, at least 20 wide, its number 11/700 in `onSolid`, “99+” past 99). | AP-9333 | Superseded by AP-9371. **The filters: All · Unread n · Waiting n**, a segmented bar under the header (the mockup's: `sf2` track, 9 round, the chosen one on `sf` with a hairline and 600 `ink`, the others `mut`), each 32dp padded to 44. ## A New chat button in place of the list's box (2026-11-17)

John, 2026-11-17, on the list above: “In the thread list there should be a button for a new thread, instead of a
direct chat input which creates a new chat.” Against the mockup he approved (`chat-new-button.html`). Code:
`ui/newChatButton.tsx`, `screens/ChatInbox.tsx`, `screens/ChatRoomScreen.tsx` (`NewChatPage`,
`NewChatScreen`), route `NewChat`. The web has the same button (`chat-inbox-web.md` AP-9386…AP-9388).

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-9380 | **Chat's and Feedback's lists have no message box.** One round button floats over the list's lower right corner: 56dp, `btn` with the pen in a square (24, `btnfg`), the toast's shadow, 16 from the right and 16 above what covers the screen's bottom (our tab bar, iOS's, the window's edge). No words on it; a screen reader hears “New chat” (Feedback's: “New thread”). The rows scroll under it and the list ends 88dp lower, so the last row scrolls clear. The fade over the box and the wide New chat bar with two pages go. | A list is for picking; a box that looks like a reply box but starts a thread is one more thing to read on a screen already full of words. The corner is where a thumb is, and the top row is full. One button, not a bar, gives the list the box's room: about one more thread on the first screen. |
| AP-9381 | **The button opens the room's new chat with the cursor in its box**, the keyboard up: a header with the way back and “New chat” (“New thread” in Feedback), the assistant's face and “Ask Dusty anything. Each question gets its own thread.”, and the room's own box (what answers, “+”, voice typing). What was typed and not sent stays in the box for the next time, as the list's box kept it. | The box the person knows, one tap away. |
| AP-9382 | **On one page the new chat is a route** (`NewChat`, `room` for Feedback's), and once its message is sent the thread takes its place, so Back from the thread goes to the list. It isn't restored when the app starts again and has no link. With two pages Chat's button does what its bar did: it closes what the right page shows and wakes the new chat there (AP-6301), and is marked selected for a screen reader while that shows; Feedback, itself on the right page, opens its new chat over it. | A thread should never have an empty “New chat” page behind it. Opening the fold with a new chat open shows it on the right page, with the same draft. |
