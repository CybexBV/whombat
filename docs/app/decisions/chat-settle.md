## Web, beyond the spec (stream W)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-6160 | **“Settled {when}”** is core's `formatUpdated` with “on” before a day or a date: “Settled 2 hours ago”, “Settled yesterday”, “Settled on Monday”, “Settled last week”, “Settled on 3 Mar” (`settledLine`). | The mock's “Settled yesterday” and “Settled 2 hours ago”; a bare weekday or date doesn't read as a sentence. |
| AP-6161 | **“after {n} quiet days”** says the person's current `settleQuietDays`, or 3 when it is off now. | `ThreadSettle` doesn't carry the days it waited; the choice in force is the best guess. |
| AP-6162 | **A Needs you row says “Waiting for your answer”** in the warn tone, not the mock's “Waiting for your yes”. | `waiting` covers questions and key requests too; a yes is only one of them. |
| AP-6163 | *Superseded by AP-6252.* **The Settled shelf shows once `settledCount` is above 0**; its count comes from the open list's first page and moves by one at once with a settle or Open again. An older Whombat sends no count and shows no shelf. | An empty shelf is noise; one count shown in one place. |
| AP-6164 | **“Thread settled.” shows at once**, with Undo, as the room changes at once; a refusal takes the toast back and shows the server's words instead. Without words: “Couldn’t settle the thread. Try again.” / “Couldn’t open the thread again. Try again.” | The toast belongs to what the person sees, not to the round trip. |
| AP-6165 | **Who may settle** is read from the open threads' first page (`state: open`, one cache for the room, the thread and Threads): nothing offers Settle before it answers; a thread not on that page counts as open and free (the server still refuses when not). | Working and waiting threads are recent; asking per thread would cost a read per row. |

| AP-6167 | *Superseded by AP-6252.* **Threads' ✓ and ↶ keep their 28px slot** on every row that has one, shown on hover or keyboard focus; rows that can't settle have no slot. | No row jumps when the pointer moves over it. |
| AP-6168 | **The open thread's Settle sits at the head's right, before ×** (the mock's place), not straight after the chip. | The mock draws it there; the head's actions stay together. |
| AP-6169 | **“Settle finished threads” is the menu's last item**, after Clear chats under the same line, not in the bad tone. | AP-6155 says after Clear chats; settling removes nothing. |
| AP-6170 | **The shelf's “Show more”** loads the next 10 (the spec's words, not the mock's “Show 11 more”). | The count of what's left changes as threads settle and open. |

chats … to archive. This should be implemented in both mobile apps.” Then: “Swipe left should say ‘Settle’ instead of
done.” Archiving a thread is settling it here; the room is the list of open threads (AP-6251), so its messages swipe.
Phone only (iPhone and Android, one app); no contract or server change.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
