## A retired Whombat (AP-693…AP-698)

DEV, 2026-10-04: John's organization had one Whombat, and the operator retired its device at control
(`replaced_by_new_appliance`) because John is installing a new appliance. Refreshing the hosted app at `/` then showed
only “You’re offline. We’ll reconnect when you’re back.” for good: `/` reopens this browser's last-used Whombat without
asking the directory (AP-64), control refuses that Whombat's tickets, and the client took every refusal but
`signed_out`/`forbidden` for a network loss and retried for ever. Control's directory (`devices.list`) does list the
Whombat with `state: 'retired'`, but nothing in the app treated `retired` as anything but a Whombat to open.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-693 | **Control's ticket refusal, told apart by the directory (client).** control refuses `tickets.client` with 404 `not_found` + `details.code: device_not_found` for a retired Whombat (`tickets/client.ts`), for one that isn't this organization's, and for one whose claim the appliance hasn't reported yet (Whombat `trust.ts` reports `claim.claimed` right after the ceremony without waiting, then every 5 minutes until control takes it). `controlTickets(control, {orgId, device})` (`@whombat/client/managed`, used by the web and Android connectors and the fake world): `signed_out`, `forbidden`, and `not_found` with `org_not_found` → `TicketDeniedError` (`ticket_denied`: “not a member”, as its doc always said); any other `not_found` asks `devices.list` once: listed `retired` → `ApplianceRefusedError('appliance_retired')`, terminal; anything else (not listed, listed in use, the list unreadable) → `appliance_unlisted`, retried with the usual backoff, and `appliance_gone`, terminal, once it has been unlisted for 10 minutes in this session (`UNLISTED_GIVE_UP_MS`; a ticket in between starts the time again). Control unreachable, 429 or 5xx is rethrown and stays `offline`. `ControlError.reason` keeps control's precise reason (`details.code`). `TERMINAL_REASONS` gain `appliance_retired` and `appliance_gone`, so `reconnectNow` (online, visible again) doesn't revive them.
