# Found on your network: connected ones left out, hide a found service (AP-871…AP-876)

John, 2026-10-08, looking at Settings → Connections → FOUND ON YOUR NETWORK on his box: “Devices that are already
connected shouldn't be discovered again, furthermore I would like to have the ability to hide a discovered device.” The
list showed his connected services as rows with a green “Connected” dot and View, between the new ones with Connect.
Whombat `docs/decisions/connections-setup.md` CS-52 and CS-53 have the rules: a connected service is left out of the list
(CS-52), and a manager hides a found service for the whole burrow, kept across scans and restarts (CS-53). Contracts
0.63.0 (CT-316) carry `DiscoveredService.hidden`, `connections.hideFound` and the runtime feature
`connections.hide_found`.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-871 | **Contracts 0.63.0.** The vendored contracts move to 0.63.0 (`SESSION_API_LEVEL` 37, window 35…38), so the app's minor goes to 0.53.0. `FEATURE_NAMES` names `connections.hide_found` (“hiding a service found on your network”); `useConnectionWrite` takes `connections.hideFound` (it changes nothing cached: the answer is the scan, which only Found on your network holds). Fixtures and tests say `hidden: false` where they build a found service. | The level bump is what makes it a minor; the update line needs a name for the feature like every other one. |
| AP-872 | **A connected service is left out (CS-52).** `foundList` (`setup.ts`, replacing `foundRows`) skips every found service with `connectionIds` — since 0.63 the caller's connections of that entry on that host, or a Custom API on that host and port; from an older runtime whatever it named — and counts them. The row's “Connected” dot and View, and the overview's set of connections the viewer can open (`visible`), are gone. A done scan that lists nothing new says why, verbatim: nothing it knows → “Nothing Whombat knows how to connect turned up on your network.”; all of it connected → “Everything Whombat found on your network is connected already.”; all of it connected or hidden, at least one hidden → “Everything Whombat found on your network is connected or hidden.” A service that is connected and hidden counts as connected. The connect page offers no found addresses (it only takes `&url=` from a row), so nothing else lists them. | The list is for what is still to do; the connected ones are on the CONNECTED card above it. Saying which of the three it is keeps an empty card from looking like a scan that failed. |
| AP-873 | **Hide on each row (CS-53).** Where the runtime has `connections.hide_found`, a ghost `sm` **Hide** sits left of the primary Connect (`aria-label` “Hide Proxmox VE at https://192.168.1.10:8006”). It calls `connections.hideFound` `{catalogId, url}` through `useConnectionWrite` and puts the answer (the scan as it is now) on screen; the row's button is disabled while it runs. Scans, polls and hides share one request count: `latest` (the newest started) and `shown` (the one whose answer is on screen); an answer older than the one shown is dropped, so a poll that was on its way can't bring a hidden row back, and an older answer that arrives first still keeps the 1.5 s polling going. A failure says, in the card's line style and as an alert, “Couldn’t hide that just now. Try again in a moment.” (Unhide: “Couldn’t show that again just now. Try again in a moment.”). When the pressed button vanishes with its row, focus goes to the footer's “Show 1 hidden”. | Hide is the quieter of the two actions (a ghost beside the primary); hiding is for the whole burrow, so it is a server write, not a local preference. The scan's own answer avoids a second request and keeps one source of truth. |
| AP-874 | **The hidden ones, behind the footer's toggle.** Not listed by default. When the answer has hidden ones that aren't connected, the footer's right side has a ghost `sm` toggle with `aria-expanded`: “Show 2 hidden” / “Hide 2 hidden” (`1 hidden` for one), its text lined up with the rows' buttons and the footer keeping its height. The footer shows whenever there are hidden ones, also while a scan runs (then with just the toggle). Expanded, the hidden rows follow the others in the same list, name and address in `--mut`, with one secondary `sm` **Unhide** (“Unhide Portainer at https://192.168.1.30:9443”) instead of Hide and Connect; after Unhide focus goes to that row's Hide. When the last hidden one is shown again the toggle goes and the list closes. A line of the card's above the rows (the empty-state line, a hiccup) gets the rows' divider under it. | The app's paired Show X / Hide X pattern; a hidden service stays reachable in the place it was hidden from, without a separate screen or a settings list. Muted text (not opacity) says “set aside” while staying readable. |
| AP-875 | **Fixtures and what is not covered.** `VITE_FIXTURE_CONNECTORS=connected`: the scan finds Proxmox VE, Home Assistant (connected: the seeded connection is the one at 192.168.1.20), Portainer and Grafana; a found service is connected when a connection of its entry has its host, so connecting one from the list leaves it out of the next scan; `connections.hideFound` keeps a hidden set (across scans) and answers the scan with `hidden` set; a scan running again keeps what the last one found until it is done. Unit tests for `foundList`, the empty lines, the toggle and the trouble lines; a fixture-app test for hide → “Show 1 hidden” → Unhide (with the focus) and for everything hidden. Verified visually on the web at 1440×900, light and dark (the list with Hide, hidden collapsed and expanded, the empty line with the toggle, a scan running with only the toggle, the focus rings). Not done: no screenshot of the failure line (the fixtures never refuse a hide), and no Android: it has no Found on your network. | DEV is fast; the risky part, the request order between polls and hides, is covered by the fixture-app test and by the rule being a plain comparison. |
| AP-876 | **Released as whombat-app 0.53.0** (minor: contracts 0.63.0 raises the session API level): version bump, tag `app-v0.53.0`, `pnpm release:dist` from the tag. | Like 0.52.0. |
