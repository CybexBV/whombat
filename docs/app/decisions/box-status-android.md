# How the box is doing, on Android (AP-1150…AP-1156)

John, 2026-10-11, approving the mockup of a “How it’s doing” section for This Whombat (Whombat box-status.md BS-01…BS-12;
contracts 0.78.0, CT-334). BS-12 is the phone's part: the same section on Android's This Whombat screen, the four tiles in
a 2 × 2 grid, then one chart at a time with the range control and a chart switcher, polling only while the screen is
visible. The data, the poller and every word come from the shared client (`@whombat/client` `host/`, AP-1135, AP-1136)
and the contracts' copy (`HOST_STATUS_COPY`, `HOST_RANGE_LABELS`); the web's rows are in `box-status.md`.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-1150 | **This Whombat starts with “How it’s doing” for owners** (`appliance.status` `owner`) on a Whombat with the `host.metrics` feature, under the update card and above VERSION (`HostStatusSection`, `managed/HostStatus.tsx`). Its parts follow the poll state (`hostSectionKind`): the box (tiles, range, charts); `not_managed` (no manager) only the Tokens card; `unavailable` the contracts' sentence (“The Whombat can’t read its measurements right now.”) and the Tokens card; before the first answer the tiles' and chart's frames as skeletons. A self-hosted server's phone has no This Whombat screen (its settings sheet's group shows the burrow and the server), so it gets no section; its spend stays on the Spend screen. | BS-01: a first section of This Whombat, owners only; the parts degrade by `reason` (BS-06). |
| AP-1151 | **Live only while This Whombat is in front and the app in the foreground** (`useHostStatus` / `useHostPoller`, `managed/useHostStatus.ts`): the shared `createHostPoller` over this session's `host.metrics` (`hostMetricsLoader`) and the burrow's `spend.daily` and `spend.overview` (asked together, each allowed to fail on its own), `setActive(focused && foreground)` from React Navigation's `useIsFocused` and `AppState` (not `background` or `inactive`). Another screen on top, leaving, or putting the phone away stops it; coming back asks only for the new points. Scrolled down to Diagnostics it keeps polling: a poll is a few hundred bytes and the screen is the section's. | BS-08 on a phone: the screen is the window. |
| AP-1152 | **The layout on a phone:** the section's mono label `HOW IT’S DOING`, the live line with the presence dot, the range as the phone's segmented control (the one Spend switches its window with; 38 dp with a 44 dp hit area) under the title as on the web, because it scopes the Processor tile's peak and sparkline as well as the Processor and Network speed charts; the four tiles in two rows (Processor and Memory, Disk and Today; the mock's 16 dp cards, 22/600 value with a 13/500 unit, 6 dp meter, 26 dp sparkline in the meta colour with the latest value as a dot in `chart1`); then the chart switcher as the Inbox's chips in a row that scrolls sideways (Processor, Network speed, Data per day, Disk, Tokens: the contracts' titles; the picked chip scrolls into view) and the picked chart's card. Each tile is one TalkBack element (“Processor, 4 %, Peak 86 % at 14:25”). | Phone width (BS-12); the app's own controls, not a third segmented style; the chips name every chart in full where five segments wouldn't fit. |
| AP-1153 | **Charts are react-native-svg, already in the app** (`managed/HostCharts.tsx`; paths in `data/hostStatus.ts`): 2 px lines, the theme's wash under Processor (`chartWash`), hairline gridlines in `line` with mono 11 numbers, five time ticks (the last `now`), columns at most 18 wide with 4 px rounded tops and 2 px between stacked parts, gaps in the data left as gaps; the disk as a 14 dp bar of the parts (2 dp apart, the ends rounded) with a legend of sizes and the system and backup disks' lines. Colours only from `theme.chart` and `theme.colors`. | BS-11; no chart library, nothing new to install. |
| AP-1154 | **A finger instead of the tooltip:** a tap or a drag along a chart picks the nearest measured point (a gap picks the closest moment with values) or the column under it; a crosshair and dots (or the other columns lighter) mark it, and a readout row above the chart says it: the moment or day, then each series' value in bold with its swatch (“14:05 · 73 % busy”, “Today so far · 2.2 GB received · 41 MB sent · 1.7 GB of it by agents’ computers”). Without a finger the readout says the latest point and nothing is marked. The pick is kept by its moment (or day), so a poll moving the points along leaves it in place, and it carries between Processor and Network speed (the same moment in both); another range lets it go. A vertical drag still scrolls the screen. | A floating tooltip under a finger is hidden by the finger; a fixed row reads the same everywhere and doesn't jump. |
| AP-1155 | **TalkBack reads a chart as one adjustable element:** its name and what it covers (“Processor, 24 hours, Peak 86 % at 14:25”; “Tokens, 3.4M today · 94 % read from cache”) with the readout as its value; swiping up and down moves along it (a day at a time on the daily charts, about a 24th of the range on the time charts, past gaps). The readout row, sparklines, meters and the disk bar are hidden from it (the element, the tiles and the legend rows say the same). No “Show as a table” on the phone: the adjustable chart gives every value. A fixture of the approved mock (`fixtures/host.ts`: 21:40 on 11 October, the mock's 14 days of traffic and tokens, the disk 40 of 87 GB) backs the tests and screenshots. | BS-11's keyboard and table access, the phone's way. |
| AP-1156 | **The disk's colours by part:** the agents' computers `chart1`, the Whombat's own data `chart2`, the work folders `chart3` (the mock's), the database `mut`, Other `line2`, Free the `sf2` track (its legend swatch outlined); while the parts are being recounted the bar's one used part takes `chart1` (`hostDiskTone`). By part, not by size, so a part keeps its colour as sizes change. The Tokens card's “More in AI” opens the Spend screen (the phone's AI → Spend); today's agents with their pictures (a 22 dp avatar; the name next to it, so unlabelled), name, a `chart1` bar and the tokens, or “No agent work yet today.” | Three validated chart colours for up to six parts; the database is usually small and Other is what no quota group counts. |

Open: the web branch may colour the disk's database and Other parts differently; align `hostDiskTone` with it when both
land (a shared helper in `@whombat/client` would keep them together).

30-day and last-hour ranges, and the `unavailable` state; not against a real box.
