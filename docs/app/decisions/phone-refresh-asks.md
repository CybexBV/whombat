# Phone refresh: approvals, screen readers, Inbox and Chat (AP-7400…AP-7449)

From the 2026-11-05 design council on the phone apps (its final spec, §1 Approvals, §2 Screen readers, §6 Inbox and
Settle, §7 Chat waiting and composer) and John's “Yes” to doing it. The other slice of the refresh (status marks,
Overview, headers, the service page) has its own file. Kept in force: approvals are decided on the approval screen
(server-asks-mobile.md AP-1747), a message's words are selected with a hold (ios-native.md AP-6277), Settled in Chat's
header, Settled pinned at the Inbox's bottom (inbox.md IBX-09), the five tabs, Inbox answers in a sheet, swipes.

Changes in part: resourceful-agents.md AP-690 and server-asks-mobile.md AP-1744 (where the lasting yes sits),
quiet-notices-mobile.md AP-4864 (how long Undo stays), chat-channel.md AP-2459 (the chip is a button with its status).

| AP-7400 | **The answers' words and order are the shared ones**: the Inbox sheet's from `needsYouButtons` (client), the approval screen's from core's `APPROVAL_ASK_WORDS`: Yes, now · Yes, tonight · No where a later time is offered, Yes, do it · No where it isn't; no copy of the words in the app. Code (a deploy, a merge, a key, a share) keeps Decline · Approve, a command its scopes. | One order everywhere means no misreading between the thread, the Inbox and the details. |
| AP-7401 | **The approval screen offers Yes, tonight** on an everyday ask the viewer may answer: it approves with `executeAt` at the start of the Whombat's quiet hours for changes (`changeWindow.get`, 03:00 when the Whombat doesn't know them), as the web's chat card does, and says “Approved. | AP-7402 | **Superseded by approval-card.md ACD-04 (every answer outlined).** **No fill without a suggestion**: while a later time is on offer the answers are equal outlined buttons (`line2`), in the sheet and on the screen; “Yes, do it” alone stays the filled `btn`. Nothing on an approval says which is suggested (ui-refresh-inbox.md UIR-39). | A filled “Yes, now” beside “Yes, tonight” recommends what nobody recommended. |
| AP-7403 | **Snooze is not an answer**: an approval's own `snooze` action (an older Whombat's or the demo world's “Not now”) leaves the answers in the shared `needsYouButtons` and becomes its quiet *Remind me tomorrow*, beside *Talk to {assistant} about it* under them; an approval whose Whombat offers no snooze gets none (inbox.md IBX-04). The two quiet words share a row of 44dp text buttons. | The fourth outlined button read as a fourth answer. |
| AP-7404 | **The thread's approval card is a door**: eyebrow “Neo needs your OK” (mono 12, `warn-text`), title 17/600, the consequence only when the data has one (why a command runs, what code's approval rolls out; never “won’t do it without a person”), then one full-width “Review and answer ›” (48dp Android, 44pt iOS) that opens the approval screen. | AP-7407 | **The lasting yes keeps its meaning and leaves the footer** (AP-690, AP-1744: approve this time and stop asking, `scope: 'agent'`, core's `toasts.always`): a `mut` text button at the content's end under a `line` divider, “Don’t ask again for pve-02…” / “Always allow Neo to deploy…”, opening the platform's own alert (iOS alert, Android Material dialog) with core's confirmation words: the title naming the action and scope, “Next time it does it and tells you after.”, where it changes back, “Keep asking” (cancel, changes nothing) and “Let Neo do it”. Only owners who may change the agent, while they may answer. | A quick tap in the footer never gives a standing permission. |
| AP-7408 | **Where it changes back is named for the phone** (core `lastingWordsAt`): a server's “on pve-02’s page in Overview” (its “When Whombat wants to change something here”, server-asks-mobile.md AP-1740), a risk row's “on the web” (the phone has no place for the six rows, AP-689). | “You can change this in Settings” sent the person to a Settings that doesn't have it. |
| AP-7409 | **Large text** (font scale above 1.3, `ui/largeText.ts`): the answers stack one per row and Decline/Approve stack with Approve on top; button labels wrap (`Button wrap`, and every `BigButton`) instead of being cut. | Labels never shrink or truncate. |
| AP-7410 | **A message is one element for screen readers**: its author line and words are one accessible view labelled “Mara Okafor, 13:52. Why is pve-02 using so much memory?” (the model's name after the time where it shows; “Pinned”), with actions Reply (opens its thread, also the default activate, in Chat and Feedback), Settle or Open again (its swipe's), More (its menu, also the long press), Copy, and one “Open {link words}” per link in its words (`data/messageA11y.ts`). The selectable text view inside (AP-6277) is hidden from them, so it isn't heard twice or as a field to edit; selecting with a hold is unchanged. Cards, receipts, the Vault line, the chip and the replies row under the words stay their own elements. | VoiceOver read a message as an editable “Text view” plus the same words again, and the swipe's actions weren't reachable. |
| AP-7411 | **Names for every control in Chat**: the swipe panels' own pressables are no elements (the row's actions carry them); the composer field is labelled once (VoiceOver reads its placeholder, Android keeps the label); the topic chip is a button “pve-02, Needs a look” (the shared status word; “About pve-02” while its status isn't known) that opens the thing's page. Avatars inside a message are part of its one element. | The iOS tree had unnamed buttons between messages and “Ask Neo anything Ask Neo anything”. |
| AP-7412 | **A permission card's settings say their value**: the setting's line reads “Room temperature, Allowed. Only between 16 and 24 °C”, the group “What Neo may do with Room temperature: Allowed”, each option “Room temperature: Off”. In dark the chosen segment is filled `btn` with `btnfg` words; light keeps the raised `sf`. | A screen reader heard “Allowed” without which setting; `sf` on `sf2` barely showed in dark. |
| AP-7413 | **Focus comes back** (`ui/rowFocus.ts`, only while a screen reader runs): after a message's menu sheet closes, to that message; after Settle, to the next row (the one before it at the end); the same for Inbox rows after their sheet and after Settle or Bring back. | Closing a sheet dropped focus at the top of the screen. |
| AP-7414 | **Undo stays 8 s** (every Undo toast, Inbox and Chat), at least Android's recommended accessibility timeout for controls (`getRecommendedTimeoutMillis`), twice that while a screen reader runs; with VoiceOver on, a toast with Undo stays until its ✕ (44pt, “Dismiss”) and its words are announced when it shows (VoiceOver doesn't follow live regions). Placement unchanged: above the tab bar and the keyboard. | The web's Undo after Settle is 8 s; a screen reader user needs the time to reach it. |
| AP-7415 | **Inbox descriptions get three lines** in the list (the sheet has all of it). | Two lines cut the restart ask's “down for about 40 seconds”, the part that decides it. |
| AP-7416 | **Settled is a flush row** under the list, above the tab bar: “Settled · N” (15/600, the count mono), a `line` hairline above it, no card or shadow, 44pt iOS / 48dp Android, a chevron; it always opens — into its rows (their own scroll, at most 45% of the screen) or, with nothing settled, into its sentence, which shows only then. The list ends above it. | The floating card with its sentence on show looked like a notice and covered calm space. |
