# This Whombat in parts, on Android (AP-1240…AP-1255)

John, 2026-10-11, about Settings → This Whombat: “The page https://dev.whombat.com/settings?tab=appliance has soo much
stuff going on, how can we improve this / clean up the page / create sections / tabs / ? What would you do to improve
this?” — and on the proposal of five parts with an Overview that starts with what needs you, “I like it, yes implement
fully”. The web and the shared model are `this-whombat-sections.md` (AP-1221…AP-1236; `this-whombat.ts` in
`@whombat/client/managed`: `whombatParts`, `whombatOverview`, `THIS_WHOMBAT_TEXT`). The phone's This Whombat was the
same one long screen: UPDATE, How it's doing with every chart, VERSION, CONNECTION, SUBSCRIPTION, RECOVERY KIT, BACKUPS,
BACKUP FILE, UPDATES, DEVICES, REMOVED DEVICES, NOT ON A MANAGED WHOMBAT, OWNERS, DIAGNOSTICS with its checklist open,
and the phone's fingerprint. This is the phone's half: the Overview stays the screen This Whombat opens, and each part
it links to is a screen of its own. The pure part is `managed/thisWhombat.ts` (unit tested); the screens are
`WhombatScreen.tsx` (the Overview), `WhombatBackupsScreen.tsx`, `DevicesScreen.tsx` (Devices & owners) and
`WhombatAboutScreen.tsx`. No contracts change, and no change to the shared model.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-1240 | **Four parts, as screens.** On a managed Whombat This Whombat is the **Overview** (`Whombat`, as before, so the settings sheet's This Whombat and pushes about the Whombat itself land on it) and three pushed screens it links to: **Backups** (`WhombatBackups`), **Devices & owners** (the `Devices` screen, renamed) and **About** (`WhombatAbout`), titled with `THIS_WHOMBAT_TEXT.parts`. No Agents’ access part: the phone has no web browser or local network settings, and what a managed Whombat turns off goes to About (AP-1248). The settings sheet's This Whombat group keeps its two buttons, “This Whombat” (the Overview) and “Devices & owners”. | A second row of segments under a large title is cramped at phone width and hides the back gesture's meaning; a list that opens screens is how the rest of the phone's settings work (Members, Vault, Connections). |
| AP-1241 | **The Overview: NEEDS YOU, HOW IT’S DOING, AT A GLANCE**, under the app's own update card (a newer build of this app) as before. It is made by the shared `whombatOverview` from `appliance.status`, the owners (`admins.list`: names, and whether the viewer's account is one), this account's devices that aren't removed (`endpoints.list`), the viewer's own passkeys only where the Whombat has `passkeys` and this phone can make them (Credential Manager, as Devices decides), and for owners the people whose new device asks to get back in now (`enroll.reenroll.list`, `pending_confirmation`); `access` is null, and the version (this app, the Whombat) is passed for a Whombat without an update row. The screen shows when the status and all those lists answered (a list that fails is left out, never guessed), and reads everything again, quietly, when the person comes back to it. | One screen that answers “is it fine, and what do I do”. Showing it all at once keeps rows and buttons from moving in under a finger; reading again on return makes a fixed thing leave NEEDS YOU. |
| AP-1242 | **NEEDS YOU** is a card, left out when empty; each row a dot (in the title's colour only; the title says what is wrong), the title (15/500), its line (mono 12, `mut`, as the other rows here) and under them one secondary button with `THIS_WHOMBAT_TEXT.actions` (“Set up backups”, “Open backups”, “Save a recovery kit”, “Open devices”, “Add an owner”, “Add a passkey”). What only an owner can fix is only there for owners (the shared model's rule, AP-1224, AP-1234); members see those as glance rows. | The button under the words keeps both readable at phone width; beside them (as the old kit row had it) a long label squeezed the sentence into a narrow column. |
| AP-1243 | **What each button does.** Set up backups opens Backups with the destination form open; Open backups opens Backups; Save a recovery kit starts the kit's flow at once (`onKit`, as the kit card does); Open devices opens Devices & owners at the person waiting; Add a passkey at PASSKEYS; **Add an owner at OWNERS** — not Settings → Members as on the web, because on the phone Members changes a person's role in the burrow and has no “make them an owner of this Whombat”; OWNERS (moved to Devices & owners) is where that is done on the phone (`admins.grant`). | Each button is the step that fixes it (AP-1224). Sending an owner to Members would let them make a burrow owner and leave “You’re the only owner” where it was. |
| AP-1244 | **AT A GLANCE**: a card of rows, each the whole 56 dp row one button (`LinkRow`: dot, title, line, a `›`), read as “title, line” (“Backed up, Last backup 3 hours ago”), opening its part: the update and the plan → About, backups and the recovery kit → Backups, the devices → Devices & owners, the version (only without an update row) → About. Then a plain row named after each part nothing above links to (“Backups” while both its rows need you, “Devices & owners” while the devices can't be read), so every part stays one tap from the Overview. | The shared rows say it is fine; the chevron goes where the details are. Without the extra rows a part could be unreachable from the Overview exactly when something there needs looking at. |
| AP-1245 | **How it’s doing on the Overview: the four tiles** (owners on a Whombat with `host.metrics`, the range above them as before), then **Show charts**, a link-style 44 dp button (`expanded` for TalkBack) that opens the chart chips and the chart under the tiles (Processor, Network speed, Data per day, Disk, Tokens), **Hide charts** closes them; opened stays opened while the app runs. A Whombat without a manager shows Tokens without the button, one whose measurements can't be read the contracts' sentence and Tokens, as before. Polling is unchanged (live while the Overview is in front). | The tiles answer “is it OK”; a screen of charts was the first thing on This Whombat (AP-1227). |
| AP-1246 | **Backups**: BACKUPS as before (where they stand, what they cover and don't, and for owners Set up backups / Back up now with the destination form in place), RECOVERY KIT (owners save it from here), then BACKUP FILE's row opening the Backup file screen (burrow owners on a Whombat with `backup.file`, AP-1211), whose back bar now says “‹ Backups”. Opened with `setUp` (the Overview's Set up backups) the destination form is open at once. | The web's Backups part (AP-1228). The backup file keeps its own screen on the phone (AP-1211): its transfers need the screen in front, so it isn't folded into dialogs as on the web. |
| AP-1247 | **Devices & owners**: the Devices screen as it was (this phone, the other devices with Remove, PASSKEYS, the owners' invitation, people waiting to get back in and the links to help them, Add a device with its link and the code), then REMOVED DEVICES (from `appliance.status`, left out when it can't be read) and OWNERS, both moved here from the old long screen. Opened with `at` (`passkeys`, `waiting`, `owners`) it scrolls to that section and follows it while what is above it loads, until a finger scrolls. | The web's Devices & owners (AP-1229) at phone length; the steps from NEEDS YOU land on the thing to do, not at the top of a long screen. |
| AP-1248 | **About**: VERSION, UPDATES (with the oldest app builds let in), **PLAN** (was SUBSCRIPTION; Check again for owners), CONNECTION (the Whombat's name and fingerprint), what a managed Whombat turns off under **TURNED OFF HERE** (was “NOT ON A MANAGED WHOMBAT”), DIAGNOSTICS (owners), and this phone's fingerprint at the bottom. It follows an update that moves or waits, as before (AP-258). | The web's About part (AP-1231); TURNED OFF HERE is the web's Agents’ access part's last section (AP-1230), and the phone has no such part. |
| AP-1249 | **Diagnostics in plain words, behind a button.** The card shows the intro and the files made (Show what’s in it, Share, Delete as before); **Make a file for support…** opens the checklist in place (not a dialog, which on a phone would be a sheet over a sheet), with Make the file and Cancel; it folds again once the file is made. Each part keeps its name; its line is `THIS_WHOMBAT_TEXT.diagnostics.scopes` (“How well this Whombat stayed connected over the last 7 days.”), the contract's developer line only for a part the app doesn't know yet. | AP-1232; “relay connects” and “restore-drill result” meant nothing to an owner. |
| AP-1250 | **Navigation.** `WhombatBackups` (`{ setUp?: true }`), `Devices` (`{ at?: 'passkeys' \| 'waiting' \| 'owners' }`) and `WhombatAbout` are managed-only stack screens, loaded lazily like the others, and restored after a killed process like This Whombat, Devices and Backup file (managed builds only). The one-time params are dropped once the screen read them (`setParams`), so a restored screen opens plainly. Backups and About have “‹ This Whombat” back bars that open the Overview when there is nothing to go back to. Pushes about the Whombat itself still open the Overview, where NEEDS YOU says what to do. | A restore shouldn't reopen a form or jump down a screen; the Overview is where a problem is explained first. |
| AP-1251 | **Accessibility.** Glance rows are one button each, 56 dp, named by their words; NEEDS YOU's buttons are the 44 dp `Button`, the rows' dots hidden (their titles say it); Show charts says whether the charts are open (`expanded`); diagnostics' parts stay 44 dp checkboxes. The touch-target audit lists the managed screens (`ui.tsx` 1, Overview 0, Backups 0, About 1, Devices & owners 1, How it’s doing 2) and the reading-order audit includes them. | README “Accessibility”; the audits catch a control added later without its size. |
| AP-1252 | **Words.** Every new word is the shared model's (`THIS_WHOMBAT_TEXT`: the parts, NEEDS YOU, AT A GLANCE, the buttons, Show and Hide charts, PLAN, TURNED OFF HERE, the diagnostics' lines); the old phone-only “DEVICES” section row (`COPY.devices`) and the “Devices” title (`COPY.whombat.devices`) are gone. | One wording on web and phone; `concise-replies.md`. |
| AP-1253 | **Not on the phone.** No badges (they sit on the web's segments; the phone's Overview is the way to every problem); no Agents’ access part; AUDIT LOG stays in the settings sheet's This Whombat group, not in About; the backup file stays its own screen (AP-1246). | Each has a place on the phone already, or has nothing to show here. |
| AP-1254 | **Tests and checks.** `thisWhombat.test.ts`: the Overview's input (owners by name or email, devices not removed, only the viewer's passkeys, only someone asking now, what couldn't be read), each button's step, the glance rows' parts and labels, the extra rows for unreachable parts, the version row without an update, no Agents’ access, the diagnostics lines; `persist.test.ts` for the new screens; the touch-target and reading-order audits. The mobile typecheck, Biome and the mobile and client unit tests once (DEV fast); nothing was run on a device or the emulator. | The logic is in the pure model; the screens are the old pieces in new places. |
| AP-1255 | **Released with the web as whombat-app 0.74.0** (AP-1236): the APK on DEV with it. | One change on both clients. |

Open: the screens haven't been seen on a phone — the Overview's first paint while the lists load, Devices & owners
scrolling to OWNERS and following it while the passkeys and the people waiting load, and the destination form opened
at once on Backups want one look on a device before release.
