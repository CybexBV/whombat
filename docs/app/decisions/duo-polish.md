# The phone app on the iPhone Duo: keyboard, layout and polish (AP-6540…AP-6559)

John, 2026-11-02, on the iPhone Duo with app 3.35.1: the app is “laggy” and “some elements seem to be wrong”. A code
read and a walk through the Duo simulator found the keyboard breaking Chat when the phone unfolds, an opaque band
cutting off every tab with the tabs down the side, viewers and sheets under the clock or across the fold, and a
handful of marks, words and margins that disagree with the rest of the app. This file is the keyboard and layout part
of that pass.

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| AP-6540 | **The keyboard is followed on the UI thread** with `react-native-keyboard-controller` (`KeyboardProvider` at the root, translucent bars so Android stays edge to edge; `ui/keyboard.tsx`). Every screen that kept clear of the keyboard uses our `KeyboardAvoidingView` (`ui/keyboard.tsx`): it pads its bottom by the part of it the keyboard covers (`keyboardOverlap`), measuring itself in the app's window when the keyboard starts and stops moving and when its layout changes; the sheets drawn in a React Native `Modal` keep React Native's own, in their own window. | React Native's view kept the keyboard's height from before the window changed size, so unfolding with the keyboard up left Chat's list gone and its box at the top or on the bottom edge. The library reads the keyboard's frame as it moves and after a resize. Its own view measured the screen once and on the phone's screen rather than in the window, which left the reply box about 50dp above the keyboard on John's phone (2026-11-16). |
| AP-6541 | **What rests on the keyboard moves with it** (`useKeyboardPadding`, `keyboardPadding` in `ui/insets.ts`, unit-tested): Chat's box and the forms' footers go from their rest padding (the gesture area, the tab bar) to 10 (12 for a footer) above the keyboard as it rises, frame by frame. With two pages the bars line up by the room above the box, so the keyboard no longer leaves 10 points of empty bar over the pill. | The padding switched on `keyboardDidShow`/`DidHide`, after the keyboard had arrived, so the box jumped each time. |
| AP-6542 | **Pulling Chat's messages down takes the keyboard with it** (`keyboardDismissMode` interactive on iOS, on drag on Android), and **choosing another tab puts the keyboard away**. Nothing puts the cursor in a box when a tab shows again. | The keyboard was hard to dismiss, and the native screen gave the cursor back to the box it had when the tab returned, raising the keyboard nobody asked for. |
| AP-6543 | **With the tabs down the side a tab's screen runs on to the window's bottom** (no `BelowTabs`), and every list pads its end by what covers the screen's bottom: our bottom tab bar nothing, iOS's tab bar its height, the window's edge the home indicator or gesture area (`useBottomRoom` in `ui/screenBottom.tsx`, `screenBottomRoom` unit-tested). Pushed screens add it too. The composer counts the same way (`BelowComposer` without `inset`). | An opaque view the height of the home indicator cut every tab's list off above the edge, and about 40 pushed screens ended their lists under the indicator. |
| AP-6544 | **Full-screen viewers measure their own safe area** (`FullScreenModal` in `ui/fullScreen.tsx`: a picture, an HTML page, a reporter's screenshot, the QR scanner), padding all four sides; the full-screen page shows the app's background until it loads. | Inside the frame beside the side tabs the safe area doesn't count the Duo's strip under the clock (right: 0), so a modal over the whole window put its buttons under the clock; the WebView flashed white in dark. |
| AP-6545 | **The toast floats over the page it is about** (`toastPlacement` in `data/screenShape.ts`, unit-tested): with two pages over the page beside the list, never across the crease or over the side tabs; above the bottom tab bar where there is one, otherwise just above the home indicator; and above the keyboard while it is up. | It was centred on the whole window: on the crease when open, off-centre and over the side tabs when closed, and behind the keyboard. |
| AP-6546 | **The line between the pages is dragged by a gesture-handler pan over both pages** that starts only when the finger goes down within 12 points of the line and moves sideways (8 points) before it moves up or down (10); the list's width follows on the UI thread (a Reanimated shared value), snapping and the kept share as before (AP-5812). The line itself takes no touches. The kept share is read as the app starts and the two pages wait for it. | The responder took every touch in a 24-point column across both pages and never let go (taps, swipes and scrolls there were lost), the drag re-laid the list out from the JavaScript thread, and the list jumped to the kept width just after launch. |
| AP-6547 | **A fold report that changes nothing the layout uses keeps the same shape** (`sameShape`, unit-tested), and the fold module sends one report after a scene update (size, safe area and traits coalesced) instead of forcing the same state again on every return to the front. The posture still arrives and nothing uses it. | Each report made a new shape and drew every screen again; the module forced one on every foreground and hinge move. |
| AP-6548 | **With two pages a sheet is the app's own, at the bottom of the page it was opened from**, that page's width at most 560 (`PageSideContext`, `usePageFrame`; the emoji grid counts the same width). One page keeps iOS's form sheet (ios-native.md). | iOS floats a form sheet as a card in the middle of a wide window: across the fold, its bottom about 60 points above the screen's. UIKit offers no way to anchor it to one page. |
| AP-6549 | **Overview and Settings mark what the page beside them shows**, as Chat, Needs you and Automations do: a server or a service row, a Settings row (and your profile) in `sel`, selected for screen readers. | Opening a server or a settings page left the list with nothing marked. |
| AP-6550 | **Side-tab labels shrink to fit** (down to 60 %, larger text up to 1.6×), and **Chat's header moves what doesn't fit beside the title into a ⋯ menu** (`fitHeaderButtons` in `data/headerFit.ts`, unit-tested from the words' widths and the text size): Feedback is kept longest, then Done threads, then Files; the title keeps at least 56 points. iOS's own menu on iPhone, a sheet elsewhere. | “Automations” was cut off at larger text, and on the 391-point list beside the open right page Files, Feedback and Done threads pushed the title off. |
| AP-6551 | **Tab titles start 22 points from the edge**, the mock's `Inbox` large title (`LARGE_TITLE_LEFT`): Needs you, Automations, Overview, Settings and Chat's header (the cards stay at 16). **Underline tabs hug their words** as on the web (2 points each side, 22 between), so the first underline starts at the margin. | Needs you was at 22, Chat at 20, Overview and Settings at 16, and Overview's Servers underline began 12 points left of its word. |
| AP-6552 | **Words and marks**: beside Overview the right page says “Pick a server or a connection to see it here.” (Overview holds servers, connections and what is pinned); Settings → Connections goes back to “‹ Settings” like the other Settings pages; rows that open a page end in the Settings list's chevron (`RowChevron`) instead of a “›” character. Overview's Connections (the services Whombat watches) and Settings → Connections (what this Whombat is signed in to) stay two lists, as on the web. | Copy that named only one of the three, a bare “Back”, and two different arrows for the same thing. |
| AP-6553 | **The approval page says who asks once and doesn't repeat its buttons**: “Neo · 14:03” where the conversation is named after the one asking (`approvalByline`), and an everyday ask whose only condition is the person's yes has no conditions card (the buttons ask it). The web, which has no byline, keeps its card. | “Neo · Neo · 14:03” and a card “You say yes · you” right above Yes and No. |
| AP-6554 | **Send looks disabled while there is nothing to send** (the app's disabled look, 45 % opacity, as `Button` has), and while files are still going up. | The black send circle looked ready on an empty box. |
| AP-6555 | **The crash on 3.35.1 (folded, “Weird”)**: an Objective-C exception thrown inside UIKit while FrontBoard delivered a scene update, with no frame of ours on the stack (the report's symbols for system libraries are wrong). Our code that reacts to the scene is `modules/whombat-fold` (a view inserted into the window that reads reserved regions, the vertical bar's traits and a hinge interaction on every layout, safe-area and trait change); `whombat-sheet` reads the window's safe area only on its own layout; `whombat-live` and `whombat-menu` don't react to scene changes. No credible cause was found; the fold module now reads once after the update (AP-6547) instead of three times inside it, which removes the one place where our code queried the scene mid-update. | Fix only what is understood; record what was checked so the next report can be compared. |

Not done: the half-open posture (still unused); Overview's and Settings' Connections lists brought into one (they are
different things, as on the web); an Android foldable run (Android typechecks and takes the same rules; the keyboard
library keeps it edge to edge with the window's resize mode as before).

## Checked

`pnpm --filter @whombat/mobile typecheck` and `test`; the demo world's Release build on Xcode 27.1's iPhone Duo
simulator: the keyboard up closed, unfolding with it up (Chat's list and both bars in place), the keyboard on the open
right page and folding with it up, a tab switched away and back without the keyboard returning, the line dragged
away from the crease and back (snapping), a vertical scroll beside the line, the answers sheet at the bottom of the
right page, a server marked beside Overview, the toast centred on the closed page clear of the side tabs, the end of
Automations above the home indicator, the approval page; closed and open, light and dark. The full-screen viewers
were not opened on the simulator (no picture in the demo's messages reached).
