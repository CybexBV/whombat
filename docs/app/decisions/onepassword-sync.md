# A 1Password vault kept in step with the Vault: the screens (AP-9800…)

John, 2026-11-24: a 1Password vault linked to the Vault, kept in step both ways. Whombat
`docs/decisions/onepassword-sync.md` (OPW-01…OPW-61, OPW-K01…OPW-K18) decides the sync, its words and its contracts
(`connections.onePassword`, `connections.onePasswordSync`, `connections.onePasswordRemovals`, `VaultEntry.source`, the
disconnect query `vaultEntries`, the Inbox action `onepassword_removals`, session minor 8). This is the web and phone
side. No HTML mock exists: the screens are built from the Vault's rows and the connection page's blocks, rows, notes and
buttons as they are, with core's words (`ONEPASSWORD_WORDS` and its line helpers) verbatim.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-9800 | **One shared model** in `@whombat/client` (`onePassword.ts`): the tag rule (`vaultEntryTag`: “1Password” while `source.kind` is `onepassword`, nothing for a place the app doesn't know), the entry's line with its tone (`vaultEntrySourceLine`), the page part (`onePasswordPage`), Remove's two choices and query (`ONEPASSWORD_DISCONNECT_OPTIONS`, `disconnectQuery`), the answer's toast and the refusal of a changed set (`onePasswordSetChanged`). The few words the spec leaves to the apps are `ONEPASSWORD_APP_TEXT`: the section KEPT IN STEP, VAULT IN 1PASSWORD above the vault's name, “Your 1Password vault” before the first check named it, “Syncing with 1Password…”, Remove's question, “Removed them from your Vault too.” / “Kept them in your Vault.”, and “The list changed since you saw it. Look again before you choose.”. | The web and the phone say the same thing, and core's words are never written again. |
| AP-9801 | **The Vault.** A synced entry's row carries the tag after its name (web: the soft `Tag`; phone: a neutral `StatusPill`, `PlaceRow`'s new `tag`), wrapping under the name on a narrow row and read with it. Its page says `onePasswordEntryLine` under what the entry is (web: under “Website login · …” in the open row; phone: under the header's mono line): “Kept in step with 1Password · 2 min ago”, “Waiting to go to 1Password”, or the problem in the warning colour, never red: the entry itself still works. | OPW-50, OPW-27, OPW-28; red is for Not working. |
| AP-9802 | **The connection's page.** A connection of a `use: 'vault'` entry (`usedByVaultSync`) has none of the agent's parts: no level, handle, LAST USED, tools, guide or access pill, and no “Used by automations” (`usedByAutomations` no longer counts it). Its vault and the linking question are never offered for a later change (`choiceSettings` gives none for it): changing either would mean linking again. CONNECTION's state, the token's check with Check again and Replace token stay. KEPT IN STEP is on the page's own 1Password tab (AP-9811): VAULT IN 1PASSWORD with the vault's name, the kept line, the waiting, failing or read-only lines, the guard's removals, the footer with the checked line and Sync now; NOT IN YOUR VAULT lists each item's title and reason (web: a block of the same card; phone: a section of its own). | As the AI gateway's page (AP-1117): the agent's lines don't apply to a connection it never uses. |
| AP-9803 | **How loud the lines are.** A failing pass in the Not working colour (it has stopped keeping them in step); waiting for 1Password's limit and a token that can only read in the warning colour. The paused line is the removals' own title, not said twice. | The limit ends by itself and the token still pulls; a failing pass doesn't. |
| AP-9804 | **The guard's removals on the page**: the title, the entries' names in the server's order, then “Remove them here too” and “Keep them here”, both outlined: neither is recommended and removing can't be undone. The answer carries the set's token; a `conflict` says the list changed and the page shows the new set; nothing was removed. On the Inbox card the server's `style` decides which is filled. | OPW-24, OPW-K12: a removal the person didn't see never happens. |
| AP-9805 | **Fresh without a frame.** While `syncing` the page part reads again every 3 s; it stops when the pass ends. A `vault.updated` frame (a pass that changed entries) reads it again too. | A pass that changed nothing sends nothing, and the page must not say “Syncing…” for ever. |
| AP-9806 | **What the writes refresh** (`useOnePasswordWrite`): Sync now's and the answer's reply go into the cache at once; the answer also reads the Vault and Home again (entries removed or kept only here, the card gone); a refusal reads the page part again. Removing the connection with `vaultEntries` drops its page part and reads the Vault again. | Every screen that shows what changed follows without a reload. |
| AP-9807 | **The Inbox card.** On a line the Inbox keeps the server's `onepassword_removals` answers (it otherwise keeps only `open`) and shows both in sight, Settle and the rest in More; the phone shows them under the row, equal, like an approval's answers. Each goes through the `onePasswordRemovals` intent on the connection the item's `ref` names; the card leaves at once and comes back if refused. | It is a question with two answers: hiding one in More would make the other look like the only way. |
| AP-9808 | **Removing 1Password** asks in place, on the web and the phone: “Remove 1Password? Your Vault stops following it. Nothing changes in 1Password.”, radio cards “Keep the entries in your Vault” (chosen) and “Remove them too”, then “Remove 1Password” and Cancel. `vaultEntries` is sent for the Vault's sync always, and never for another connection. The phone doesn't use an alert here: it can't show which choice is the default, and its long buttons stack badly. | OPW-50, OPW-K14; keeping is the safe default. |
| AP-9809 | **Connecting** needs nothing new: the catalog-driven form asks the service account token and “Your Vault entries” (its first answer chosen), and the vault only when the token sees several (`field_choice_needed`, as Moneybird's administration). A box below session minor 8 doesn't list the entry, so neither app offers it. | OPW-01, OPW-02, OPW-K17. |
| AP-9810 | **Fixture mode** (`VITE_FIXTURE_ONEPASSWORD=synced\|waiting\|failing\|paused`, option `onePassword`, best with `VITE_FIXTURE_CONNECTORS=connected`): Mara's 1Password connection, five entries it brought (one waiting to go, one whose item has no website now) beside Grafana and Printer admin, five items not in the Vault, and per state the last pass: fine 3 min ago, waiting until 11:40, failing with a read-only token, or paused with four removals and the Inbox card. Sync now runs a pass for a moment; the answer is refused for an old set; Remove keeps or removes the entries. The connecting form's token sees two vaults. Off by default, so the mocks' screens stay as drawn. | To build, test and look at the screens without 1Password. |
| AP-9811 | **The sync is the page's own tab.** The server's page for a 1Password connection opens on its tab `onepassword` (“1Password”, no panels; `ONEPASSWORD_TAB`), then the Summary with Worth a look. Both apps draw KEPT IN STEP and NOT IN YOUR VAULT there, from `connections.onePassword`; the Connection tab (web) and the Connection screen (phone) keep only the connection's own details and Remove. The phone's Settings → Connections row for 1Password opens its page, as the web's does, not the Connection screen. An app below session minor 8 would show that tab empty (both draw an unknown tab from its panels, and it has none), so the server leaves it out for such an app and its page opens on the Summary, the head still naming the vault and the count. The fixture world's page sends the same tabs. | One place for the sync, the one the page opens on; the Connection tab is the same for every connection. |

AP-9800…AP-9811.
