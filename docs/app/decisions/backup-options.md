# Backup options, the list of backups and bringing a Whombat back in the apps (AP-6800…AP-6849)

John, 2026-11-03: see the backups, set how many are made and kept, keep a way back in with a passkey, and bring a
Whombat back on a new box (Whombat [backup-options.md](../../decisions/backup-options.md) BKO-01…BKO-14). These are
the web's parts (AP-6800…AP-6849; the phone's are its own rows). Mocks: `1-backups-page.png`,
`2-options-and-detail.png`, `3-bring-back.png` with their final changes (MOCKS.md). Words and views are
`@whombat/client/managed` backup-options (`BACKUP_OPTIONS_TEXT`, `backupHero`, `backupGroups`, `policySummary`,
`keepTicks`, `restoreSteps`) and kit-passkey; the React Query hooks are `@whombat/client/react` backupOptions.
