# Dashboards on Android (AP-2120…AP-2199)

Stream M of Whombat `dashboards.md` (DS-01…DS-19), 2026-10-18: one Summary for every server, connection and device,
on the phone. The panels are contracts 0.89.0 (CT-394…CT-398): a connection's or device's from the Whombat server
(`things.page`), a server's built in `@whombat/client` (`serverDashboard`, stream K, `dashboards-client.md`
AP-2000…). The web's part is `dashboards-web.md` (AP-2050…). Mocks: `docs/design-handoff/dashboards/*.png` (desktop;
the phone stacks everything in the order given).

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-2120 | **One stacked renderer** (`screens/thing/Panels.tsx`, chart cards `ChartCard.tsx`) for connections, devices and servers: every panel in the order given, the whole width; `place`, `span` and `width` are not used on the phone. Its context names the thing (`{id, name, canManage}`, so a server's `server:pve-02` works as a connection's `connection:con_ha`), the tabs a link may open, the range, and how a link runs. A panel with nothing to say is left out as before (AP-1953; an empty bar list too). | DS-04: “Phones stack all panels in the order given”; one look, no per-kind phone work. |
| AP-2121 | **Colours** by the builder's word through `thingSeriesToken` (blue, orange, green, violet → `chart1…4`; amber `warn`, red `bad`, grey `mut`) and `swatchColor` on the native theme; a figure's, bar's or meter's `warn`/`bad` tone wins over its colour. No hex in components. | DS-06, DS-18, CT-395. |
| AP-2122 | **The range control** sits at the top of the Summary (the first tab), under the tabs: the server page's segmented control sharing the width, the ranges' short words in mono (“1h · 6h · 24h · 7d · 30d”), each read as “Last 24 hours”. A connection's offers `ThingPage.ranges` (none when absent or one, or on a runtime without `things.dashboards`); a server's `SERVER_DASHBOARD_PHONE_RANGES` (90 days left out: no room, little use on a phone) with “Live · every 10 s” and a green dot under it. Picking one reads the page again; the previous range stays on screen while it loads (`keepPreviousData`). The range lives on the screen, not in the link (the phone has no address to share); it opens on 24 hours. | DS-03, DS-16. |
| AP-2123 | **Figures** stay the server page's tiles two by two (a fifth takes the row; AP-1954), each with its small line (30dp, 1.5 px in its colour with its wash, at the tile's foot so a row's lines line up) or its meter (5dp). TalkBack reads one element: “CPU: 2 % of 4 cores. Last 24 hours: steady, with a peak” — the small line said as its trend (rising, falling, steady; a peak over twice the average). | DS-07; a small line needs words for those who can't see it. |
| AP-2124 | **Chart cards** take the whole width: the title as the card's mono label, `meta` as its grey line, the figure (26/700, its tone) with its rest, the average and the peak on the right (under it when they don't fit), a legend for two lines or more (colour, words, latest in bold), the chart (150dp), and up to four facts under a hairline, two by two. | DS-06 at 360dp. |
| AP-2125 | **One scale, three lines**: the axis's fixed ends when given (a disk from 50 %), else 0 to a round top over the lines, the limit and the forecast (1, 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 × 10ⁿ, so the middle line is round too); the side words carry the unit (“100%”, “60°”, “8 GB”) and the gutter is as wide as the longest. A right-axis line is still scaled onto the left one, the legend and readout saying its own figures (AP-1957). The words along the bottom come from the range (`timeTicks`, `formatTick`) and only those that fit stay, the first and the last always (the web's `fitTimeTicks` rule). | §2 of the brief; a phone has no room for two axes. |
| AP-2126 | **A picked moment** (a finger along the chart, or TalkBack's swipe up and down by a 24th of the range): a crosshair and dots, and a small box over the chart's top on the side away from the finger with the time (`formatTooltipTime`) and every line's figure. The chart doesn't move: nothing appears above it. The limit is dashed in `warn` with its words at the left; the forecast a dashed line in the line's colour from the last point to a dot with its words, in room after the last point (its days, at most 1.25 × the points' width; a farther day still sits at the far end, as the last word along the bottom); `style: 'bars'` stacks the lines per point (`ColumnChart`, its words fitted the same way). | DS-06's hover on a touch screen; the old readout row above the chart pushed the chart down under the finger. |
| AP-2127 | **A server's Summary** is `useServerDashboard` (K): the head (the name in the mono face, the status dot and its one badge, the chips as one line, the fact lines, the address · synced, and “This Whombat: Runs here, in …” in `brand` when it runs there), then on Summary the range control, the panels, Asks you first (owners), Also connected and “Ask about pve-02”. Guests, Backups, Array and Plugins stay as they were. | DS-02, DS-09, DS-17. |
| AP-2128 | **A Summary's links on a server**: a tab link opens the phone's segment for it (`docker`, `containers`, `vms`, `instances` → Guests; `pools`, `disks`, `notifications` and Unraid's `system` → Array, where the phone keeps Unraid's notifications; `updates` → Plugins), and a button the builder words “Open System” says where it goes here (“Open Array”); a tab the phone doesn't have (Storage, Sensors, a Proxmox server's System) leaves its card or notice without a button. A link into the same server opens the guest (`?guest=`, on its `gtab`), the segment (`?tab=`), or nothing for `?console=` (consoles stay on the web); any other link opens as before; “Look into it” opens the new-chat sheet with the question ready. | The web's tabs are more than the phone's; never a button that does nothing, or one that says somewhere else. |
| AP-2129 | **Table rows** (CT-396): numeric columns on the right, a small bar (32 × 4) before a figure with a `meter`; a small line (22dp, with its wash) under the row's words from its first `spark` cell; a `button` cell as a small outlined button (32dp, padded to 44dp) beside the row, not inside it, so TalkBack reaches both — left out for a console and when it goes where the row goes. TalkBack reads a dot that isn't fine (“Needs a look”, “Problem”, “Off”) and the small line's trend under its column's words. | DS-08 at phone width; labels for dots and small lines. |
| AP-2130 | **What the old Summary had** stays reachable: Unraid's unread notifications with Archive and Archive all move to the **Array** segment, under the array card; Unraid's and its plugins' updates are on Plugins, the containers' on Guests; the pools, what Incus, QEMU/KVM and Unraid are, and the storages are the Summary's own panels (`serverDashboard`). Removed from the phone: the four tiles, Needs a look with its ways out, the banner card, the Unraid Summary cards (pools, updates), the Incus and QEMU/KVM cards with their “on the web” lines, and the About it fold (its facts are the head's now; “This Whombat: Runs here” stays in the head), with their helpers (`serverTiles`, `serverLooks`, `serverFacts`, `uptimeWords`) and tests. | DS-09: the Summary shows, the segments do. |
| AP-2131 | **Bar lists**: each line's name (mono when asked) with its grey words, its figure on the right (mono, in its tone), a 6dp bar in its tone (blue, `warn`, `bad`), the line under it in its own tone; `columns` stack on the phone; a line with a page opens it (a chevron). | DS-08. |
| AP-2132 | **Worth a look** shows five; “Show 4 more” is a 44dp `brand` row at the card's foot that opens the rest in place, then “Show fewer”. | DS-05. |
| AP-2133 | **Older runtimes**: without `things.dashboards` a connection's page is read without a range and shows no range control; a 0.88 page stacks as given. A server's Summary is built in the client either way. | DS-17. |
| AP-2134 | **The demo world** (`fixtures/thingPages.ts`) has the 0.89 Summaries over the range asked (the hosts service's grid: 1 h and 6 h every 5 minutes, 24 h every 15, 7 d every 2 hours, 30 d every 6): Home Assistant as `ha.png` (100 of 102 integrations, nine notices, the disk's forecast, errors per point as bars), Hetzner Cloud as `hetzner.png` (its server with a small bar, a small line and Open, the note with “Add as a server”), UniFi, Mail (the generic Summary) and the heat pump's readings; the first tab is “Summary”. | The renderer shows something real in the app's demo mode and in tests. |
| AP-2135 | **The demo world's servers** borrow their kind's dashboard fixture (K's `dashboardFixtures`: pve-02 for Proxmox VE, blackbox for Unraid, the-titan for Incus, the-falcon for plain Linux) for their figures now, sensors, storages, `hosts.serverMetrics` and a guest's `hosts.guestMetrics` (a guest of the fixture's, picked by the id); their guests, hints, alerts and the kinds' detail stay the world's (`fixtures/serverDashboards.ts`), so every other screen keeps its story. | `serverDashboard` draws a real Summary in demo mode without replacing the world. |
| AP-2136 | **One read of a server**: the guest screen reads its server with the Summary's query (`range: 24h`, `SERVER_DASHBOARD_DETAIL_RANGE`), so opening a guest from its server's page reads nothing again; the server screen reads only through `useServerDashboard`. | K's note: the hook reads `hosts.getServer` with the range. |
| AP-2137 | **Rows keep their names on a narrow screen**: a table row's title and a bar's label don't shrink; the grey words after them do (to nothing when the figures and a button need the room; TalkBack still reads them). A list whose lines have marks (a check, a dot) keeps the room for one on every line, so titles line up. | Seen on the emulator: “whombat-prod” and “Disk 2” vanished behind their notes. |

Checked on the emulator (368 × 822 dp, light and dark): Home Assistant, Hetzner Cloud, pve-02 and blackbox in the demo
world; screenshots in `~/.cache/wb-tmp/dash-mobile/shots/`.
