# Monitoring on the phone (AP-9900…AP-9929)

John, 2026-11-24: Whombat monitors servers, networks and services (Whombat `docs/decisions/monitoring.md`, MON-01…MON-16
and each part's rows). This is the phone's part of the second wave, the same as the web's in the phone's own patterns,
built on the shared parts in `@whombat/client` (`home/monitoring.ts`: Keep quiet's choices and times, `holdView`,
`heldLine`, `thingStateWords`, `historyView`, `limitView`, `limitLine`, `lineLimit`; `react/monitoring.ts`:
`useKeepQuiet`, `useThingHistory`, `useSetWatchLimit`). The phone's own arrangement is `data/monitoring.ts` and
`screens/thing/Monitoring.tsx`; the demo world's states are `fixtures/monitoring.ts`.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-9900 | **Keep quiet… is in the page's ⋯.** A service page's ⋯ sheet gets a Keep quiet… button under its own actions; a page whose ⋯ would hold nothing else gets a 44pt ⋯ (iOS's own menu on iPhone, a sheet elsewhere) with Keep quiet… alone; a server's ⋯ has Rename, then Keep quiet…. Offered on a Whombat at session minor 9, to who may manage the thing (What Whombat watches' `mayChange`, or a window it may end). | One way in on every thing screen, as the web; the server decides who may. |
| AP-9901 | **The Keep quiet sheet** is the one-Whombat sheet (`OneSheet`): the thing's name as its badge, “Keep {name} quiet”, what it means, the choices as radio cards with until when under each (“Until 15:05”, “Until tomorrow 09:00”), Pick a time, an optional note, Keep quiet and Cancel. The choices are read when it opens, so “Until tonight” isn't offered late in the evening and nothing moves under the finger. A refusal stays in the sheet's note. | The phone's sheet for a decision with words; radio cards are its choices (phone-connect.md AP-2911). |
| AP-9902 | **Pick a time is a row of days and the clock**: Today, Tomorrow, then each weekday with its date for a week, as chips, and the `HH:MM` field (iOS's own compact time picker on iPhone). It starts two whole hours ahead, or at 09:00 tomorrow after 21:00. The shared `keepQuietBody` reads it in the Whombat's zone and says when it is past or more than a month ahead. | The phone has no date-and-time field; a week of days covers what people keep quiet for, and the server allows a month. |
| AP-9903 | **The hold under the head**: while a thing or a server is kept quiet, a bordered line under the head says its words (“Quiet until 23:00 · Moving the access points”), who kept it quiet under them, and a 44pt End now for who may end it; a server's sits above its readings. End now's toast says Whombat watches it again; Keep quiet's says until when, with Undo, which ends it. | MON-04, MON-63; the words are the server's. |
| AP-9904 | **A cause is the state**: a thing down because of something else shows the cause's words in its state pill (the server sends them as its badge, grey) and “Open media ›” under the head to the cause's page (a machine opens its own screen). Quiet with its server, which keeps its own state, says “Quiet while pve-02 is kept quiet” in the hold's line instead. The hold's heads-up line in Worth a look (`quiet-window`, `quiet-cause`) is left out where the page draws the hold. | MON-07, MON-68; one sentence once. |
| AP-9905 | **A held line** in Worth a look stays greyed with the shared words (“Kept quiet by Mara until 23:00”); one held by the window has End now (44pt) where Undo would be, one held by a cause has neither. | MON-63: Undo would only end the line's own quiet. |
| AP-9906 | **The week's line sits in the head**, by when it was checked (connections and devices) or after when its readings were taken (servers), and is left out of More detail, where the server also puts it for older apps. | MON-09: one line every page shows; behind More detail it is easy to miss on a phone. |
| AP-9907 | **What happened** is a card on the Summary, after the panels (a connection's) or after the readings (a server's): its 30 days' uptime as its line, the outages and problems newest first (still going with its tone's dot, ended grey), each with when and how long and its cause, five then “Show N more”, and a server's own alerts folded under them. “Nothing went wrong in the last 30 days.” when there is nothing; a Whombat before minor 9 shows no card. | MON-09, MON-51; the phone has no history tab, and the Summary is where people look first. |
| AP-9908 | **Change the limit** is in What Whombat watches on a server's Summary: under a check with a limit, “Warns above 90 %, the default” and each item with its own (“/mnt/backup above 95 %”), each with a 44pt Change; and in the ⋯ of a server's Worth a look line whose check has one, for its item, above Stop watching as the gentler choice, with what applies now under it (“Now 80 °C”). The sheet has the value with its unit, the default and the range, Save, Use the default (when it has its own) and Cancel; the toast says the new limit with Undo. | MON-15; where the person sees the check and the line it makes. |
| AP-9909 | **Lists say a held thing's state**: the Overview's tiles keep the shared line (“Quiet until 23:00”, a cause's words) over a chosen view's; a server's card says “Quiet until 23:00” under its system line, from Home's things. | MON-04, MON-07; the fleet's rows don't carry a window. |
| AP-9910 | **Website or address** asks only the fields that fit what it watches (`shownWhen`): a website its address, the word to look for and how often; a port, a ping or a name lookup their address and how often; a check-in how often and how late. Where it runs from is asked as any `fromService` choice (`field_choice_needed`: This Whombat and the servers it watches); a check-in always reports to this Whombat. | MON-01, MON-22; the same rule as the web's form. |
| AP-9911 | **The demo world** keeps UniFi quiet until 23:00 (its line held, End now), the-falcon too (its alerts held), Seerr down while its machine `media` on pve-02 is stopped, a week and 30 days of history per thing (pve-02 with incidents and its own alerts, most things with nothing), limits on every server (pve-02's `/mnt/backup` at 95 %), and Keep quiet, End now, Change the limit and its Undo working on any thing. | Every new state can be seen and pressed in a demo build. |
| AP-9912 | **A service page's strip folds held lines** (council round 1, 2026-11-25): Worth a look's lines quiet with the whole thing (kept quiet, or down because of something else) fold under their own “Show N on hold” after the strip's lines and their “Show N more”, which doesn't count them; End now stays on each. When every line is on hold, the card's name stands over the fold alone. | The same as a server's page and the web (AP-9933): the line under the head already says why. |
| AP-9913 | **Keep quiet's sheet keeps its buttons in sight**: Pick a time's days and clock share one row (the days scroll, the clock at the row's end), so with Pick a time open the whole sheet, Cancel included, fits an iPhone's tallest sheet (402 × 874 pt) without scrolling. iOS's sheet already keeps the home indicator's room under the last button, so the scroll view adds none; with larger text it scrolls to Cancel. | The days and the clock on two rows made the sheet taller than iOS lets it grow: Cancel sat under its edge until scrolled to. |
| AP-9914 | **What happened says each outage once**: a server's “isn’t checking in” alert that overlaps a `down` incident is left out (shared `historyView`), and the server's other alerts, open, sit under a small “From the server” heading after the outages and their “Show N more”. The demo world's offline alert uses the hosts service's own rule (`agent.heartbeat_missing`). | The same outage twice, with nothing to say which list a fold belonged to (web AP-9944). |
| AP-9915 | **A list says the window before the cause** (shared `thingStateWords`): kept quiet and down because of something else, a tile or row says “Quiet until 23:00”, as the page does. | Web AP-9943. |
| AP-9916 | **A refusal stays in the open sheet** (council round 2, 2026-11-25): when the Whombat refuses Keep quiet or a limit (a warning at or above the critical limit: “Set it below the critical limit of 90 °C.”), the sheet stays open and says why where it was asked: under the limit's field, or under Pick a time's row for Keep quiet. The shared hooks give the refusal's words back (`{ problem }`) and toast nothing; End now and Undo, which have no sheet, still toast. | The sheet is a native modal: a toast showed behind it, so a refused Save looked dead. Web AP-9946. |
| AP-9917 | **Pick a time's days fade at a side with more days past it**: the scrolling days fade into the sheet's colour over their last 32 pt on that side (`daysEdges`), so a day the edge cuts off reads as more to scroll to; nothing fades when every day fits or at the end of the scroll. The row and the clock stay on one row (AP-9913). | On a 402 pt iPhone the fourth day showed as a stray “(” beside “Time”. Spacing can't fix it: how many days fit depends on the phone's width and its text size. |
| AP-9918 | **The demo world refuses a warning at or above its critical limit**, as the hosts service does, in its words: a sensor's own critical limit is 95 °C there, wear's default 90 %, or the server's own when it has one; a critical limit at or below its warning is refused too. | So the refusal in the sheet can be seen and pressed in a demo build (AP-9911). |
| AP-9919 | **Overview counts a thing down because of something else as not answering** (council round 3, 2026-11-25): its tile stays grey with its cause, but Connections working leaves it out of the working (“4 of 5”, “1 not answering”, a grey part in the bar) and the Connections heading's line says “1 not answering”. Kept quiet, it counts as its dot says even when also down because of something else; one only quiet with its server isn't down. The shared `notAnswering`, `ownFace` and `overviewSections`. | “5 of 5” working above a tile saying “Not answering” contradicted itself. The window wins as on its tile: the person chose not to hear about it. Web AP-9948. |
| AP-9920 | **The server head's week line has no “·” of its own**: the row's gap sets it apart from when its readings were taken, as the thing page's head does. | On a narrow phone the row wraps and the week's line started its new line with a stray “·”. |
| AP-9921 | **A cause is said once on a thing's head even when its badge was cut**: the line under the state is drawn only when the shared `holdSaysBadge` says the pill doesn't already say it (`holdLineShown`). | The server cuts the cause's words to 60 characters for the badge; comparing whole words drew a second line with the same words. |
| AP-9922 | **Limits are shown read-only to who can't change them**: What Whombat watches on a server's Summary lists each check's limit and its items' for everyone, Change only for who may change them (`watchLimitLines`). | As on the web: what Whombat warns at is worth knowing whoever reads it. |
| AP-9923 | **The demo world's outages are ones Whombat makes**: pve-02 was running too hot (its critical alert at 95 °C), UniFi's gateway was offline; no warning (running hot, an access point offline) as an outage. | An incident opens only while a dot is red or a thing doesn't answer. Web AP-9950. |
