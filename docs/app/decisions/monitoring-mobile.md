# Monitoring on the phone (AP-9900…AP-9929)

John, 2026-11-24: Whombat monitors servers, networks and services (Whombat `docs/decisions/monitoring.md`, MON-01…MON-16
and each part's rows). This is the phone's part of the second wave, the same as the web's in the phone's own patterns,
built on the shared parts in `@whombat/client` (`home/monitoring.ts`: Keep quiet's choices and times, `holdView`,
`heldLine`, `thingStateWords`, `historyView`, `limitView`, `limitLine`, `lineLimit`; `react/monitoring.ts`:
`useKeepQuiet`, `useThingHistory`, `useSetWatchLimit`). The phone's own arrangement is `data/monitoring.ts` and
`screens/thing/Monitoring.tsx`; the demo world's states are `fixtures/monitoring.ts`.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-9900 | **Keep quiet… is in the page's ⋯.** A service page's ⋯ sheet gets a Keep quiet… button under its own actions; a page whose ⋯ would hold nothing else gets a 44pt ⋯ (iOS's own menu on iPhone, a sheet elsewhere) with Keep quiet… alone; a server's ⋯ has Rename, then Keep quiet…. Offered on a Whombat at session minor 9, to who may manage the thing (What Whombat watches' `mayChange`, or a window it may end). | One way in on every thing screen, as the web; the server decides who may. |
| AP-9901 | **The Keep quiet sheet** is the one-Whombat sheet (`OneSheet`): the thing's name as its badge, “Keep {name} quiet”, what it means, the choices as radio cards with until when under each (“Until 15:05”, “Until tomorrow 09:00”), Pick a time, an optional note, Keep quiet and Cancel. The choices are read when it opens, so “Until tonight” isn't offered late in the evening and nothing moves under the finger. A refusal stays in the sheet's note. | The phone's sheet for a decision with words; radio cards are its choices (phone-connect.md AP-2911). |
| AP-9902 | **Pick a time is a row of days and the clock**: Today, Tomorrow, then each weekday with its date for a week, as chips, and the `HH:MM` field (iOS's own compact time picker on iPhone). It starts two whole hours ahead, or at 09:00 tomorrow after 21:00. The shared `keepQuietBody` reads it in the Whombat's zone and says when it is past or more than a month ahead. | The phone has no date-and-time field; a week of days covers what people keep quiet for, and the server allows a month. |
| AP-9903 | **The hold under the head**: while a thing or a server is kept quiet, a bordered line under the head says its words (“Quiet until 23:00 · Moving the access points”), who kept it quiet under them, and a 44pt End now for who may end it; a server's sits above its readings. End now's toast says Whombat watches it again; Keep quiet's says until when, with Undo, which ends it. | MON-04, MON-63; the words are the server's. |
| AP-9904 | **A cause is the state**: a thing down because of something else shows the cause's words in its state pill (the server sends them as its badge, grey) and “Open media ›” under the head to the cause's page (a machine opens its own screen). Quiet with its server, which keeps its own state, says “Quiet while pve-02 is kept quiet” in the hold's line instead. The hold's heads-up line in Worth a look (`quiet-window`, `quiet-cause`) is left out where the page draws the hold. | MON-07, MON-68; one sentence once. |
| AP-9905 | **A held line** in Worth a look stays greyed with the shared words (“Kept quiet by Mara until 23:00”); one held by the window has End now (44pt) where Undo would be, one held by a cause has neither. | MON-63: Undo would only end the line's own quiet. |
| AP-9906 | **The week's line sits in the head**, by when it was checked (connections and devices) or after when its readings were taken (servers), and is left out of More detail, where the server also puts it for older apps. | MON-09: one line every page shows; behind More detail it is easy to miss on a phone. |
| AP-9907 | **What happened** is a card on the Summary, after the panels (a connection's) or after the readings (a server's): its 30 days' uptime as its line, the outages and problems newest first (still going with its tone's dot, ended grey), each with when and how long and its cause, five then “Show N more”, and a server's own alerts folded under them. “Nothing went wrong in the last 30 days.” when there is nothing; a Whombat before minor 9 shows no card. | MON-09, MON-51; the phone has no history tab, and the Summary is where people look first. |
| AP-9908 | **Change the limit** is in What Whombat watches on a server's Summary: under a check with a limit, “Warns above 90 %, the default” and each item with its own (“/mnt/backup above 95 %”), each with a 44pt Change; and in the ⋯ of a server's Worth a look line whose check has one, for its item. The sheet has the value with its unit, the default and the range, Save, Use the default (when it has its own) and Cancel; the toast says the new limit with Undo. | MON-15; where the person sees the check and the line it makes. |
| AP-9909 | **Lists say a held thing's state**: the Overview's tiles keep the shared line (“Quiet until 23:00”, a cause's words) over a chosen view's; a server's card says “Quiet until 23:00” under its system line, from Home's things. | MON-04, MON-07; the fleet's rows don't carry a window. |
| AP-9910 | **Website or address** asks only the fields that fit what it watches (`shownWhen`): a website its address, the word to look for and how often; a port, a ping or a name lookup their address and how often; a check-in how often and how late. Where it runs from is asked as any `fromService` choice (`field_choice_needed`: This Whombat and the servers it watches); a check-in always reports to this Whombat. | MON-01, MON-22; the same rule as the web's form. |
| AP-9911 | **The demo world** keeps UniFi quiet until 23:00 (its line held, End now), the-falcon too (its alerts held), Seerr down while its machine `media` on pve-02 is stopped, a week and 30 days of history per thing (pve-02 with incidents and its own alerts, most things with nothing), limits on every server (pve-02's `/mnt/backup` at 95 %), and Keep quiet, End now, Change the limit and its Undo working on any thing. | Every new state can be seen and pressed in a demo build. |
