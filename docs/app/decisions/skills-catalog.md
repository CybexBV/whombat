# A short skill catalog that speaks of the assistant (2026-10-18)

Whombat `docs/decisions/less-is-more.md` LM-02 and LM-03 (John, 2026-10-18: “Proceed with all your recommendations”),
the app's part; the server's and the packs' are LM-P01…LM-P08 there. Ten shipped packs list the assistant in their
`roles` and say “Teaches your assistant to…”; the other eleven stay shipped and installable under a collapsed “More
packs”. In the assistant shell no pack goes to the whole burrow. Builds on less-is-more.md AP-1795…AP-1809 (one Skills
page, a pack is one line, the assistant's action). No contract change.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-1847 | **The assistant shell's catalog, in three places, each pack once** (`assistantCatalog`, `shownInstalled`): INSTALLED lists every installed pack except a shipped one that isn't the assistant's and that the assistant doesn't have; SHIPPED WITH WHOMBAT lists the assistant's shipped packs (their `roles` hold `assistant`) that the burrow hasn't installed in any version; the rest — every other shipped pack the assistant doesn't have, installed or not — is under More packs. A pack the burrow has in any version is never offered in SHIPPED too (it used to be, after any version bump, and its Add answered “already attached”). | On John's box 19 shipped packs are installed from the crew's days: without this, Sales craft and Legal craft sat in INSTALLED with “Add to Dusty”, and every pack the release bumps would have shown twice. |

Nothing to show: no button; nothing in either list: no section (AP-1752). A member adds a pack the burrow has (it attaches the installed one) and reads “An owner adds it” for one it hasn't. | LM-02: an office crew's packs don't fit one assistant, but nothing is removed. |
