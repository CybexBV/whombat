# One Whombat on Android (AP-1350…AP-1379)

John, 2026-10-17: Whombat becomes one Whombat you talk to, connected to your servers, home and services (Whombat
`docs/decisions/one-whombat.md`, ONE-01…ONE-22). This is the phone's half, stream MA of the build
(`docs/implementation/one-whombat/assignment.md` §7.3 in Whombat): in assistant mode the app's tabs are **Home, Things,
Chats, Watch** (mock 4, `docs/design-handoff/one-whombat/phones.png` and `index.html` §4), with the approval sheet, a
server's page, task chats, Watch and the device skill card. Contracts 0.80.0 (CT-336…CT-343); no contract change. The
web's half is `one-whombat-shell.md` (WS) and `one-whombat-things.md` (WT); the pure models shared with them are copied
here until they land (AP-1372).

The phone uses the shared models and hooks: `@whombat/client`'s `home/` (greeting, Needs you intents, things' glyphs
and words, times, Watch's rules), `@whombat/client/hosts` (status words, a guest's kind, state and power) and the hooks
of `@whombat/client/react` (`home.ts`) and `@whombat/client/hosts/react`. The phone's own pure parts are in
`apps/mobile/src/data/` (`shell.ts`, `home.ts`, `watch.ts`, `chats.ts`, `servers.ts`, `deviceSkill.ts`; unit tested),
the screens `screens/{Home,Things,Thing,Server,Chats,Chat,Watch}Screen.tsx` with `NeedsYouSheet`, `GuestSheet` (since
PX-06 the guest's own screen, `proxmox-mobile.md`), `NewChatSheet`, the pieces in `ui/assistant.tsx`, `ui/oneSheet.tsx`, `ui/deviceSkillCard.tsx`, and the demo world in
`fixtures/assistant.ts`.
