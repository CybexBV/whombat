# Connections redesign, on Android (AP-1170…AP-1176)

John, 2026-10-11, finding Settings → Connections too long (the connected services, then “Found on your network”, then
every service you could add), approved a redesign: the Connections screen shows only what you have, problems first, and
“Add a connection” becomes its own screen with search and category chips. The web's rows are AP-1157…AP-1169. Android's
Connections stay read-only (Whombat connections-catalog.md CX-74): a catalog connection opens the native Connection
screen, everything else opens its page on the web, where people connect. Android has no “Found on your network” and
doesn't get one here. The model is pure, in `data/connections.ts` (`connectionsOverview`, `addConnectionRows`,
`addConnectionChips`, `addConnectionGroups`, `matchesConnectionSearch`, `addConnectionFooter`); both screens read through
`data/useConnections.ts`.

## Rows that say only where it is (AP-1177, with the web)

The same request as the web's AP-1177…AP-1180 (connections-redesign.md). A CONNECTED row's one mono line says where it
is — the host and port from the entry's address, else the account — and “Look only” when agents may only look
(“home.lan:8123 · Look only”); who looks after it and a gateway's line left the row (`ConnectedRow` loses `lookedAfter`
and `gateway`, `connectedRows` its options; the connection's screen still shows both). The title drops the address the
default label ends with (`labelName`), so “Home Assistant · home.lan:8123” is not said twice; TalkBack says the name,
the state, the address and Shared / Only you. The address comes first now (it was the server's detail first), as on the
web: a Nextcloud row shows `cloud.example.com`, not the account `mara`.
