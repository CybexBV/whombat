# Sign in at the box on Android (AP-3525…AP-3549)

John, 2026-10-20 (Whombat `docs/decisions/signin-at-box.md` BX-01…BX-08): “I want to loosen up the security a bit, when
we enroll a device we have to download a recovery kit, I don't want this anymore. If we have physical access to the
appliance we should be able to auth / re-enroll again.” This is stream M of Whombat's
`docs/implementation/signin-at-box/assignment.md`: on the phone, no kit step after the claim, the kit row quiet, and
“Use its screen” on “Let this device in” and “Set up this device” with its two screens. The shared step machine, the
session ops, the `screen` pin source and every word are the client's (`packages/client/src/managed/screen-signin.ts`,
`COPY.screen`; `signin-at-box-web.md` AP-3500…AP-3524). Not in the mocks: the phone follows its own pairing and setup
screens (the managed journeys' `Frame`).

The parts: `apps/mobile/src/managed/ManagedApp.tsx` (the route, `ScreenSignInFlow` for the identity and
`ScreenSignInSession` for the enrollment session), `managed/ScreenSignInScreens.tsx` (the way-in row, `ScreenSignInSteps`
over a session and its screens), `managed/screenSignIn.ts` (what the phone adds: the field and Android's back),
`managed/screens.tsx` (`PairScreen`, `SetupScreen`), `managed/ApplianceRoot.tsx` (the strip),
`managed/WhombatBackupsScreen.tsx` (RECOVERY KIT).

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-3525 | **No kit step after the claim** (BX-01): claim → where backups go (AP-260, skipped by itself when there's nothing to choose) → the Whombat. The new record says `kitPending: false`; an older record's `true` is never read (the kit route's `onDone` no longer writes it either). The kit route is only This Whombat's “Make a kit” now (`{ name: 'kit', device }`), back to the Whombat on Back and on Android's back. The claim's step kickers are the client's (“CLAIM · STEP 1 OF 2”, “2 OF 2”). | John: no download at enrollment; the box's screen is the way back now. |
| AP-3526 | **No kit reminder in the strip.** The strip under the status bar said “Recovery kit not saved” with “Save it” on every screen for an owner without a kit (MA-04 §4); it is gone, and with it the strip's `appliance.status` read. The strip keeps connection trouble, a passing note, the subscription and the app's update. | BX-01: no kit is not a warning, so nothing nags about it. |
| AP-3527 | **RECOVERY KIT on This Whombat → Backups is a quiet row**: “No recovery kit” with a muted dot and “Not needed: you can sign in at your Whombat’s screen. A kit helps if it has no screen.”, owners keep “Make a kit” (the existing kit flow, rotation included). The Overview's lists are the shared model's (`whombatOverview`): the row is AT A GLANCE (opening Backups), never NEEDS YOU; the phone's `saveKit` mapping stays for nothing to break. | BX-01, the same words and place as the web. |
| AP-3528 | **“Use its screen” on Let this device in**: a card with one row, “At your Whombat? Its screen can show a code for this device.” and a 44dp “Use its screen” button, between the computer's QR code (the easy path, AP-534) and “OR SHOW A CODE”, so the divider still sits on the pairing form it introduces; without the QR path it's above the email field. | The brief's “a row like the passkey row, above the email form”, on a phone whose pairing screen starts with the QR code. |
| AP-3529 | **On Set up this device** the way is listed in the card of ways (after a passkey's, before the links and the kit, the web's order), and “Use its screen” is a full-width secondary button under “Use a passkey”, as the passkey's way and button already are on the phone. | The phone's setup screen lists ways and puts their buttons under the list; the screen's way follows it. |
| AP-3530 | **The flow** is one route, `{ name: 'screen', device, back: 'pair' \| 'setup' }`, with two screens in the managed `Frame` (kicker “ADD THIS DEVICE”, the tip about who can see the screen): “Use your Whombat’s screen” with the email (prefilled with the signed-in email, the email keyboard) and “Show a code”; then “Type the code from its screen” with the letters, “Let this device in” and “Show a new code”. Back (the bar and Android's) goes from the letters to the email (kept) and from the email back to where it was asked from; Android's back leaves the flow from its other screens too (loading, a gate, the hard stop), since the signed-in tree's own handler passes on this route. A stop the machine can't get past (the Whombat too old, no screen, not the box whose screen the person sees) is the flow's own frame with those words as its line and Back. What was said about the last try goes as soon as something new is typed. | Two short steps like the pairing and the claim; back one step at a time, as the phone does elsewhere. |
| AP-3531 | **The letters field**: one field, DM Mono like the claim's letters, the keyboard in capitals (`autoCapitalize="characters"`), no autocorrect or spell check, `textContentType="oneTimeCode"` and `autoComplete="one-time-code"` for autofill, focused when the screen opens; what's typed is grouped `KXQ MTR` as it's typed (`formatScreenLettersInput`: spaces and dashes dropped, at most six), so `maxLength` is 7. “Let this device in” is enabled at six letters; the machine says “Type the six letters from the screen.” for a letter the box never shows. The field empties when the box shows other letters (a new code, or a wrong guess turned them) and keeps them otherwise (`lettersAfter`). After the field is emptied Gboard may show lower-case keys until the next word; what's typed is upper-cased anyway. | What the box shows is what's in the field; a wrong guess means new letters on the box, so the old ones are no use. |
| AP-3532 | **Which identity** (BX-03, `screenSignInPeer`): the phone's pin, or without one the directory's for this exchange only; a pin that differs from the directory is the identity-changed hard stop before anything is asked (with Forget, as everywhere, AP-64). A Whombat not in the organization or retired gets the recovery flow's gate. The enrollment session is opened by the flow (its own, like the pairing's) and closed when it's left; it leaves for the list when control ends it for good (AP-696). | The same rules as every other way in; nothing new is trusted before the box's tag matched. |
| AP-3533 | **Let in** (`enrolled`): the record is `screenSignedInRecord` (the pin as it was and enrolled, or a new `screen` pin of the identity the exchange proved), stored, made the last used, the passkey nudge due, and the Whombat opens — as a pairing approval does. `endpoint_enrolled` (`already`) opens a pinned Whombat; without a pin nothing was proven, so it goes back to Set up this device. | BX-03: the pin is written only after the box's own confirmation. |
| AP-3534 | **Checks.** Unit tests (`screenSignIn.test.ts`: the field after each step, Android's back); the machine, the words and the kit row are the client's tests. `pnpm check`. The debug APK on the `whombat-mobile` emulator (read-only instance on port 5624, run under `agent-resource run`, closed after), light and dark, through a throwaway preview root (not committed) that runs `ScreenSignInSteps` over a fake box answering with contracts 0.106.0's `screenSignInPakeResponder`, so CPace ran on Hermes both ways: Let this device in and Set up this device before and after, the email (and an email nobody has), the letters empty, part-typed (`kxq-mt` shows `KXQ MT`), a wrong guess (the field empties, the error goes on typing), the right letters (let in), Android's back from the letters to the email, the three stops (an older Whombat, no screen, a box answering with a tag that isn't the box's), and RECOVERY KIT. “Use its screen” measures 127 × 44dp. Shots: `~/.cache/wb-tmp/signin-at-box-mobile-shots/` (`sheet-*.png` side by side). Not run against a managed Whombat (none runs stream S's ops yet). | The logic is the shared machine's (tested there); the layout and the exchange on Hermes are checked on the device. |
