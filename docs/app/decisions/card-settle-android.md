# Cards that settle into a line, on Android (AP-746…AP-749)

The phone's side of `card-settle.md` (AP-730…AP-735); the rules are the shared `cardDisplay`, `foldIntoRuns`,
`workingLabel` and `threadWork` in `@whombat/client`, unchanged.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-746 | **A settled line is the run line's row; tapping it opens the card in place, and the card's “Hide card” returns the line.** `SettledLineView` (ui/chat.tsx): 44dp, 8dp dot in the tone (live in `progress`, muted in `mut`), the lead 14/600, ` · detail` in `ink2`, one line, a `ChevronDown` 16 in `mut` at the right; a button labelled `Show card: {lead}, {detail}` with `expanded: false`. Opened, the line is replaced by the card (`CardSummaryView` / `TurnFailedCardView`) with a 44dp `ChevronUp` “Hide card” (`expanded: true`) in its top right corner, in the card's first row — not absolutely positioned, so TalkBack reads it after the card's words. The open state is per message, local to the screen. A run that changed something adds a second row: its `PR #3` and `+12 −8` chips (mono 11.5 on `sf2`, `+` in `ok`, `−` in `bad`) under the text and “Open ›” to the run at their right; the chips row also opens the card but isn't a TalkBack stop of its own. | The line has to read like the run line John likes, and a phone has no room for chips and two controls on one row. Keeping the line on top of an opened card would have shown a live run's bar twice. |
| AP-747 | **The compact live card**: `Run #5 · Verify and deploy` (14.5/600) with the elapsed time (mono 12, ticking each second on the app clock), the 6dp bar, `Now: …`, then the chips, “Watch ›” (brand) and the 44dp open control. A run that needs a person is the existing full card. | AP-733. |
| AP-748 | **No Undo on an approval's line and no Cancel on a schedule's line on the phone.** The phone's approval card has no Undo (the approval screen has it, one tap from the opened card) and the schedule card is “cancelled on the web” (AP-691); “where the card has it” keeps them where they were. | Nothing new to decide on the phone; the card opens in place. |
| AP-749 | **What a run did folds under it in the conversation and the thread** (`chatItems` uses `foldIntoRuns`): hidden messages aren't rows (keys stay message ids; the author line goes to the next shown message); their lines are indented 18dp, under the run's dot, and open their own card (a learned skill's Remove included). The phone opens links to a thread or conversation, never to one message, so a link to a folded message lands on the list its run is in. **The thinking row** reads `workingLabel` from `useAgentNow` on the app clock, ticking only while the agent reports actions there; TalkBack gets the words without the clock. **A thread's reply row** (`ThreadRepliesRow`) is one link: `25 replies ›`, the agent's 18dp face, `Banjo is working · 38%` with a 40dp bar, `Banjo needs you` in warn/600, or `Banjo is thinking` with small dots. Its runs: `runs.list?status=live` (cached, patched by `run.updated`), asked for only when a root on the screen has replies; its thinking: the conversation's typing cache, all threads. The demo world has the case with `EXPO_PUBLIC_WHOMBAT_SETTLE=1` (`fixtures/settle.ts`: #incidents' report thread, a deploy thread with a live run, a thread where Pippa thinks). | AP-732, AP-734, AP-735 on the phone; one query for the burrow's live runs instead of one per thread. |
