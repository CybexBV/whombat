# Settings → This Whombat in five parts (AP-1221…AP-1236)

John, 2026-10-11: “The page https://dev.whombat.com/settings?tab=appliance has soo much stuff going on, how can we
improve this / clean up the page / create sections / tabs / ? What would you do to improve this?” In the managed fake
world (app 0.73.0, an owner, 1440 × 900) the tab was 4,924 px, about five and a half screens, with 21 section labels:
How it’s doing (one and a half screens of charts) first, the only real warning (“Unprotected” backups) two and a half
screens down, one-row sections each with a label and a card (Web browser, Audit log, Connection, Recovery kit, Updates,
This device), Devices spread over five blocks, and four forms always open (the backup file's ten checkboxes and
password, the network list's add row, the device code, the diagnostics checklist). I proposed five parts with an
Overview that starts with what needs you (a mockup) and he said “I like it, yes implement fully”. This supersedes the
order of settings-simplify.md SS-44 and box-status.md AP-1137's “How it’s doing first” (it is first on the Overview).
The phone's half is `this-whombat-sections-android.md` (AP-1240…AP-1255). No contracts change.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-1221 | **Superseded by settings-groups.md STG-02 (Overview · Backups · Screen · About).** **Five parts.** This Whombat is split into **Overview · Backups · Devices & owners · Agents’ access · About**, a `SegmentedControl` (`soft`, the range control's look) under the Settings tabs, at `?tab=appliance&section=backups\|devices\|access\|about` (Overview without `section`). Each part fits about one screen. The parts' words and which ones show are `@whombat/client/managed`'s (`this-whombat.ts`: `WHOMBAT_PARTS`, `whombatParts`, `THIS_WHOMBAT_TEXT`), shared with the phone. | Settings keeps its seven tabs (SS-01); a second level inside the busiest one instead of a sixth screen of scrolling. A control that looks unlike the tabs above says it is a level down. |
| AP-1222 | **Which parts show.** Overview, Agents’ access and About always; Backups on a managed Whombat or where the backup file is offered (`backupFileAvailable`); Devices & owners on a managed Whombat. A `section=` that isn't shown falls back to the Overview. | Only parts with something in them (SS-06's “only the sections that apply”). |
| AP-1223 | **Overview = NEEDS YOU, HOW IT’S DOING, AT A GLANCE.** `whombatOverview()` puts what is wrong **and the viewer can fix** in NEEDS YOU (dot, title, a line, one button), worst first; everything else becomes one line in AT A GLANCE (dot, title, line, chevron), a link to its part. A problem the viewer can't fix (a member seeing “Unprotected”) is a glance row with its warn dot, never a button they can't press. NEEDS YOU is left out when empty. | The old page read the same whether anything was wrong or not; this answers “is it fine, and what do I do” in the first screen. |
| AP-1224 | **What needs you** (managed): no backup destination → “Backups aren’t set up · If this Whombat breaks, its data can’t be restored.” **Set up backups** (opens Backups with the destination form open); backups failing or behind → their words with **Open backups**; no recovery kit → “Recovery kit not saved” **Save a recovery kit** (the kit flow at once); someone waiting for an owner to let them back in → “{name} is waiting for you” **Open devices**; the viewer is the only owner → “You’re the only owner · Keep at least two, so someone can always get in.” **Add an owner** (Settings → Members); passkeys offered and the viewer has none → “No passkey yet · Open this Whombat on a new device with one touch.” **Add a passkey** (Devices & owners, at its passkeys). Owner-only items need the viewer to be an owner of the Whombat (`appliance.status.owner`). | Each is a real risk or a one-touch improvement, and each button is the step that fixes it. |
| AP-1225 | **Superseded in part by about-page.md AP-9960 (the row that opens About is titled “About”).** **At a glance**, in this order and only when known: the update's first row (“Up to date · Software 2026.9.2”, or what it waits for, or why it failed, with its dot) → About; the plan (`licenceSummary`, the plan named in the title and the limited usage after the date: “Team plan · Active” over “Valid until 24 October 2026 · Members 1 of 5”) → About; backups (“Backed up · Last backup 3 hours ago”, “Waiting for the first backup”) → Backups; the recovery kit → Backups; devices (“2 devices · Owners: Mara Okafor, Hugo Berg”) → Devices & owners; agents' access (“Web browser available · Agents may reach your local network”) → Agents’ access; on a Whombat without an update row, the version (“App 0.74.0 · Whombat 2026.9.2”) → About. | One line per part says it is fine; the chevron goes where the details are. |
| AP-1226 | **Badges.** A part with items in NEEDS YOU shows their count on its segment (a small `--warn` pill; the segment's accessible name says “Backups, 1 needs you”). | The way back to a problem from any part. |
| AP-1227 | **Superseded in part by settings-groups.md STG-10 (no Tokens chart).** **How it’s doing on the Overview: the four tiles**, with the range on the right as before, and **Show charts** (a link-style button, `aria-expanded`) that opens Processor, Network speed, Data per day, Disk and Tokens under them; Hide charts closes them. Opened stays opened for the visit (`sessionStorage`). A self-hosted server (Tokens only) shows Tokens without the button. Owners only, as before (BS-01). | The tiles answer “is it OK”; the charts are for when you look closer. One and a half screens became one row. |
| AP-1228 | **Backups part:** BACKUPS (as before, with its actions), RECOVERY KIT, then BACKUP FILE as one card with two rows, “Download a backup” and “Restore from a file”, each with its intro and a button that opens a dialog (`Modal md`) holding the flow as it was (the checklist, password, making, ready / choose, upload, password, preview, restore, outcome). The maker and the restorer live in the card, not the dialog: closing it mid-way keeps the work, and the row's line says where it is (“Making the file · 40 %”, “Ready · 1.8 MB”, “Uploading · 62 %”, “Restored”). | The checklist was a third of a screen open all the time for something done rarely. Work that takes minutes mustn't be lost by closing a window. |
| AP-1229 | **Superseded by settings-groups.md STG-02 (Devices is You’s).** **Devices & owners part:** DEVICES is one list: this browser first (“Chrome on Windows · This browser”), then the others with Remove; under them one row “Add a device · Open a link on the new device, or type the code it shows.” with **Add a device** (the link, as before) and **Enter a code** (the code field opens in place, then the asking device as before). Then PASSKEYS, people waiting to get back in, REMOVED DEVICES (moved here from the old main list), OWNERS. | Five blocks about devices were one subject; the code field was always open for something done once per device. |
| AP-1230 | **Superseded by settings-groups.md STG-04, STG-03 (the switch on What it may do, the ranges in Advanced).** **Agents’ access part:** WEB BROWSER, LOCAL NETWORK (the switch), the network list (its add row behind **Add network**, opened at once when the list is empty), WORKING DIRECTORY (self-hosted), and what a managed Whombat turns off, under **TURNED OFF HERE** (was “NOT ON A MANAGED WHOMBAT”). When none of these apply: “Nothing to set here on this Whombat.” | All of it is about what agents may reach; the old label read like a warning about the page. |
| AP-1231 | **Superseded in part by settings-groups.md STG-09, STG-03 (versions under Technical details; audit log, diagnostics and the fingerprint in Advanced).** **About part:** VERSION, UPDATES (with the oldest app builds let in), PLAN (was SUBSCRIPTION, with Check again), CONNECTION (fingerprint), AUDIT LOG, DIAGNOSTICS. | Facts and tools you look up, not things to watch. |
| AP-1232 | **Diagnostics in plain words, behind a button.** The card shows the intro and the files made, with **Make a file for support…** opening a dialog with the checklist and Make the file. The scopes keep their names; their lines are the app's (`THIS_WHOMBAT_TEXT.diagnostics`), not the contract's developer list: Versions “Which versions of Whombat and its parts are running.”; Health “Whether each part works, how long it’s been running and whether AI sign-ins work. Not whose they are.”; Updates “What’s running, what’s next, and why an update waited or failed.”; Backups “Whether backups work and when the last one ran. Not where they go, and nothing from them.”; Connection “How well this Whombat stayed connected over the last 7 days.”; Errors “How often something went wrong over the last 7 days, counted by kind. Nothing from your conversations.” | CLAUDE.md “talk like people”: “draining/quarantine”, “restore-drill result” and “relay connects” meant nothing to an owner. What a scope contains doesn't change. |
| AP-1233 | **Old addresses.** `?tab=devices` and `?tab=appliance#devices` go to `section=devices` (`settingsRedirect`); the account menu's version link opens About (superseded in part by account-menu.md ACM-05: its “Update ready” does); a passkey notice opens Devices & owners; a push about an update, a backup or security opens the Overview (its NEEDS YOU). Switching parts replaces the address (like the tabs) and drops the hash. | Links from before keep landing where their content is. |
| AP-1234 | **Members** see the same parts with what each section already allowed them (read-only rows, their own devices and passkeys); NEEDS YOU only holds what they can do (their passkey). | SS-07. |
| AP-1235 | **Tests and checks.** `this-whombat.test.ts` (the parts, NEEDS YOU and AT A GLANCE for owners and members, badges, order, parsing `section`); the web tests find their sections in their part (`?section=`), the Overview's tiles and Show charts, the backup file and diagnostics dialogs; `search.test.ts` for `section` and the redirects; the managed e2e specs open the right part. | |
| AP-1236 | **Released as whombat-app 0.74.0** with the phone's half: version bump, tag `app-v0.74.0`, `pnpm release:dist` from the tag, APK on DEV. | A visible change on both clients; no contracts change. |
