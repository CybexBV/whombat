# Dashboards: the client model (2026-10-18)

Stream K of Whombat `dashboards.md` (DS-01…DS-19): a server's Summary built in `@whombat/client` as the same
`ThingPagePanel`s a connection's page is made of (DS-02, contracts 0.89.0 CT-394…CT-398), the shared layout helper, the
fourth chart colour, and fixtures of John's world. The web (stream W, AP-2050…) and the phone (stream M, AP-2120…) draw
them. The mocks John approved are `docs/design-handoff/dashboards/server.png` (pve-02) and `unraid.png` (blackbox).

| Id | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-2000 | **The API** (`@whombat/client/hosts`, `hosts/dashboard.ts`, words in `dashboard-words.ts`): `SERVER_DASHBOARD_RANGES` (all six; `SERVER_DASHBOARD_PHONE_RANGES` without 90 d), `parseDashboardRange`, `DASHBOARD_RANGE_WORDS`, `SERVER_DASHBOARD_LIVE_WORDS`, `serverDashboardKind`, `serverDashboardTabs(kind)`, `serverDashboardSeries(detail)`, `serverDashboardVolumeSeries(detail)`, `dashboardGuests(detail)`, `serverDashboard({detail, series, guests, volumes, range, timeZone, now})`, `serverDashboardHead(detail)`, `serverControlLevel`, the words helpers `seriesStats`, `dashboardNumber`, `peakWhen`, `volumeForecast`, `sizePair`, `rateWords`, the links `serverTabHref`/`serverGuestHref`/`serverConsoleHref`; `placePanels` and `thingSeriesToken` live with the other page helpers in `@whombat/client` (connections use them too) and are re-exported here; the hook `useServerDashboard({burrow, serverId, range, timeZone})` is in `@whombat/client/hosts/react`. The old `seriesStats(series, key)` of `chart.ts` (unused) gave its name to the new one. | One builder for both clients; a skeleton first so W and M build against it while the builders fill in. |
| AP-2001 | **`chart4` is violet**: `#6e3bb4` light, `#8450d4` dark, in `chartColors` (CSS `--chart4`, native `theme.chart.chart4`), outside `ThemeColors` (the feedback widget's budget). Validated with the dataviz validator as a categorical set with chart1…3 on each theme's `sf`, every pair (blue and violet meet in Disk I/O and the temperatures): lightness band, chroma, normal-vision ΔE ≥ 15 to blue (15.5 light, 15.4 dark), CVD pass in dark and the existing orange↔green pair the only warning in light; 3:1 on `bg` and `sf` is a test. The mock's lighter lavender fails against blue for colour-blind readers, so it is darker. `thingChartModel`'s right-axis colour is now `--chart4` instead of `--role-researcher`. | DS-18; colours only through tokens and computed, not eyeballed. |
| AP-2003 | **`placePanels(panels)`** → `{top, chart: {panel, span}[][], main, side, bottom}`: each place keeps the panels' order; chart rows fill three columns and a card that doesn't fit starts the next row; a panel without a place gets `thingPanelPlace(panel, afterChart)` where “after a chart” means a chart-placed or chart-kind panel came before. It doesn't stretch cards: the builders give spans that fill their rows. | DS-04's rules in one pure place for both clients. |
| AP-2004 | **`thingSeriesToken(color, index)`**: blue/orange/green/violet → `chart1…4`, amber → `warn`, red → `bad`, grey → `mut`; no colour → `chart1…4` by index. | §2 of the brief: one mapping, two renderers. |
| AP-2005 | **`useServerDashboard`** reads `hosts.getServer` with `range: 24h` (the web's server page reads the same, so one read serves the head and the Summary; the page's range drives the history, not the page), `hosts.serverMetrics` with the range and `serverDashboardSeries` (one comma list cut at the API's 500 characters) every 60 s with the previous range kept while a new one loads, and `hosts.guestMetrics` (`cpu_pct`) for the five busiest guests. The live `hosts` events already refetch the server's page. | DS-16; no extra polling for what is already live. |
| AP-2006 | **Words** (DS-15): averages and peaks with one decimal under 10 and none above, so the mock's “Avg 14.2 %” reads “Avg 14 %” (DS-15 wins over the mock's made-up figure); the peak's time in the person's zone — “Now” when it is the newest point, “Sun 03:10” within six days, else “6 Oct” (day before month, as DS-15's “Full about 18 Dec”); sizes in the total's unit, GB or TB, but GB for both while less than a terabyte is used (“310 of 1,000 GB” as the Unraid mock, “2.1 of 7.2 TB”); network in Mb/s (Gb/s from 1000); what a range moved as “Total **38 GB** in · **11 GB** out” (the mock's “Today” is wrong for other ranges). | One way to say a number on every page. |
| AP-2007 | **Places per kind** (DS-09 and the mocks): top the banner (only with the hub's `banner`) and the figures; chart row 1 CPU · Memory · Temperatures, row 2 Network (span 2) · Disk I/O — without sensors Network moves up and Disk I/O takes row 2 (span 3), without network or disk I/O points the row closes up. Unraid keeps the mock's one row: CPU · Memory · Disk temperatures. Main: Unraid's Array, then the busiest guests (containers on Docker hosts and Unraid, “Virtual machines” with the stopped ones after on QEMU/KVM). Side: Worth a look, then Proxmox's Backups and Cluster, the kind's pools (Unraid, Incus, libvirt's storage pools or VM folders), Unraid's Virtual machines, the kind's facts (Unraid, Incus, QEMU/KVM — what the phone's “About it” and the web's cards showed). Storage (Proxmox's storages, else the host's pools and file systems; not on Unraid, whose array is its storage) whole width at the bottom in up to three columns as the mock, or in the main column when there is no guests table, so a plain host's page has no empty column. | The mock's layout; nothing stretches and no column stays empty. |
| AP-2008 | **Figures**: CPU (“of 16 threads · load 2.9”, blue line), Memory (“41 / 64 GB”, “64 % used · 9 GB cache”, violet line), Hottest (sensor and its limit, amber line) when there are sensors, Network (Mb/s now, in and out, green line), Guests (“12 of 14 running”, the running mix, a meter; “Containers” on Docker hosts and Unraid with “N stopped” / “1 VM running”, “VMs” on QEMU/KVM), and Storage only when Hottest or Guests is missing (at most five, as the mock). Unraid's are the mock's: CPU · Memory · Array (“18 / 24 TB”, “76 % · parity valid”) · Cache (the pool called cache, else the first) · Containers. Tones only near a limit: CPU from 80/95 %, memory 85/95 % (the phone's tiles), sensors from 85 % of their limit, disks 80/90 %, Unraid pools 80/90 %, Incus and libvirt pools 85/95 %, Unraid parity disabled or a disk missing red. | DS-07 with the thresholds the old screens used. |
| AP-2009 | **Chart cards**: figure now with what it is out of, “Avg”/“Peak … Sun 03:10” from the hosts service's own `summary` when it sends one (over raw samples), else the points; CPU's foot User · System · I/O wait · Load; Memory's Guests (running guests' memory) or Used · ZFS ARC or Cache · Free · Swap (amber from half used); Temperatures up to four sensors hottest first in amber, blue, green, violet, the dashed line at the hottest's limit (“85 °C limit”), axis from `tempScale` with `°` ticks, foot “71 / 85 °C”; Unraid's Disk temperatures draw the HDD/SSD/NVMe sensors with the hottest disk now (“39 °C hottest, disk 3”), “Limit 55 °C” (Unraid's hot mark; 70 °C when it has only SSDs) and how many disks are spun down. While the history loads the cards are there without lines (nothing moves when it comes). | DS-06. |
| AP-2010 | **Worth a look**: open alerts (critical first), then the hints (warnings first), the banner's alert not again; each with one way out — an alert “Look into it” (`ask`, “<title>. Can you look into it?”, as the old notices), a hint its first fix (`hintFixes`, `unraidHintFixes` without Manage) as a tab link (only tabs the kind has), a guest's page (`?guest=…&gtab=backups\|snapshots`), Unraid's own page (`url`), else the guest it names, else its section's tab. Unraid's start/stop array, check parity and plugin updates become links to Array and Plugins, where those buttons are; a notification's Archive links System (W moves “From Unraid” and Updates there, DS-17); Unraid's notes the hints leave out (normal importance) are one line “N notes from Unraid”. Labels fit 30 characters (“Open it”, “Back it up” when a guest's name is long). With something in it, Worth a look comes right after the figures in the panels' order (the phone's stack); empty, it comes after the main column with “Nothing needs a look.”. Acknowledging (“Remind me tomorrow”) stays on Watch. | DS-05, DS-09: the Summary shows, the tabs do. |
| AP-2011 | **Busiest guests table**: five, busiest first (running and unhealthy only; QEMU/KVM lists its VMs, stopped after); columns Guest (mono name linking its page, under it “CT 131”, “VM”, the OS of a libvirt VM, a Docker container's first tag, “This Whombat”, “restart to apply 1 change”), CPU (meter + “6.1 %”), Memory (“3.0 / 8 GB”), the range (“24 h”: the guest's own `cpu_pct` small line, a dash without one), and a button: Console (`?console=<guest>`, when the guest has one, the server allows consoles and is online) else Open. A dot only when it isn't simply running. | DS-08/DS-09; the console address the web uses today. |
| AP-2012 | **Forecasts** (DS-08): the hosts service's `fullInDays` first (it drives the “full soon” alerts, so both agree), else a least-squares line through the volume's last 30 days (`useServerDashboard` reads `vol_pct:*` for 30 d every 10 min, `ServerDashboardInput.volumes`; without it the range's points, at least a day of them), the hub's rule: growing at least 0.01 % a day and full within a year. Under a bar: “Full about 10 Nov” (amber, within 60 days) or “Full in about 5 months”; nothing when it isn't filling up — DS-15 over the mock's “Not filling up”. An Unraid data disk past 80 % says “89 % full” when no forecast. | Storage is the most common problem; words that don't depend on the page's range. |
| AP-2013 | **The head** (`serverDashboardHead`): the badge “N to look at” (open alerts and warning hints) while the server isn't fine, Unraid's “Array started” while it is, else the status words (“Offline” for an offline server, whatever its old note); chips: the system (“Proxmox VE 9.0”, “Unraid 7.1”, “Debian 13”), the kind (“Incus 6.0”, “QEMU/KVM”, “Docker”), the control level from the switches (`serverControlLevel` → “Full control”, “Restart and consoles”, “Watch only”); facts “**Ryzen 7 5700G** · 8 cores / 16 threads”, “**64 GB** DDR4”, “Up **12 d 4 h**”; the address; “Agent synced 6 s ago” or “Last heard 2 h ago”. | DS-01 and the mock's head. |
| AP-2014 | **What the old Summaries showed, and where it is now**: the web's figures, temperature chart, busiest guests and hints (`SummaryTab`, `HintsCard`) and the phone's tiles, Needs a look and facts are in the panels; Unraid's array card (state, use, parity) is the Array bars with its parity line and the Array figure, its pools card the Pools bars, its updates and notifications are Worth a look lines linking Plugins, the guest and System; the Incus and QEMU/KVM cards (version, projects, networks, firmware) are the kind's facts and pools; “Back up now” opens the guest's Backups; the phone's Asks you first, Also connected and Ask stay the screens' own. `LibvirtSummary` is a VM's own page, not a server's, and stays (DS-17). | DS-09's “everything stays reachable”. |
| AP-2020 | **Fixtures** (`@whombat/client/hosts/fixtures`: `dashboardFixtures(now)`, `dashboardFixtureAnswer`, `withDashboardFixtures(handle, now)`): pve-02 (the mock's 14 guests, home-assistant CT 131, local-lvm full in about 5 months, last night's backup, the cluster), blackbox (Unraid 7.1: parity, three WD Red disks, disk 1 at 89 %, the NVMe cache mirror, 16 containers, the pbs VM), the-titan (Incus 6.0 with the dev box as This Whombat, /srv/ci filling up), kvm-lab (five VMs, the 88 % pool), the-falcon (Ubuntu VPS without sensors or guests, an alert about /srv) and raspi (a Docker host). History on the hosts service's grid for every range from the same curves: a quiet day, the nightly backup at 03:10 Amsterdam as the peak, storages growing as their forecasts say, the service's own `summary`. Opt-in for the fixture servers (the main world's tests keep their pve-02 and blackbox). | Deterministic data drawn like the mocks, usable by tests, the web and the phone. |
