# A service's Summary in slots on the phone (AP-7130…AP-7159)

John, 2026-11-04, approved the Plex mockups (`~/.cache/wb-tmp/mockups/plex-redesign/`, its `#phone` and `#foldable`)
and said “Implement this fully”. The contract and the slots are Whombat `docs/decisions/service-slots.md` (SLT-01…SLT-12);
this is how the phone app (iPhone, Android, open foldables) draws them. The logic is `src/data/serviceSlots.ts`, the
cards `src/screens/thing/Slots.tsx`.

| id | decision |
|---|---|
| AP-7130 | **A page in slots is one with a slot look** (a `gallery` with `look`, a `list` with `look`; `isSlotPage`). Its cards head themselves with their name at 17/700 and their meta beside it (`OneCard`'s `heading` look, set for the page through `OneCardLookContext`), so Live, Inventory, Shelf, Trend, People and Machine read as one page; every other page keeps the mono labels. `OneCard` takes the cards' `meta` itself, so it can stand beside the heading. |
| AP-7131 | **Superseded in part by phone-refresh-status.md AP-7464 (the head’s lines, More detail).** **A service's head** (SLT-10, any page with `open`): its mark and name (30/700), its badge as a pill in its tone under the name, the verdict under it — its facts and address only when it has no verdict —, “Runs on” and what couldn't be read, then two 46dp buttons sharing the width: Ask {assistant} (filled) and Open {service}. The Summary's “Ask about Plex” at the bottom goes, the head has it. |
| AP-7132 | **Restart and the page's own buttons (Check for updates) sit behind a 44dp ⋯ at the end of the back bar** on a service's page, in a sheet named after it with Close; pressing one closes the sheet first, so its question isn't hidden behind it. A page without any has no ⋯. |
| AP-7133 | **Worth a look on top is a strip** (SLT-08, any notices panel placed `top`, a server's “Right now” too): tinted in its worst line that still asks (`stripTone`; a heads-up only: no tint), each line with its icon on a tinted 36dp tile, the card's name in mono capitals over the first line, the title closed with a full stop and the detail as one sentence (`stripSentence`), its link (an outlined 30dp button with a chat or outside mark, padded to 44) and its button under the words; the ⋯ menu (held on the words too, iOS's own menu on iPhone) or a quiet line's Undo on the right. |
| AP-7134 | **The strip's order and fold**: what asks (and a heads-up with a button) first, then the heads-ups, then quiet lines (`stripOrder`), all in the strip — none folded away under “Show 2 heads-ups” as in the side card —, the first three, then “Show N more” (`foldStrip`). Without a line there is no strip at all. “Ask {assistant} to handle these” stays at its foot when two lines or more ask. |
| AP-7135 | **Live rows on a phone are cards to swipe** (SLT-02): 318dp wide (narrower when the card is), snapping, the next card's edge showing, page dots under them that VoiceOver and TalkBack read as “1 of 3”; one item alone takes the width. A card on `sf2`: its 74dp poster, title 16.5/600, sub, the person's initial in a round mark with “name · note”, its tag (mono, on a tint of its tone), then the progress across the card in the progress colour — `mut` while `paused` — with `note` (its part before “ of ” stronger, `noteParts`) at the start and `noteEnd` at the end. The card is its item's link; its buttons sit under it. |
| AP-7136 | **Live in a wide page's half is compact rows stacked** (mock “Rows turn compact”): a 50dp poster, the words, the progress under them across the row, the tag at the progress line's end instead of `noteEnd`. |
| AP-7137 | **The shelf on a phone is one row of 104dp posters to swipe** (SLT-04); in a wide page's half three across, two rows at most and never a short last row (`shelfShown`: six of twelve). Who asked wears a tag on the picture (their initial and `note`, else their name; `shelfTag`), solid `sf` so it reads over any poster. The panel's link (“See all in Plex”) is in its head. |
| AP-7138 | **Counts on a phone are tiles two by two** (SLT-03): on `sf2`, the icon and the name, the count 24/700, what changed under it in its tone (`figure.note`, else the line's detail); in a wide page's half rows with a 38dp icon tile, the detail under the name and the count with its change on the right. A list without counts' `figure` shows `right` (`countTiles`). |
| AP-7139 | **A count's own button (Scan) is a small icon button**, 30dp padded to 44, in the tile's top corner and beside the row (`ThingIconButton`, named “Scan Movies”, greyed with its reason while scanning), not a long press: it is seen and reached with VoiceOver and TalkBack without a menu. The panel's buttons (Scan all) sit in its head. |
| AP-7140 | **New icons for a library's kind**: film (Film), tv (Tv), music (Music), photo (Image), video (Video), in `screens/thing/noticeIcons.ts`, which Worth a look's lines and the counts share. |
| AP-7141 | **People** (SLT-05): a bar whose line has `person` draws a 30dp round mark with its initial (`HumanAvatar`, named), the name over its note, the figure on the right in mono and a thin bar (4dp, a lighter chart colour) under the words. |
| AP-7142 | **A chart with `labels`** (SLT-06) puts one word under each bar instead of times, whatever the page's range; the readout says the label (“Today”), and the last bar and its word stand out (the others lighter) until a finger picks another (`ColumnChart`'s `accent`). A line chart with labels takes them as its words. |
| AP-7143 | **On an open foldable or a tablet a page in slots is two columns** (SLT-09; `slotColumns`), from the same width a page's cards go into columns (AP-6741): what is on top and isn't Worth a look across, then on the left the panels spanning two or three columns and after them the main column's, on the right the strip first, then the one-column panels and the side column's, then the bottom across. Phones stack the panels in the order given. |
| AP-7144 | **Each column sits in its own half of an open foldable**: the page measures where it is in the window and, with a fold reported (`screenShape`'s `fold`), sizes the columns so the fold falls in a 28dp room between them (`halfWidths`); without a fold, or with one far off the page's middle, the columns share the width. Cards inside know they are in a half (`SlotHalfContext`): rows instead of swiping and tiles. |
| AP-7145 | **People stays its own card** beside This week rather than inside it as in the mock: the contract sends it as its own panel with its own title and meta, and a client that merged them would hide them. The machine card draws as any bars card (its icons, small line and words on the right are the mock's examples, SLT-12). |
| AP-7146 | **On a wide page the service head's two buttons stand beside the name**, in the right half and as wide as their words (mock “Two halves, one page”), the name, badge and verdict in the left half; on a phone they share the width under the verdict. |

AP-7130…AP-7146 were checked with `pnpm check` (the strip's order and fold, the live rows, shelf tags, count tiles, the
columns and halves and the day words unit-tested in `serviceSlots.test.ts`) and the demo world in a debug build on the
Android emulator (368 × 822 dp at 3×), light and dark: the head with Ask and Open and its ⋯ sheet, the strip, the
stream cards with page dots, the library tiles with their Scan, the shelf, the week with “Today” standing out, people
with their initials and the machine. The open foldable was the same emulator set to 860 × 1080 dp with the list hidden:
Now playing as rows and the shelf three across on the left, the strip, the libraries as rows, the week, people and the
machine on the right, the buttons beside the name. That emulator reports no fold, so the columns shared the width; where
a fold puts them (`halfWidths`) is unit-tested only. Neither the iPhone Duo simulator nor an Android foldable was run.
