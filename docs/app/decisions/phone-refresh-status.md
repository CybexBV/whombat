# Phone refresh: status, Overview, headers and service pages (AP-7450…AP-7499)

From the 2026-11-05 design council on the phone apps (three reviewers, two rounds; its final spec §3, §4, §5 and §8 are
this file's) and John's “Yes” to doing it. The web's refresh of the day before (`docs/decisions/ui-refresh-status.md`,
`ui-refresh-chat.md`) is the model: the phone uses its shared helpers (`statusLevel.ts`, `headFacts`, `ranOnSwitches`)
and words. The approvals, Chat's screen reader work, the Inbox and Settle, the reply row and the composer are the other
half of the refresh (`phone-refresh-*.md` beside this one).

Code: `packages/tokens/src/native.ts`; `apps/mobile/src/ui/statusMark.tsx`, `BackGlyph(.ios).tsx`, `headers.tsx`,
`UnderlineTabs.tsx`, `tabsLayout.ts`; `data/home.ts`, `homeServers.ts`, `statusOrder.ts`, `useStatusOrder.ts`,
`needsYou.ts` (the tab badge), `chatRuntime.ts`, `dashboard.ts` (`rangePlace`), `thingPage.ts`; `screens/HomeScreen.tsx`,
`home/ServersCard.tsx`, `home/ServicesCard.tsx`, `ThingPageScreen.tsx`, `thing/Panels.tsx`, `ThreadScreen.tsx`,
`ChatRoomScreen.tsx`; `navigation/TabBar.tsx`; `app.config.cjs`. `headFacts` moved from the web into `@whombat/client`
(`home/thingPage.ts`). No contract, schema or migration change.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-7450 | **The native theme carries `warnText` and `okText`** (`NativeTheme.colors`, from the tokens' `statusTextColors`, as the web's `--warn-text`, `--ok-text`). A status word on any surface uses them; `warn` and `ok` stay for marks and fills. The headline's amber sentence and a page's “couldn't read” line use `warnText` too. | `warn` and `ok` fail 4.5:1 as text on `sf2` in light (UIR-01); one pair of text colours on web and phone. |
| AP-7451 | **Marks by shape** (`StatusMark`, `ui/statusMark.tsx`): not working a 6pt dot inside a 1.5pt `bad` ring, 12pt across; needs a look an 8pt filled `warn` dot; learning a **10pt** hollow 1.5pt `mut` ring (the spec's; the web's is 8 inside 12); connected none. With a label it is an image with that name, else decoration. The pill (`LevelPill`) is the web's: the mark and the word in mono 11.5/500, the level's text colour on `sf2`, 24 tall; connected a 6pt `ok` dot so pills keep one shape. Level and word come from the shared `statusLevel`. | Shape carries the level without colour; the phone's ring is drawn a little larger so it reads at phone sizes, as the spec asked. |
| AP-7452 | **Superseded in part by docs/decisions/overview-one-page.md OVP-09, OVP-11** (on Overview a connection is a compact tile, its mark at the top right; Settings → Connections' connected rows still have no green dot). **A connection row says its status in words** (Overview's Connections tab, `phoneServiceRows`): the right-hand dot is gone; under the name the mark and the word lead one line — “Connected · used today”, “Needs a look · 169 of 171 integrations · 38 errors today”, “Learning · Neo is learning it” — then why it isn't connected (`reason`, `statusReason`) on a line of its own, two lines before it is cut. The word is 500 in the level's text colour, except **Connected, which is `ink2`**: green on every healthy row was loud, and colour is kept for what isn't fine (the pill on a page keeps its green). TalkBack reads “Home Assistant, needs a look, {reason}, {line}”. Settings → Connections' connected rows lose their green dot too (a row in another state keeps its dot). Supersedes connections-page.md AP-2554 in part (the dot and the state in its tone) and connections-redesign-android.md AP-1172 in part (the green dot). | Spec §3: a dot alone said nothing to someone who can't tell amber from green; a word does. |
| AP-7453 | **Superseded by docs/decisions/overview-one-page.md OVP-01, OVP-53** (Overview has jump chips to its sections, not tabs). **Overview's tabs carry a mark only when something in them isn't working** (`overviewTabs`): the not-working ring after the count, and “Servers, 5, something isn’t working” for TalkBack; what only needs a look has no mark there (its rows and the Inbox say it). `UnderlineTabs` takes a `level` beside its tone dot. | Spec §3: a tab mark for every amber thing was noise; red means broken. |
| AP-7454 | **Superseded in part by docs/decisions/overview-one-page.md OVP-54** (a live update keeps a server and those that run on it together; the list sorts when Overview loads, not when a tab opens). **Worst first, never under the finger** (`statusOrder`, `useStatusOrder`): Overview's server cards and connection rows sort by level when the list loads (a tab opened is a list loaded) and on a pull to refresh; a live update keeps the order on screen, adds what is new at the end and drops what is gone. Within a level the server's order stays. | Spec §4; the web's UIR-06 sorts cards worst first, and a list that reorders while you reach for a row is hard to use. |
| AP-7455 | **Superseded by docs/decisions/overview-one-page.md OVP-04, OVP-07, OVP-11, OVP-53** (the pill beside the name only when it isn't fine; the processor's last 24 hours as a line). **A server's card**: the name, then the pill under it (`LevelPill`, its level from `serverStatusLevel`: “Running” when fine, the problem's words — “Disk array failed”, “Running warm” — or “Offline”), then the system and uptime. **The processor line is gone** from the card and from its TalkBack label; it is on the server's page. Supersedes connections-page.md AP-2581 in part (the dot when fine, the CPU line) and AP-2586 (the pill on the name's row). | Spec §4: the pill under the name never squeezes the name, and one pill look across Overview (UIR-22). |
| AP-7456 | **Superseded by docs/decisions/overview-one-page.md OVP-11, OVP-52** (the figures two by two with their labels; the mock's block bars in `chart1`). **The figures** (`figureColumns`): four across while each tile keeps 72dp, else two by two; two by two above text size 1.3 whatever the width; measured on the card, so a narrow page beside the list counts. Labels are 12/500 sans in `mut` (the 9.5 mono capitals didn't fit 72dp at 12: “STORAGE”). The meters are **neutral** (`ink2` lit, `line2` unlit): a figure has no status level of its own; a figure past its limit keeps its number in `warnText` or `bad` (text, not the bar). | Spec §4; labels ≥ 12 for reading, and bars in a status colour suggested a level the data doesn't have. |
| AP-7457 | **The headline's red sentence opens its Inbox row**: when `homeStatus` names an item (`itemId`), the sentence stands on its own line as a 44dp link with “›”, opening that item's page (`NeedsYouItem`, whose back now says where it was opened from: “Back to Overview”); the rest (“2 other things need you.”) follows under it. Without an item the sentence is text as before. | Spec §4 and the web's UIR-14: the loud sentence leads to the one place that acts on it. |
| AP-7458 | **Back is each platform's own** (`BackButton` in `ui/headers.tsx`, used by every back bar and header). iPhone: the SF Symbol `chevron.backward` (SwiftUI through `@expo/ui`, no new dependency; semibold 20, `brand`) and the previous screen's title 17 `brand`, 44pt; iOS's edge swipe as before. Android: Material's `arrow_back` shape (Lucide arrow-left, 24dp, `ink`) in a 48dp round target named “Back to {previous}”, with **the screen's own title as the large title under it** (Material's large top app bar) rather than repeated in the bar. Chat-like headers whose title follows at once (a branch thread, Settled, Feedback) show the glyph alone (`compact`). “✕ Close” on a right page's first screen stays. | Spec §5. Repeating the title beside the arrow on every one of the sixty screens that already show it large would say it twice. |
| AP-7459 | **Android's predictive back is on** (`predictiveBackGestureEnabled`, `android:enableOnBackInvokedCallback`): the system's back gesture previews going home from a tab's root, and in-app back (the stack, sheets, `BackHandler`) runs through the same dispatcher. Checked on the API 36 emulator: a page goes back, an open Inbox sheet closes first. | Spec §5: system and predictive back. |

The header already folded by fit; the order now matches the spec's two named buttons. |

While it shows, iPhone's back keeps only its chevron and the chip gives it room. | Spec §5 and the web's UIR-67: once the question is gone you still know where you are. |
