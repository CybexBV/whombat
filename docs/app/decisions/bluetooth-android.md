# Bluetooth on Android (AP-3750…AP-3799)

John, 2026-10-20: “Implement your original idea so we can pair keyboards, mice, speakers, etc”. The product decisions
are Whombat's `docs/decisions/bluetooth.md` (BT-01…BT-11; BT-11 are the words); this is stream M of
`docs/implementation/bluetooth/assignment.md` there: on the phone, This Whombat's BLUETOOTH card with its Add a device
sheet. The shared model, every word and the fake are the web stream's (`bluetooth-web.md`, AP-3700…AP-3710:
`@whombat/client` `host/bluetooth.ts`, `@whombat/client/host/fake` `bluetooth-fake.ts`), on contracts 0.109.0. No
mockup: NETWORK and its Wi-Fi sheet, built earlier the same day (`hardware-support-android.md`), are the pattern.

The parts: `apps/mobile/src/managed/BluetoothSection.tsx` (BLUETOOTH and a device's action sheet), `BluetoothSheet.tsx`
(Add a device), `bluetooth.ts` (the pure part, tested), the kind icons in `MachineParts.tsx`, `useBluetooth` and the
`bluetooth` op set in `useMachine.ts`, the demo machine's Bluetooth in `fixtures/machine.ts`. This Whombat's screens
only gain insertion lines (`WhombatScreen.tsx`, the demo's `SettingsPages.tsx`).
