# Connections that sign in properly: the phone (AP-3950…AP-3999)

John, 2026-10-21: “We have lots of connections, but some of them don't work correctly regarding the sign-in process. For
example Plex auth is not working when it has 2FA.” The product decisions are Whombat's
`docs/decisions/connection-signin.md` (SG-01…SG-22); this is stream A of
`docs/implementation/connection-signin/assignment.md` there: the phone's side of SG-01 (signing in on the service's own
page), SG-06 (a code that belongs to a challenge) and SG-09 (a session's two-step code, asked once), on contracts 0.111.0
(CT-730…CT-749). The rules and every word are the shared client's (`connection-signin-web.md`, AP-3900…AP-3916:
`signInChoices(…, serviceLogin)`, `serviceLoginBody`, `reconnectServiceLoginBody`, `SERVICE_LOGIN_WAIT_TEXT`,
`ConnectProblem.challenge`, `asksTwoStepCode`, `codeShown`, `setSecretBody`). No mockup: Nextcloud's login on the phone
(phone-connect.md AP-2914) is the pattern.

The parts: `screens/ConnectScreen.tsx` (Connect {Name}), `screens/connect/parts.tsx` (`LoginWaitView`),
`screens/connect/useLoginWait.ts` (the poll), `screens/connect/ConnectionChanges.tsx` (SIGN-IN on a connection's
screen), `data/connectForm.ts` and `data/connectionChange.ts` (the pure part, tested), the demo's answers in
`fixtures/connect.ts`.

It starts with a call, so it works the same on a managed and a self-hosted Whombat (`waitsHere`, with Nextcloud's login), never through the Whombat's web page. Connected, “{label} connected.” and the connection's page replaces the form, as every sign-in (PC-10). | SG-01, SG-02, SG-05; the Nextcloud flow is the model. |
