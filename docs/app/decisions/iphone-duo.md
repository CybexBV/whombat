# Folding phones: the iPhone Duo and Android foldables (AP-5803…AP-5816, AP-6300…AP-6303, AP-6340, AP-6730, AP-6740, AP-6741)

John, 2026-10-26: “We need full support for the iPhone Duo”, then, after the design, “Implement this fully.” Apple's
first foldable (on sale since 23 October, iOS 27.1) has a 466 × 678 pt screen closed and a 951 × 669 pt one open (669 ×
951 held upright), measured on Xcode 27.1's iPhone Duo simulator. Built with the iOS 27.1 SDK the app already reached
the screen's edges, and the inner screen ignores the portrait lock, so app 3.1 showed the phone layout stretched: the
clock — iOS 27.1 keeps it, with its own buttons, in a vertical strip down the right edge — sat over Chat's “Threads”,
the fold ran through every message, the four tabs spread over 950 points and a line of text reached about 150
characters. Safari, closed, keeps its buttons in that strip under the clock.

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| AP-5803 | **The layout follows the window's shape, never the device's model** (`src/data/screenShape.ts`, unit-tested): the window's size, its safe area and what the system reports about it (below). Tabs: down the edge where the system keeps its own vertical bar; without one, along the bottom below 600 points wide and down the left from 600. Pages: two from 840 points wide (and room for two of at least 320), one otherwise. The same rules give the Duo closed and open, Split View, an unfolded Android foldable and a phone their layout. | Apple's guidance is to decide by size class, not idiom or orientation, and the inner screen ignores orientations anyway. 600 and 840 are Material's medium and expanded widths and fall where Apple's compact and regular do on the Duo (466, 669 and Split View halves are one page; 951 is two). |
| AP-5804 | **On the Duo the tabs live in the strip under the clock** (`SideBar` in `TabBar.tsx`): the bottom bar's icons, badges and words stacked in the middle of the space below the clock, 72 points wide or the system's inset if wider (84 on the Duo). A phone keeps its bottom bar; a wide window without a system bar has the rail on the left. Tapping a tab goes back to the tabs and shows it, as on the bottom bar. | It is where iOS puts its own controls on the Duo (Safari does it), the strip is lost to the clock anyway, and the closed screen is short (678 points): the 80-point bottom bar goes back to messages. The page beside the strip is 382 points, about a phone's width. |
| AP-5805 | **One navigation state in every shape** (`SplitStack.tsx`): the root is React Navigation's native stack with its own `NativeStackView`, drawn as the stack (a phone, or beside the side bar) or as two pages — the tabs, the stack's first route, as the list on the left and the routes above it as a stack of their own on the right. Folding and unfolding change only the drawing, so a thread open on the closed phone shows beside Chat when it opens and on top of Chat when it closes, and drafts are kept per conversation as always. | Two navigators, or a detail stack inside each tab, would need the state translated on every fold, and the screens rewritten to take props instead of routes. The switch between one page and two remounts the screens, which read from the cache. |
| AP-5806 | **What opening does with two pages** (`splitRouter.ts`, unit-tested): from the list, a link or a tapped notification it replaces what the right page shows (nothing happens when it already shows it); from the right page it goes on top there, as on a phone; going to the tabs from the right page returns to the list's own route. The right page's first screen has “✕ Close” where it would have “‹ Back”. The list marks what it opened (Chat marks the thread's first message). | A list and its detail: picking another item replaces the detail instead of piling up screens behind it. |
| AP-5807 | **Superseded by AP-5812**: the list is the narrower page and the person moves the line. **The two pages meet on the fold**: the list ends on the crease and the right page starts there, a hairline between them; without a usable fold the list takes 45 % of the content. iOS reports the fold as a region 40 points wide (the crease at 475.5 of 951 with 20-point margins for buttons) and calls it inactive while the phone lies flat; the app takes the crease itself, inactive or not. On the Duo open the list is 475.5 points and the right page 391.5, a phone's width, with the 84-point strip beside it. | The crease is there whether the hinge is flat or not, so no line of text should cross it; a 40-point gutter wasted a phone's margin on each page, and the pages' own padding (14 points and more) keeps text and buttons off the crease. |
| AP-5808 | **`modules/whombat-fold`**, a small native module of our own: on iOS a view behind the app reads `UITraitCollection.verticalBarEdge`, `reservedRegions(kind: .division)` and `(.occlusion)` (iOS 27.1) and a `UIHingeInteraction`, again on every size, safe-area, trait and hinge change; on Android Jetpack WindowManager's `FoldingFeature`. It reports the bar's edge, the folds, the clock's and cameras' regions (window points) and the posture. Older iPhones and iOS versions report nothing, which is a phone. | React Native and Expo expose none of this; the community packages were untested on the hardware and the Duo's APIs are days old. The iOS headers in Xcode 27.1 were the reference. |
| AP-5809 | **The app stays clear of the clock before the tabs show** (`AppFrame`): while the navigator hasn't drawn its side bar, the frame keeps the system's strip empty, so signing in and choosing a Whombat put nothing under the clock; once the side bar is there it covers the strip. Each content area has its own safe area, measured inside the frame, so screens don't count the edge the bar covers. | The clock covered a header button on every screen; screens only count the top inset. |
| AP-5810 | **Superseded by AP-5814**: beside Chat one line too. **The right page's placeholder**: beside Chat its threads (the Threads screen without a back button); beside Connections, Watch and Settings one line: “Pick a connection to see it here.”, “Pick something to see it here.”, “Pick a setting to see it here.” | Chat is where most reading happens, so its right page is never empty; elsewhere a blank page would look broken. |
| AP-5811 | **Superseded in part by AP-6548** (with two pages a sheet sits at the bottom of its page). **Showing counts as in front**: Watch's host status and the live activities keep updating while the app is `inactive` (beside another app in Split View, under a pulled-down notice) and stop only in the background. Sheets keep a phone's width, at most 560 points, in the middle of a wide window. | In Split View a visible app is inactive; its status froze. Apple centres sheets on the inner screen; one stretched over 951 points read like a banner. |
| AP-5812 | **Superseded in part by AP-6546** (how the line is dragged: a pan over both pages on the UI thread). **The list is the narrower page, and the person moves the line between them** (`screenShape.ts` `dividerAt`, `listWidthFor`, unit-tested; `SplitStack.tsx` `Divider`): until it is moved the list takes the narrower side of the fold — on the Duo open the width right of the crease, 391.5 points, and the right page 475.5 with the crease 84 points into it — and 45 % without a fold. The line is a hairline with a grip in its middle, 24 points wide to touch; dragged sideways it stops where either page would be narrower than 320 points and snaps onto the crease within 16 points of it. Where it is left is kept on the phone as the list's share of the content (`whombat.pageSplit` in the secure store), so it comes back after a restart and on the next unfold. Screen readers adjust it as a slider, 32 points a step. | John, 2026-10-27: “I want the wider column on the right”, “resize the columns by drag the line in the middle … saved automatically.” The reading (a thread, a server's page) happens on the right; the list needs less. The crease becomes a place the line can rest, not one it must. |
| AP-5813 | **With two pages the headers with a line under them line up** (`LinedHeader` in `ui/headers.tsx`): Chat's and Feedback's, a thread's, and the back-arrow header of Threads and a conversation. Each reports how tall it is by itself and all of them take the tallest one's height; with two pages the back-arrow header has 6 points under it instead of 12, so at the usual text size all are 58 points and nothing grows. | John, 2026-10-27: “these should align when unfolded.” At larger text sizes the headers grow by different amounts, so they measure instead of sharing a number. |
| AP-5814 | **Superseded in part by AP-6301**: beside Chat the right page is a new chat; Threads opening there stays. **Beside Chat the right page waits with one line**: “Pick a thread to see it here.”; Threads opens there from Chat's header, as on the phone. Connections, Watch and Settings keep their lines (AP-5810). | John, 2026-10-27: “I don't want Threads on the right by default.” |
| AP-5815 | **With two pages a Watch item and a Connections Needs you item open on the right page, not in a sheet over the list** (the `WatchItem` and `NeedsYouItem` routes): the sheet's badge, words and buttons under “✕ Close”, the list's row marked. A chat a button starts or a page it opens takes the item's place there; the other buttons close the page once they went through; while a decision is on its way the page keeps the item that left the list, and an item gone for good says so. With one page the sheet stays, and an item open when the phone folds shows as a page with “‹ Watch”. Sheets that act on what the right page shows (a guest's power, a server's updates) stay sheets. | John, 2026-10-27: “when I click on it a card hovers up, but instead we should see it in the right column.” Needs you on Connections is the same sheet, so it follows. |
| AP-5816 | **A conversation doesn't scroll to its top from a tap on its header where nothing of the system's is above it** (`ChatView`: `scrollsToTop` only with a top inset). On the Duo the header is at the window's top edge, and iOS takes a tap there — on Chat's Threads or Feedback button too — as a tap on the status bar, scrolling the list under the finger to its first message. | Found while checking AP-5814: opening Threads from Chat's header threw Chat back to its oldest message. On a phone the status bar sits above the header and nothing changes. |
| AP-6300 | Superseded by AP-9380, AP-9381 (the button floats over the list, on a phone too, and Feedback has it). **With two pages Chat's list has a New chat button where its box was** (`NewChatBar` in `ui/chat.tsx`, the pill's 46 points in the same bar, so its line meets the right page's reply box): a pen in a square and “New chat”. Tapping it closes what the right page shows and puts the cursor in the new chat's box there (AP-6301). It is marked, as the list marks what it opened, while the right page shows the new chat. Feedback keeps its box, since it is a page of its own on the right; a phone keeps Chat's box at the bottom of Chat, where sending opens the thread as before. | John, 2026-10-30, on the Duo open with a thread beside Chat: two boxes side by side, “Ask Dusty anything” and “Reply…”, looked alike, so a reply could start a new thread and a new question could land in a thread. “The chat input on the left side should simply be a start new chat button, and the new chat appears on the right side.” The list is for picking, as on Connections and Watch, and the right page is the wider one. | AP-6301 | Superseded in part by AP-9382: on one page the new chat is a route. **Beside Chat the right page, while nothing is open there, is a new chat** (`NewChatPage` in `ChatRoomScreen.tsx`, drawn by `EmptyPage`): a header “New chat”, the agent's face and “Ask Dusty anything. | AP-6303 | Superseded in part by AP-9380: the bar holds only the reply box. **`ComposerBar`** holds what the reply box and New chat share: the hairline over them, the room for the gesture area or the tab bar under them, the keyboard, and with two pages the height the bars line up to. | One frame keeps the button and the box the same height, also when the text size or the inset changes. |
| AP-6340 | **Folding or unfolding keeps the tab** (John, 2026-10-31): going from one page to two or back draws the list's tabs somewhere else, so React Navigation drops their state and mounts them anew, which started them on Chat while the bar still marked the tab you were on. The stack keeps the tab the list last showed (`LastTabContext`, a ref in `SplitStack`); the tabs start on it when they mount anew, and the bar shows it until their state is back. | AP-6730 | **Beside Chat the person can hide Chat's list, and what is open takes the whole width** (John, 2026-11-03: “the option to hide the thread list and show the chat screen in full width. A button at the start of the right page's header (a thread's, the new chat's, Feedback's, Settled's; `ListToggleButton` in `ui/headers.tsx`, “Hide threads” / “Show threads” to VoiceOver and TalkBack) hides the list and the line between the pages, and the same button shows them again. The other tabs hide their list while something is open (AP-6740). | On the open phone a long thread reads better at full width; Chat's list is one tap away. |
| AP-6740 | **Beside the other tabs the list hides too, while something is open there** (John, 2026-11-04: “see a service / connection like Plex in full screen, similarly as with a chat”). | AP-6741 | **A wide page puts its cards in columns, as the web does.** A connection's, service's or device's page and a server's Summary measure their own width; from 680 points (an open Duo or foldable with the list hidden; the page beside a list stays narrower) their panels sit where the web's `placePanels` puts them: what goes across the width stays across it, chart cards two to a line (a wide card, or one left alone, takes its line), the main and side columns side by side (≈ 65 % · 35 %, a service's settings 1 · 1), then the bottom (`wideLayout` in `data/dashboard.ts`, `WidePanels` in `screens/thing/Panels.tsx`). A page with only one column (Plex's Overview) keeps one column across the width, as on the web. | Stretched to 800 points the stacked phone cards read badly; the web already decides where each card goes. |

Not done, deliberately: anything on the outer screen while the phone is open (Apple's scene accessories); landscape
on the closed screen (the portrait lock stays; the inner screen rotates regardless); a layout for the half-open phone
held like a laptop (the module reports the posture, nothing uses it yet); Apple Pencil. Android foldables get the same
layout from the same rules (the rail on the left, two pages on the unfolded screen when it is 840 points wide).

## Checked

`pnpm check`; the demo world's Release build on Xcode 27.1's iPhone Duo simulator (iOS 27.1): closed and open, light
and dark — Chat with its threads beside it, a thread opened from Chat (marked in Chat, “✕ Close”), the phone closed with
the thread open and opened again, a server page beside Connections, a settings page beside Settings, a sheet. The open
phone held upright was not seen there (the simulator kept the inner screen sideways); the rules for it are unit-tested.

AP-6300…AP-6303 were checked with `pnpm check` and the demo world's Release build on the same simulator: open, light
and dark, the new chat beside Chat with New chat marked, a thread opened from the list (one box, “Reply…”), New chat
closing it with the cursor in the new chat's box, a question sent from there opening its thread in place and marked
in the list, “✕ Close” back to the new chat without the keyboard, and the phone closed showing Chat with its own box.
An Android foldable was not run; it takes the same layout from the same rules.

AP-6730 was checked with `pnpm check` and the demo world's Release build on the iPhone Duo simulator, open, light and
dark: the list hidden beside a thread and beside the new chat with the side bar in place, Inbox keeping both pages, Chat
full width again after it, kept after the app restarted, the list shown again; closed, no button. An Android foldable
was not run.

AP-6740 and AP-6741 were checked with `pnpm check` (the rules and the layout unit-tested) and the demo world on the
iPhone Duo simulator, open, light and dark: the button beside Plex and beside pve-02, none on Overview's empty page,
Plex's Summary full width with Now playing beside Worth a look, its settings in two equal columns, “✕ Close” bringing
the list back, Chat keeping its own choice, pve-02 opening full width after it, “Show the list”. An Android foldable
was not run.

AP-5812…AP-5815 were checked with `pnpm check` and the demo world's Release build on the same simulator, open, in light
and dark: Chat with the empty right page, a thread and Threads beside it with the header lines meeting, the line dragged
both ways, onto the crease and against the minimum, kept after the app restarted, and a Watch and a Needs you item on
the right page, and Chat staying on its newest message when Threads opens from its header (AP-5816).
