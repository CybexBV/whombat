# Quiet notices on Android (AP-4860…AP-4899)

John, 2026-10-22 (main checkout): UniFi said “Access point offline” for an access point he took offline on purpose, and
the only way out was “Look into it”. Every Worth a look line is now a Problem, Only you or a Heads-up, and who may manage
a thing can Ignore a line until it changes, be reminded tomorrow, or stop watching it. What each line is and does is
Whombat's `docs/decisions/quiet-notices.md` (QN-01…QN-24, QN-S*); the wire is contracts 0.122.0 (CT-1060…CT-1068). The
shared client part and the web are `quiet-notices.md` (AP-4800…AP-4859); this is the phone's, built on the same helpers
(`quietMenu`, `noticeLink`, `quietLineText`, `quietUndo`, `thingWatchView`, `needsYouActions`, `needsYouIgnoreDone`,
`useQuietActions`, `useThingWatch`, `serverDashboard`'s `quiet`).
