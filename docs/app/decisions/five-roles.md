# Five roles with a focus (AP-628…AP-634)

John, 2026-10-02: “re-think the amount of agents we are using, we have too much to choose from”, then “This makes a lot
of sense, I like this new approach”, and “Implement this fully on a new branch.” The spec is Whombat
`docs/decisions/role-consolidation.md` (RO-01…RO-30, details RO-DC01…RO-DC10): five roles — the automatic Assistant,
Associate, Developer, Operator and Lead — each with an optional focus, five characters, the Associate in Pippa's look,
Lead once there is a team, the legacy picker on older Whombats. The shapes are contracts 0.37.0 (CT-228…CT-233,
vendored in `ab4cb25`). The role-level choices made here are RO-DA01…RO-DA13 (Whombat
`docs/implementation/five-roles/app-decisions.md`). Not mocked (B-05): the role rows and the focus follow the role
cards and the audit log's filter chips; the focus select follows Configure's fields. All copy is core's unless listed
as ours.

**Gating.** The new picker, the job line, the focus chips and Configure's focus select need the runtime feature
`agents.focus` (`hasAgentFocus`, `packages/client/src/roles.ts`); without it the hire screen is 0.36's card grid exactly
as before and sends no `focus` or `job` (RO-22). Labels and looks need no feature: an older Whombat's legacy role reads
as its canonical role with its focus and wears its own look.

## Amendment: no focus to choose, the role's names, titles and skills (AP-636…AP-642)

capable of everything that was assigned. So instead we should make it clear what the agents capabilities / skills are.
Also, we should make it more personal, so we need to show the names of the agents and the job titles next to it.” The
spec is Whombat `docs/decisions/role-consolidation.md` RO-40…RO-47; the shapes are contracts 0.38.0 (CT-234…CT-236:
`ROLE_CAPABILITIES`, `rolePacks` without a focus, `HIRE_PACKS_MAX` 16 / `LEGACY_HIRE_PACKS_MAX` 8, feature
`agents.role_skills`, “Software Developer”, Hazel and Ace). Role-level choices are RO-DA21…RO-DA30 (Whombat
`docs/implementation/five-roles/app-decisions-0.27.md`). Supersedes AP-629's focus chips, job-line gating and rolled
names, AP-630 and AP-631's role-with-focus lines.
