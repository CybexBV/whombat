# The thread link as Slack's (AP-940…AP-945)

John, 2026-10-09, with a screenshot of a Slack message (“6 replies · Last reply 4 days ago” after two faces): “Can you
make the UI of the reply area in a chat look more like Slack … Currently we have a sort of badge, but I like the
approach of Slack better.” The badge was the mock's thread link (README “Thread link”: stacked 24px heads, “5
replies” brand/600, `last 10:06` in mono, in a bordered pill). The user's word wins over the mock here; the rest of
the row stays as drawn. No contract change: `replyCount`, `threadParticipantIds` (the last three repliers) and
`lastReplyAt` already say everything.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-940 | **Web: the thread link is a quiet row, a box only on hover.** `MessageRow`'s link (`[data-thread-link]`): the last repliers' faces side by side (24px, 4px apart, not overlapping, no ring), “5 replies” (14px 600 `--brand`), then “Last reply 2 hours ago” (`--type-small`, `--mut`, one line with an ellipsis). No border or fill at rest. On hover or keyboard focus a quiet box shows (`--sf`, 1px `--line2`, `--radius-small`), “View thread” takes the last reply's place and a chevron (Lucide `ChevronRight`, 16px, `--mut`) sits at its right end. The link stretches to a card's width (it ends where a 640px card does) so the box is the same for every thread, as Slack's; it is pulled out by its 1px border and 6px padding so the faces line up with the text above. 38px tall, the pill's height, so nothing under it moves. Work in the thread (card-settle AP-735) stays after a hairline; alone (no replies yet) its head lines up with the text too. | The bordered pill read as a badge stuck under every message; Slack's row is lighter in a busy channel and still says who answered, how many and when, with the box as the hover affordance. |
| AP-941 | **“Last reply …” as Slack words it** (`lastReplyLabel` in `@whombat/client`, shared with Android): `Last reply just now`, `… 12 min ago`, `… 2 hours ago`, `… 3 days ago` (core `formatAgo`) under a week, then `Last reply on 3 Mar` (`on 3 Mar 2025` in another year). Replaces `last 10:06` (core `formatThreadLast`, now unused here). Ticks with the room's minute clock. | Relative time is what Slack shows and what tells someone whether a thread is still moving; a clock time needs the reader to know today's date. |
| AP-942 | **Whombat's faces, not Slack's squares.** A person is `PersonAvatar` at 24px (the photo fills the disc, else the initials on their tone; it was a hand-made initials disc that put a photo inside a ring of its tone); an agent is its head (20px) on a 14% tint of its role colour (FA-06, FA-07). An id the bootstrap doesn't know shows the silhouette. | Slack's avatars are rounded squares everywhere; Whombat's are discs and wombat heads everywhere, so the thread's faces match the authors' beside them. |
| AP-943 | **The link's name, and the visual check.** The parts are separated by spaces in the markup (a flex row draws none), so the link reads “5 replies Last reply 2 hours ago” (it read “5 replieslast 10:06”); “View thread” is `aria-hidden` and the chevron decorative. `visual/messaging.spec.ts` masks the link over what shows of it (an overlay pane hides its end at 1200px), which covers the mock's narrower pill: the rows around it are still compared. That spec's #billing-v2 states were already over budget on main before this change (0.185 %, the agent names the mock draws differently since the five agents), with the same pixel count after it. | The accessible name is the link's text; the mock can't follow a deviation the user asked for, and masking only the link keeps everything else held to it. |
| AP-944 | **Android: faces, “N replies” and “Last reply …”** (`ThreadRepliesRow`): the last repliers' `PrincipalAvatar`s (20dp, 4dp apart), `25 replies` (13/600 `brand`), `Last reply 2 hours ago` (12.5 `mut`, one line, shrinks first); the `›` is gone, as in Slack. While there is work in the thread its line (the agent's face, `Banjo is working · 38%`, `needs you`, `is thinking`) takes the last reply's place: a phone row has room for one of them and the work is the newer news. `ChatItem.replyHeads` and `lastReply` come with `replies` (`timeLabels().lastReply`: computed under a week, kept by day after, AP-901). The whole row stays one 44dp link; its label says the last reply too. | The phone matches the web; the arrow was the only hint the row opens something, and the faces and the muted line now say it the way Slack's phone app does. |
