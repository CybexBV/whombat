# Website feedback in a chat with your assistant (2026-10-18)

Whombat less-is-more.md LM-01 (stream F, LM-F01…; contracts 0.87.0, CT-383, CT-384): in the assistant shell an
integration's reports land in a chat between the person whose settings it runs on and that person's assistant
(“Website feedback · Billing portal”), not in a channel. Channels aren't shown in that shell, so on John's box the
reports went to `#reports`, where nobody looks. The runtime moves existing integrations to their chat on its own; the
crew shell keeps channels. This is the web app's and the phone's part.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-1827 | **Chat mode is the assistant shell on a runtime with `feedback.chat`** (`useFeedbackChatMode`). Everywhere else — the crew shell, an older Whombat — the form keeps its CHANNEL step and “Add Dusty to #channel”. `feedbackWords(name, {chat})` carries the chat's words (`chat`, null elsewhere). | An older runtime still needs a channel (CT-384); the crew shell keeps channels (LM-01). |
| AP-1828 | **The words**: REPORTS GO TO “Your chat with Dusty”, “Each report becomes a thread there, which only you can see.”; someone else's chat “Joris’s chat with Rosie”; when saving moves the reports (another owner's chat, or a channel not moved yet) “Save, and new reports come to your chat with Dusty instead.”; the intro “A form for your website where visitors report bugs and ideas: each one comes to your chat with Dusty, who looks into it.” | Plain, one sentence each (AP-1751). A chat is private where a channel wasn't: the hint says so. |
| AP-1829 | **Where an integration's reports go** (`useReportsDestination`): its chat — the viewer's own, with its path, or another owner's, named and not linked (only that person can open it) — else its channel as before. The integrations list says “Burrow Billing · reports go to your chat with Dusty”. | One rule for the list, the page and the test card. |
| AP-1830 | **The form in chat mode sends no channel** (`settingsInput(form, {chat: true})`, `validateForm` alike) and reads no channel's members: ASSESSED BY and DEVELOPED BY offer none or the person's own assistant only, which a new integration starts with. | The runtime ignores a channel there and refuses any other assessor (“Reports come to your chat with Hazel, so only Hazel can assess them.”). |
