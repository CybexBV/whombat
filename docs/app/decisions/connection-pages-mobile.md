# Connection pages on Android (AP-1950…AP-1966)

John, 2026-10-18 (main checkout): every connection and device gets a page like a server's, technical (“show
information about Home Assistant itself, like processes, CPU usage, disk space”), then “implement this fully”. Whombat
decides the page in `docs/decisions/connection-pages.md` (CP-01…CP-20); this is the phone's part (CP-19: the same page
from the same contract). Contracts 0.88.0 (CT-390…CT-393); the web's part is `connection-pages-web.md`
(AP-1900…AP-1949).

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AP-1950 | **A connection or a device opens its own page** (`ThingPageScreen`, from `things.page`) wherever the phone opened one: Things, Home's rows, links (`/things/connection%3Acon_…`). On a Whombat whose runtime lacks `things.pages` the Thing route still replaces itself with the Connection screen (CP-18). | One place per thing (CP-01), and older boxes keep working. |
| AP-1951 | **The head** is the server page's at phone width: the thing's glyph (24dp, `ink2`) and its name (28/700), the status dot and the badge's words (“Needs a look”, “1 access point offline”), the fact lines in order (13.5 `mut`, the strong parts semibold in `ink`, mono parts mono), the address in mono, **Runs on pve-02 · CT 131** (`brand`, a 44dp link with a chevron) opening the guest's screen (or the server's page without a guest), and the `problem` line in `warn` with “Figures from 13:58.” when the figures are older. No “Figures read at …” when all is well. | CP-03, CP-04, CP-08; AP-1751: how often it is read is a mechanic. |
| AP-1952 | **Tabs are the server page's segmented control**, each as wide as its words with its count after it (“Integrations 171”), scrolling sideways to the screen's edges when they don't fit a 360dp phone. Settings is the last, a button with a chevron that opens the Connection screen (not a tab); a learning device without a connection has none. `?tab=` in a link opens that tab; `?tab=settings` opens the Connection screen over the page (once); an unknown tab opens Overview. | CP-05, CP-19; Home Assistant has six to eight tabs, which can't share 360dp evenly. |
| AP-1953 | **Panels stack in the server's order**; widths are ignored. A panel with nothing to say is left out (a table or list without rows or words for none, empty facts or access, a chart with nothing read; “Whombat did here” without changes; “Show more here” without widgets or suggestions); Worth a look always shows, with its `empty`. Cards are `OneCard` with the panel's title as the mono label and its `meta` as a grey line under it; a table's or list's link (“Backups”) is a `brand` header link. | CP-03 and the less-is-more rules (AP-1752). |
| AP-1954 | **Figures** are the server page's tiles two by two (a fifth spans the row): mono label, the value 22/700 (`warn`/`bad` in its tone), the `rest` in 13 `mut` after it, `sub` under it; one element for TalkBack (“Disk: 30 / 40 GB, 76 % used”). | Same look as the server's Summary tiles. |
| AP-1955 | **Worth a look**: each notice's icon in its tone, its title and detail, and its way out on the right in `brand` (“Look into it”, “Show them”, “Connect again”); the whole 52dp row is the button. “Look into it” opens the new-chat sheet about the thing with the question ready to send; a tab link switches tab and scrolls to the tabs; Settings opens the Connection screen; a tab the phone doesn't show leaves the row without a button. The count beside the title is in the worst tone. | CP-17; the label says what the tap does, which a chevron alone didn't. |
| AP-1956 | **Tables**: the first column is the row's title (mono when its cell is), its note after it in grey; every other column joins the line under it with “ · ” (in its tone, tags mono); numeric columns stand on the right, stacked; the first state dot leads the row. TalkBack reads every column by its label. The first 25 rows show, then “Show all 171”. | The assignment (CP-19); nothing clips or scrolls sideways (AP-1754). |
| AP-1957 | **Charts**: the latest figures above the lines, in the series' colours (`chart1`…`chart3`, a third one added to the phone's swatches); a finger (or TalkBack's swipe) picks a moment and the row says its time and figures. Lines share one scale: a right-axis series (memory in GB beside CPU in %) is scaled onto the left one, the side numbers are the left one's, the readout says each line's own figure. The panel's `line` is a dashed `warn` line. At most three hour labels and Now along the bottom. | A phone has no room for two axes; the figures are in the readout. |
| AP-1958 | **What Whombat did here** (`changes.list?thing=`, the panel's `limit`): each change's title, detail, when and (a person's change) who; **Undo** while it can be undone (“Undone.”, or “Undo waits for a person’s approval.” as a toast), “Undone” once it was, **Review** where it links somewhere; “Show all” opens Activity. **Activity** lists them all, 20 at a time with “Show older”, and says “Nothing was changed on Home Assistant yet.” when empty. | CP-05, CP-06; ONE-08's Undo. |
| AP-1959 | **What Whombat may do**: each group with its tier as a pill (Allowed `ok`, Asks you `warn`, Off and Mixed neutral) and its number of tools; Look only says “Look only: every change is refused.” first. **Change** (header link) opens the Connection screen for those who may manage it only. | CP-06; a member can't change it. |
| AP-1960 | **Ask about Home Assistant** ends the Overview (as on a server's Summary): the newest open chat about the thing, else the new-chat sheet with the thing as subject (`{kind, id}` from its thingId). `NewChatSheet` gained `initialText`: a notice's question or a chip's “Keep “Database size” on the Home Assistant page.” is ready to send; without it, what was typed stays. | CP-17; the server page's pattern. |
| AP-1961 | **Actions** (Restart) show only to those who may manage the thing (`canManage`), as secondary buttons beside **Open Home Assistant** (the browser); one that isn't allowed is disabled with its `reason` under the row. The confirm is the one-Whombat sheet (`OneSheet`): the thing's name as badge, the server's title, body and button, Cancel; it sends `things.action`, closes and shows the server's words as a toast (“Restarting Home Assistant. It’s back in about a minute.”); a refusal stays in the sheet in plain words. | CP-12; the phone's guest power sheet. |
| AP-1962 | **When the page doesn't load**: “This page didn’t load.” with Try again, or “That isn’t one of your things.” on a 404; for a `con_…` thing either way also **Open its settings** (the Connection screen), since a server that doesn't serve pages yet also answers 404. | Never a dead end on an older or unusual server. |
| AP-1963 | **Live**: read again every 60 seconds while the screen is on top (`useIsFocused`; TanStack's focus manager pauses it in the background), pull to refresh reads the page, its changes and its widgets again; the pull spinner shows for a pull only, not for the 60-second reads. Links keep `?tab=` (`/things/connection%3Acon_ha?tab=logs` → `whombat://things/…?tab=logs`; a server's page ignores it). | CP-04. |
| AP-1964 | **One screen per thing**: the Thing route has `getId` = its thingId, so “Runs on pve-02” (a server) or “Also connected” opens on top of the page instead of replacing it, and Back returns to it. | React Navigation 7 replaced the params of the Thing on screen. |
| AP-1965 | **Things and Home leave out a thing with `partOf`**; a server's Summary lists them in **Also connected** (its Proxmox API) before “Ask about pve-02”, each opening its page. Home Assistant's Home row is called by its name (its glance is technical now; UniFi stays “Network”). | CP-10, CP-16. |
| AP-1966 | **The demo world** (`fixtures/thingPages.ts`): Home Assistant as John's mock (2026.10.2 Container, runs on pve-02 · CT 132 — a `homeassistant` container added to pve-02 —, CPU 5 %, memory 3.0 / 8 GB, disk 30 / 40 GB 76 %, MariaDB 2.8 GB, 169 of 171 integrations, the three notices, containers, backups; tabs Integrations, Logs, Backups; Restart recorded as Mara's change), UniFi (21 of 22 access points, the internet chart, Devices), Hetzner (1 of 1 server, cost), Mail (the generic Overview) and the heat pump (readings when switched on, what Dusty found while learning, “Open the chat”). Its Home row is technical too. Every answer parses with its endpoint's schema. | The page renders without a server, in the app's demo mode. |

**Not on the phone (the web has them):** choosing what a connection runs on (the picker, `connections.setRunsOn`) —
the phone opens what it runs on; removing or moving a widget kept on a page (until `applyWidgets` patches only its own
place, which the web stream changes in `packages/client`); a Restart's “Restarted Home Assistant” in Home's Done
recently (the phone has none).
