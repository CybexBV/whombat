# Whombat Subscription Terms

These terms are between **Cybex BV**, a company in the Netherlands (“we”, “us”), and you. They cover **Whombat
Link**, the subscription for your Whombat: what it gives you, what it costs, the free month, paying, cancelling and
what happens when it ends. You accept them when you add a Whombat to Whombat Link at app.whombat.com. You can take
Whombat Link for yourself or for a business.

Whombat Link pays for our service, not for the software. The software that runs on a Whombat is open source under the
AGPL-3.0, and it works without Whombat Link: at home, and away from home with Tailscale or Headscale.

## 1. What Whombat Link gives you

For each Whombat you add to it:

- you open your Whombat from anywhere, without port forwarding;
- it gets its own secure web address;
- notifications reach your iPhone and Android phone;
- the Whombat apps work away from home;
- sign-ins like Google work;
- you support Whombat’s open-source work.

## 2. Price

- **€2.50 a month or €25 a year for each Whombat**, VAT included. You choose monthly or yearly when you subscribe,
  and you can switch later in Manage subscription.
- If you add or remove a Whombat, the new price applies from your next payment. Nothing extra is charged and nothing
  is paid back for the month or year you're in.
- A promotion code, or free time we give you, makes Whombat Link cheaper or free for the time it says. After that the
  normal price applies.

## 3. The free month

- Your **first month is free, and you don't need a card.** It starts when you add your first Whombat. Each account
  gets one free month, for at most two Whombats.
- Add a card before the free month ends to carry on. Without a card, Whombat Link ends as section 6 says. We never
  charge you without a card you added.

## 4. Paying

- Payments go through **Stripe**. We never see your full card number.
- Your subscription **renews by itself** every month or every year until you cancel it. Each month or year is paid at
  its start.
- If a payment fails, Stripe tries again for a few days. If it keeps failing, the subscription ends (section 6).

## 5. Cancelling and your money back

- You can **cancel at any time** in Manage subscription at app.whombat.com. Whombat Link then keeps working until the
  end of the month or year you paid for, and nothing more is charged.
- **Within 14 days of your first payment you get your money back on request.** Write to
  [info@whombat.com](mailto:info@whombat.com) from your account's email address. We then end the subscription.
- If you take Whombat Link for yourself, you also keep every right that consumer law gives you.

## 6. When Whombat Link ends

Whombat Link ends when you cancel and the time you paid for is over, when the free month ends without a card, or when
payments keep failing.

- It keeps working for **one more week.** Your Whombat tells you, and asks you to turn on Tailscale as a second way in.
- After that week, your Whombat's own address, notifications on your phones, the apps away from home, shared links and
  remote help stop.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Your Whombat keeps working at home, and away from home with Tailscale or Headscale. Your
  data, agents, automations, backups and updates all stay.
- Subscribe again at any time and it picks up where it left off, at the same address.

## 7. Your data

Whombat Link carries your Whombat's connections encrypted, and we can't read what's in them. Our cloud keeps only what
it needs for your subscription: your account, your Whombats with their names, addresses and Link state, and your
subscription. The Whombat Privacy Policy has the details.
